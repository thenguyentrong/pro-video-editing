## Core idea
Denmark sells "everyday wonder": small, real moments (swimming in the harbour, a bike ride, a family dinner on a dune terrace) instead of big sights. Three film languages share one brand block: a comic presenter with a pun, a wall-to-wall city montage, and a quiet face–memory film. All end on the same stacked type: red "DENMARK" over white "THE LAND OF EVERYDAY WONDER".

## Rules with numbers
- **ASL 1.16–1.67 s**, median 1.04–1.52 s. The montage is the fastest (1.16 s) and the most even (6–11 cuts per 10 s from start to end).
- **Cuts ignore the beat** (8–46 % on onsets, at or below chance): they follow movement, looks and lines.
- **Heavy geometric caps** (Montserrat 800–900 look-alike), white, with red for "DENMARK" and for the "-SEN" pun. Title 5.5–8.5 % H, stacked, leading ≈0.9–0.95.
- **Almost no text** in the montage films: one title, a 2 % H location bug, one URL or tagline.
- **End block 3–4.7 s**; the comedy film plays its end card in silence (last 3 s).
- **Loudness −12 to −16 LUFS**, LRA 2.8–5 LU (flat music beds).

## Recipe
1. Pick the language: presenter pun (comedy, voice), city montage (music, ≈1.1 s), or face–memory (music, ≈1.6 s).
2. Use real, small moments; feature contemporary architecture shot top-down or symmetric.
3. Keep the cut density even; match shapes across cuts.
4. End on the stacked brand block on navy or over an aerial, 3–4.5 s.
