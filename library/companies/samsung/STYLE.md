## In one sentence
Samsung shows MORE: a narrator explains every feature, each feature gets a loud CGI "hero title" moment and then a bright lifestyle proof, the frame is always full, and the sound is mastered 4-6 LU louder than Apple.

## Sub-styles

### 1. Unpacked chapter film (launch films, 3-3.5 min)
- Cold open in the dark (light line along the product, sparkle burst, 5-9 s) -> promise montage of hero titles -> name card -> 5-7 chapters of 25-45 s (performance, display, cameras, Galaxy AI, security) -> product hero + 3-line lockup -> SAMSUNG on black.
- Each chapter = CGI hero title shot (3-5 s, one continuous push) + 5-10 proof shots at 1.6-2.5 s (lifestyle, then over-the-shoulder UI).
- VO throughout, one calm narrator, one sentence per shot.
- Two-product films change palette and tone at the turn (Fold8: dark engineering -> white pastel playful), joined by a reveal gag (chocolate bar = folded phone) and a 0.85 s silence.

### 2. Galaxy AI demo spot (30-35 s)
- Problem line (5% H, centred) -> real UI demo with a glass prompt pill ("Add my dog" -> Generate) -> escalating results (each prompt bigger than the last) -> playful payoff (cartoon stickers on real footage) -> lockup.
- Licensed vocal pop track, no narrator; tiny 0.15-0.2 s music stops on taps.

### 3. Tech spectacle (TV / display feature films)
- Each claim becomes an abstract CGI metaphor (colour balls, aurora, light-speed tunnel), then the TV in a warm room shows it, then a split "Conventional | Real QLED" / "60Hz | 144Hz" / "Off | On".
- Fixed two-line super: benefit ("Ultra-fine Contrast"), then 1-3 s later "with <technology>".
- Long continuous CGI moves (ASL 3.3 s, 44% of shots > 3 s), cuts not tied to the beat (13% on onsets vs 11% chance).

### 4. Monochrome teaser (vertical social)
- Black-and-white dance/light, a metronome start (3 x 2.13 s) that accelerates to 0.4-0.6 s, one 0.6 s colour product flash, question CTA "Ready to unfold? / Coming soon", logo.
- This is the only place where Samsung is close to Apple's restraint (55% empty frames).

## Measured ranges
- Launch films: ASL 2.1-2.8 s (median 1.6-2.3), quarters fairly flat (1.7-3.3), 10-21% shots < 1 s, 18-34% > 3 s; densest windows 6-10 cuts / 10 s in lifestyle montages (basketball, party).
- Feature spot: ASL ~1.8 s, 5-6 cuts / 10 s flat.
- Vertical teaser: ASL ~1.2 s, accelerating (quarters 1.73 -> 0.74).
- Non-hard transitions: 29% (S26), 42% (Fold8), 40% (Neo QLED) of all shot boundaries.
- Loudness: -9.9 to -12.6 LUFS integrated.
- Empty frames (>= 70% of pixels one flat tone): 4-22% in films and ads.

## Typography
- Samsung Sharp Sans (proprietary, geometric, single-storey "a"), Bold for titles, Medium/Regular for labels. Free stand-ins (by eye): Outfit SemiBold/Bold, Urbanist Bold.
- Hero title = eyebrow + headline + tail: eyebrow line ~4-7% H, headline cap 7-12% H ("Privacy Display" line 16.7% H incl. descender), tail ~2.3% H right-aligned under the headline end.
- Titles are inside the 3-D scene: lit with bloom, tilted along the product, embossed in a floor.
- Name split around the product: "Galaxy [phone] S26 Ultra", "Introducing [the all-new] Galaxy Z Fold8", "10:16 [content] Ratio" (cap ~12% H on white).
- Feature labels: white, cap 3-5% H, left third or next to the subject, held 2-4 s, fade 0.3-0.5 s.
- Accent colour words: lilac or Galaxy-AI blue ("The all-new").
- End lockup: 3 lines - eyebrow "The new / The all-new" (3.5% H), product name bold (4.6% H line), "Galaxy AI ✦" (3.5% H); over the product hero, held ~4 s. Legal micro-text at the bottom on almost every shot.

## Colour
- Product palette: violet/lavender/lilac devices on violet-to-navy gradients (launch films), off-white #EEE studio for the playful product parts.
- AI palette: blue -> teal four-point sparkle, iridescent blue/teal/purple glow sweeps.
- Lifestyle: warm, saturated, clean (string lights at night, sunny parks), no grain, no letterbox.
- TV films: hyper-saturated CGI (magenta, cyan, rainbow) against warm brown rooms.

## Camera
- CGI exploded views, chips, pixels, vaults - always moving (slow push or fly-through).
- Lifestyle with the phone in hand; POV over-the-shoulder UI on the real device for every software feature; lens macros; drone landscapes.
- Hands as storytellers (designer hands conjuring CGI layers, a hand pulling paper away).

## Transitions
- Push into a lens / chip / screen that becomes the next scene; light slits and white flashes for an "unfold"; screen-to-full-frame; phone-edge wipes; zoom-blur whips (0.4 s) in friend montages; page curl; objects flying from card to card; kinetic type that starts huge and pulls back.

## Sound
- Launch films: one narrator from second 2 to the end card; music bed never leaves; short drops (1-1.5 s, 15-30 LU down) before a new chapter or product, then a +18 to +40 dB hit on the title.
- AI spots: licensed vocal pop, 0.15-0.2 s stops on UI taps, the loudest moment on the playful payoff.
- TV film: no narrator, diegetic sound (commentary, voice command), one 0.7 s silence before the biggest AI moment.
- Everything mastered loud (-10 to -12.6 LUFS).

## How Samsung differs from Apple's restraint
| | Apple (iPhone 17 Pro / Watch 12 films, see film-taste measured-numbers) | Samsung |
|---|---|---|
| Words | music + 2-5 words per card, no narrator | narrator explains every feature + labels + spec numbers + legal lines |
| Type size | cap ~4% H, flat, centred | headline cap 7-12% H, lit/tilted in 3-D, eyebrow + tail |
| Frame | >= 70% empty in about half the shots, product on black | 4-22% empty: full, saturated frames |
| Cut pace | ASL ~1.0-1.4 s, flat | ASL 2.1-3.3 s in films (continuous CGI moves + VO), 1.8 s in spots |
| Transitions | invisible: hard or match cuts | 30-40% visible transitions (fly-through, flash, wipe, whip, curl) |
| Sound | ~-16 LUFS, music never drops | -10 to -12.6 LUFS, VO + bed, deliberate drops before hits |
| Proof | the product itself | split-screen before/after, UI demos, CGI metaphors |
| Close | line in 3 chunks + logo | 3-line lockup over product, then SAMSUNG on black |

## The 8 rules
1. Build in chapters: CGI hero title (3-5 s) -> lifestyle proof -> real UI demo; 25-45 s per chapter, one VO sentence per shot.
2. Pace: ASL 2.0-2.8 s in launch films, ~1.8 s in 30 s AI spots, ~1.2 s and accelerating in vertical teasers.
3. Title system: eyebrow 4% H + headline cap 7-12% H (geometric bold sans) + tail 2.3% H; put the headline in the 3-D scene (glow, tilt).
4. Split the name around the product: "Galaxy [phone] S26 Ultra".
5. Galaxy AI = blue sparkle + glass prompt pill + iridescent sweep; show prompt -> tap -> full-frame result, each result bigger than the last.
6. Fill the frame (< 25% empty) and make invisible tech visible: CGI metaphor + before/after split + "benefit / with <technology>" two-line super.
7. Move through things: lens/chip push-ins, light-slit unfolds, screen-to-full-frame, zoom-blur whips - 30-40% of boundaries.
8. Loud, continuous sound (-10 to -12.6 LUFS) with a 1-1.5 s drop before each chapter title; end on a 3-line lockup (4 s) + SAMSUNG on black (~1 s).

## Recipe (real footage + HTML/GSAP, Hyperframes)
- Footage: phone-in-hand POV and lifestyle (warm practicals at night, sunny day), product renders/turntable on `linear-gradient(160deg,#6b6290,#1d1b33)`, stock CGI loops for tech chapters (particles, chip fly-through).
- Hero title component: eyebrow (Outfit 600, cap 3% H, opacity .9), headline (Outfit 700, cap 10% H, letter-spacing -0.01em, `text-shadow: 0 0 28px rgba(150,180,255,.55)`), tail (Outfit 500, 2.3% H, right-aligned). Wrap in `perspective: 1200px`; GSAP: `from {y: 40, opacity: 0, filter: 'blur(12px)'}` 0.8 s `power3.out`, then a slow `rotateY: -18 -> -10, scale: 1 -> 1.06` over the shot (4 s, `none`).
- Feature label: Outfit 500 cap 3.5% H at left 8%, top 45%; fade 0.4 s, hold 2.5 s.
- Prompt pill: `border-radius: 999px; background: rgba(255,255,255,.18); backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, 45% W x 9% H at bottom 10%; type the prompt at 35 ms/char; 0.15 s music stop + tap ring; result crossfade under a glow sweep (`linear-gradient(100deg,transparent,#60a5fa 40%,#5eead4 55%,#c084fc 70%,transparent)`, `mix-blend-mode: screen`, x -100% -> 100%, 0.6 s `power2.inOut`).
- Before/after split: two videos 50/50 with a 2 px white divider, labels 4% H at 85% H.
- Transitions: push into a lens = `scale 1 -> 6` on the outgoing clip with `transformOrigin` on the lens (0.7 s `power3.in`), incoming clip `scale 1.4 -> 1` (0.7 s `power3.out`); light slit = white bar `scaleX 0 -> 1` then full white 0.2 s.
- Mix: VO -16 LUFS short-term over a bed, master to about -11 LUFS integrated; a 1.2 s duck (-20 dB) before each chapter title, a hit on the title cut.
- End: product hero 4 s with the 3-line lockup (eyebrow in a mid blue ~#3A7BD5, name bold, "Galaxy AI ✦"), then wordmark on black 1 s.
