## Recipe (real footage + HTML/GSAP)
- Shoot: phone-in-hand POV (55 mm, f/2.8, warm practical lights), one lifestyle wide per chapter; product renders or turntable footage on a violet-to-navy gradient.
- Chapters of 25-45 s; each = 1 hero title shot (3-5 s) + 5-10 proof shots at 2-2.5 s.
- Title: eyebrow 3% H weight 500 + headline cap 9-11% H weight 700, tracking -0.01em, tail 2.3% H right-aligned. GSAP: headline `y: 40 -> 0, opacity 0 -> 1, filter: blur(12px) -> 0` over 0.8 s `power3.out`; eyebrow 0.2 s later. For the 3-D look wrap in `perspective: 1200px` and animate `rotateY: -25deg -> -12deg` slowly (4 s, `none`) while the camera push runs (`scale 1 -> 1.08`).
- Glow text: `text-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(160,190,255,.6)`.
- Feature label: 3% H, fade 0.4 s, hold 2-3 s, placed beside the subject.
- AI result reveal: a gradient band (`linear-gradient(90deg, #3b82f6, #22d3ee, #a78bfa)`, `mix-blend-mode: screen`, opacity .7) sweeps left -> right in 0.6 s while the edited clip crossfades under it.
- End: product hero 4 s + 3-line lockup, then logo on black 1 s.
- Mix around -10/-12 LUFS; cut the bed for 1-1.5 s before each new chapter title; add 0.15 s music stops on UI taps.
