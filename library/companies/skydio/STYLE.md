## Core idea
American public-safety realism with a tech-company voice. Customers' real jobs (police, fire, utilities) come first, the product second. Specs are plain sentences set like subtitles, and numbers count up on real flight shots. The brand even names its proof standard in a title (real flights, no CGI).

## House rules (measured)
- **Type:** neutral grotesk (closest free: Inter 500), sentence case with a full stop, centred, ≈2.2-5 % H, white with a soft shadow, no box. One proof word in brand blue. Product wordmark wide and geometric (≈12-25 % H) with a letter-spaced sub-name (≈.5em). Name supers lower-left (≈3 % + 2 %).
- **Counters:** a spec value climbs in 3 steps across 3 real flight shots (e.g. speed, flight time), ≈5 % H centred.
- **Rhythm:** teaser and manifesto fast (median 0.77-1.15 s, 45-64 % under 1 s) then one long payoff shot (7-8.4 s); launch film ASL 2.15 s with 8-10 s takes for the dock arm and the product family.
- **Sound:** quiet masters for teaser/manifesto (-21.8 / -24.5 LUFS), -14 LUFS for the launch; 0.4-0.6 s drop-out before the product name or the human beat, then a +30 to +40 dB hit. VO ≈2.0-2.4 words/s.

## How they make trust visible
- A real-only cut of the product, titled as such, next to the launch film (which uses renders only for design and the product family).
- Customer footage with its own burn-in (body-cam timestamps), real dispatch consoles, named employees.
- The robot dock and arm shown working in long takes; the real operator UI before the autonomy claim.
- Claims of scale ("most deployed" type sentence) sit on footage of real installed docks.

## Recipe
1. Teaser: 6-8 real flight shots, one centred spec sentence each (Inter 500 3.5vh), one blue proof word, 0.6 s silence before the wordmark, 1.2 s black, final fly-by, URL card.
2. Launch: 10-13 s of customer work, need, the dock in long takes, wordmark at 25 %, counters on real flights, operator UI, design renders kept separate, hero flights, wordmark; VO ≈2 words/s; button ending.
3. Manifesto: anaphora VO (6-8 lines), 12-15 fragments under 0.6 s at the start, each hard fact on its real shot, customer footage, named people at the end, call to action.
