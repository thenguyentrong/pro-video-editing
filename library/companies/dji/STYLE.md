## In one sentence
DJI proves every feature with the product's own beautiful footage: travel-postcard landscapes and happy people, one label bottom-left, a "SHOT ON" watermark top-left, a narrator who names each spec, and an end on black with the product name and logo.

## Sub-styles

### 1. Feature-run launch film (Mini 5 Pro, Osmo Pocket 4)
- 9-18 features in ≈100 s, each a 3-12 s mini scene of 2-4 shots; one narrator (100-113 words/min) says one sentence per feature with its spec.
- ASL ≈1.1-1.2 s, flat across the film (Pocket 4 quarters 1.15 / 1.15 / 1.06 / 1.20 s); cuts on the beat (45 % vs 33-37 % chance).
- One long CGI tech move (Mini 5 Pro: 10 s push into the sensor) or none.
- Flat, VO-led mix at −17 LUFS (LRA 3 LU).

### 2. Expedition flagship film (Mavic 4 Pro)
- No narrator; a big score; four-continent expedition (glaciers, deserts, cities) with a 20 s paraglider chase as the set piece.
- Spec callouts float next to the parts of the flying drone (bold caps title + 1-2 regular lines).
- ASL ≈1.9 s; cuts strongly on the beat (39 % vs 11 % chance); loud −12.7 LUFS with +1.4 dBTP peaks.

## Measured ranges
- Film ASL 1.1-1.9 s; 30-52 % of shots under 1 s; quarter curves flat.
- Loudness −12.7 (music only) to −17 LUFS (with narrator), LRA 3-12 LU.
- End: product spins/lights up on black 3-6 s → name card 2-2.5 s → logo 3-4 s → YouTube end screen 6-13 s, all in silence after the music ends.

## Typography
- DJI's own sans. Free stand-ins: **Montserrat** (name card: Light + Medium) and **Open Sans** (labels: Bold + Regular).
- Name card: tracked thin "INTRODUCING" (cap ≈2 % H, +0.3 em) over the product name (cap 5.5-5.8 % H; model word medium, "PRO" light), centred.
- Feature label: bold caps cap ≈2.5-2.6 % H (+ optional regular subline ≈2.4 % H), left x ≈6.5 % W, y ≈75-80 % H; cut in and out with the shot, no animation.
- Watermark "SHOT ON / <product>" top-left (≈1 % + 3 % H) on every product-made shot; legal micro-text ≈1.2 % H.

## Colour
- Golden hour / blue hour postcards, saturated but natural; flagship film adds teal-orange contrast. Bright holiday colours for consumer cameras. Close on pure black.

## Camera
- 35-65 % the product's own footage; 20-50 % people using the product (hands, remote screen, selfies); 10-15 % CGI and product macros. Split screens (wide | tele, normal | log) and 9:16 insets on a blurred copy for vertical modes.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts on the beat, whip pans, a gimbal roll as a transition, CGI push into the sensor, split-screen wipes. No flashes.

## Sound
- Feature films: narrator + bright pop bed, flat mix, short 0.35-1.2 s drops before the name card or the end.
- Flagship: music only, epic score, loud master.

## Recipe
- Write one sentence per feature with its spec; pick one hero location look (golden hour) and 2-4 shots per feature, ≈1.1-1.2 s per shot on the beat.
- Lock the type system first: name card centred, label bottom-left, watermark top-left; never move them.
- End on black: product 3-5 s, name card 2.5 s, logo 3 s.
- Mix: VO films ≈ −17 LUFS flat; music-only flagship ≈ −13 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
