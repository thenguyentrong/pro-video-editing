# The timeless allure of Hermès Cut  (Hermès, 2024-04-12, 2:00, 16:9, YouTube @hermes)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fqva6oLu0rU
Purpose: luxury product/savoir-faire film (watch), no voice
One-sentence point: A watch goes from a pencil sketch through metal, light and hands back to the sketch: the craft is the story.

## Structure
- 0-3.4 dusk sky with clouds (3.4 s, no sound for the first 1.3 s).
- 3.4-15 a silhouette hand with a pencil; a metal stamping press, blanks in a tray, a glowing furnace.
- 15-23 a hand sketches a circle; an eye in extreme close-up; a craftsman looks through a case ring (match on the circle).
- 23-35 polishing, rows of case rings on racks, CNC machining, bracelet links.
- 35-39 cyan anodised trays of links with the brand name stamped (a sudden colour change).
- 39-58 bracelet assembly in black gloves, dial printing, scattered numerals, an eye behind glasses.
- 59-90 the movement: gold plates, jewels and gears through a loupe, hands being set, case closing. Darkest section.
- 90-91 the only wide: a watchmaker at his bench in a pool of light.
- 92-105 the finished watch: case macro, bracelet, dial; loudest point at 104.35 s.
- 106-115 back to paper: hands slide sketches across a table, the pencil drawing of the watch (the circle closes).
- 115.36-119.8 Hermès logo on warm white (4.4 s).

## Cut rhythm
- 90 shots in 120 s. ASL 1.33 s, median 1.18 s, 38 % under 1 s, 45 shots per minute.
- ASL per quarter 1.30 / 0.94 / 1.25 / 2.14 s: fastest in the machining/assembly quarter, slow at the end.
- Cuts per 10 s: 6, 10, 6, 9, 13, 9, 12, 6, 5, 6, 4, 3.
- Longest 4.42 s (end card), 3.64 s (18.56, sketch), 3.36 s (opening sky). Shortest 0.2 s.

## Typography
- None in the film; the product carries the brand (dial, links). End: logo black on warm white, ≈10 % of frame width, centre.

## Colour/grade
- Warm amber/brown with deep black shadows (workshop under tungsten), cool steel in machining, one cyan section (35-39 s), a warm paper white for the sketch beats. Fine grain, low-key light.

## Camera/shots
- Macro and extreme macro, slow slider moves, eyes and hands, almost no faces in full. Only one wide shot in two minutes.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts; match cuts on circles (sketch circle → case ring → eye → loupe); circular structure (sketch at the start and end).

## Sound
- No voice. Score builds from near silence (first 1.3 s silent) to a peak at 104 s, with mechanical sounds of presses and tools. Integrated -16.1 LUFS, **LRA 13.9 LU** (very dynamic), true peak +0.8 dBTP.

## What makes it premium
- The process is the story; no claims, no text.
- Hands, eyes and metal only: the people are craftsmen, not models.
- A circular structure (sketch → object → sketch).
- A wide dynamic range: quiet start, big crescendo, quiet logo.

## Recipe
- 80-90 macro clips (hands, tools, material) for 2 min, 0.5-2 s each; open on a 3-s calm shot (sky or empty bench) and close on the first image again.
- Match-cut on shapes: keep the circle at the same frame position across 3-4 cuts.
- Grade: warm low-key (`sepia(.25) contrast(1.2) brightness(.8)`), one off-palette section of 3-4 s for surprise.
- Sound: start silent 1-1.5 s, mix process SFX on top of the score; end card 4-4.5 s on warm white with the logo only.
