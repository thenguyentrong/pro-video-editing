## Core idea
Hermès films **show and never explain**. No narrator, almost no text, no claim. Each film has one organising principle that is visual: a colour progression, a breath (close and quiet → wide and open), or a circle (sketch → object → sketch). The horse appears as a symbol without comment.

## Rules that repeat
- **No voice** (one whispered tagline in Barénia). Music with a wide dynamic range: LRA 10.5-13.9 LU; quiet masters (-16 to -20.6 LUFS) except the fashion piece.
- **Slow median for luxury:** 1.18-1.42 s, only 21-38 % of shots under 1 s, 34-45 shots per minute.
- **Opening and closing calm:** a locked wide or a sky shot of 3.4-3.7 s first; a 4.4-8.6 s product/logo moment last.
- **Text appears only in the last 10-15 %**, small (≈3.3-5.2 % H), white geometric sans caps or one sentence-case line on the diagonal; or no text at all.
- **End card:** carriage logo black on warm white (#fdfbf6), ≈10 % of frame width, 1.9-4.4 s; the fashion film has no end card.
- **Physical transitions:** smoke as a wipe, a round hole as a mask between colour worlds, match cuts on circles.
- **Product never worn in the hero end:** it lands on an object (a concrete plinth with a hard sun shadow).
- **Craft = hands + eyes + material**, extreme macro, one wide in two minutes.

## Recipe
1. Pick one visual principle (colour chapters / quiet-to-open / circle) and write it in one line.
2. 25-35 shots per minute; open with a 3-4 s locked calm shot; no shot under 0.5 s except a single black flash.
3. Sound: no VO; start near silence (-30 LUFS short-term or 1.3 s digital silence), open up +10 LU at the switch.
4. Last 15 %: product on a plinth, two small lines (Montserrat 3.3vh / 5.2vh, tracking .08-.1em), logo on warm white.
