# Apple — video style (10 examples, 2025-2026)

Examples (folder = slug): `apple_iphone18pro_intro`, `apple_iphoneair_film`, `apple_airpodspro3_film`, `apple_macbookneo_hello`, `apple_ios26_liquidglass_keynote`, `apple_shotoniphone_rose_newtrick`, `apple_detectives_8xzoom`, `apple_privacy_safari_trackers`, `apple_dearapple_watch`, `apple_nido_ep1_vertical`.
All numbers come from the tools (cuts2/stats2, ebur128, audio2) plus checks by eye on sheets and key frames. Music and VO facts come from Apple's official captions (I could not listen). Sizes were measured on 480p frames (±1 px ≈ ±0.2 % H).

## The core idea
Apple films say **one thing at a time**. Each shot or card holds one idea, the frame has a lot of empty space or one strong subject, text is small and calm, and the product or the brand line comes last. The craft is in restraint: few words, one grade, one accent colour, hard cuts, and silence at the end.

## Sub-styles

### A. Product precision spot (iPhone Air, AirPods Pro 3)
- Light studio sweep (#f3f3f5-#f4f3f6, darker grey top band) or pure white void; **46-65 % of frames mostly empty**.
- Rhythm = card / product / card: one-line feature cards hold **1.4-2.0 s**, product moves 1.5-3 s, proof inserts (sample photos) **0.67-0.83 s**.
- Cards: SF Pro Display medium, sentence case, centred, cap ≈ 7 % H (Air) or 2-line labels cap 3.5-4 % H placed in the empty half beside the product (AirPods). One keyword tinted lighter blue-grey, or a gradient for "Apple Intelligence".
- Card changes by 0.3-0.5 s cross-dissolve or by a vertical push (product slides up, text exits up). Hard cuts for product shots, on beats (AirPods: 50 % of cuts on onsets vs 34 % chance).
- Music only, no VO; SFX for the feature (shutters, heartbeat, charging tone, AI tone). The logo ends in silence (Air: 3 s silent logo).

### B. Narrated launch film (iPhone 18 Pro, MacBook Neo)
- VO carries every fact; **no feature supers at all**, only a small end title (cap 3-4.5 % H).
- Two looks: *dark engineering* (iPhone 18 Pro — steel/teal low-saturation grade, macro, exploded renders, CAD, thermal/X-ray, extreme real-world proof, ASL 1.2 s, 53 % of shots < 1 s) and *bright hands-on magic* (MacBook Neo — white table, one pair of hands, practical + VFX tricks, long takes with cuts hidden in hand moves; ~3.7 s per set-up by eye).
- Story as a chain (iPhone: chip → heat → vapor chamber → battery) or as a tour (MacBook: feature by feature, each as a physical gag), bookended by the opening line.
- Sections open with a long breath shot (4-9.5 s) and then accelerate; a short audio suck-out (0.5 s) before the biggest hit.

### C. Keynote segment (Liquid Glass)
- Presenter filmed like a documentary: real studio, daylight, two framings (wide + medium), slow dolly; talking shots 6-21 s; lower third white SF Pro, 2 lines, right-aligned at x ≈ 95 %, y 81-91 %, cap ≈ 3 % H, no box.
- B-roll on every new noun: clean 3D UI on floating/tilted devices on white, hands on devices, physical glass prototypes, architecture opener (12 s, music only).
- ASL 3.8 s but UI animates inside shots every 1-3 s; edited to speech (cuts on onsets 42 % vs 49 % chance). Speech-led mix ≈ −19 LUFS.

### D. Story / proof ad (Shot on iPhone, Detectives, Privacy, Dear Apple, Nido)
- Story first, product last: supers and product appear only in the **last 10-15 %** (Privacy: 44-57 s of 60; Detectives: 54.7-58.4 of 60; ROSÉ: one super at 26.9 s of 30).
- Cinema grammar: 2.39:1 letterbox noir (Detectives), symmetric 4:3 wides (Privacy), music-video fisheye (ROSÉ), telenovela vertical (Nido), testimonial collage on a clean canvas (Dear Apple).
- Proof is built into the film: BTS inserts of the iPhone in its rig (ROSÉ: 4 of 24 shots), the feature used as the film technique (Detectives' crash zooms), real found footage (Dear Apple), "Shot on iPhone 17 Pro" in the title card (Nido).
- End line: white SF Pro medium, centred, cap 4.5-7.5 % H, 1.2-2.5 s; brand line can build word by word in place ("Privacy. That's iPhone."). Apple logo on black (1-3 s), sometimes a partner logo first (T-Mobile).
- Cut to action or speech, not to the beat (onset hits 14-30 % vs 22-33 % chance), except SFX gag hits (Detectives 38 % vs 13 %).

## Typography (all sub-styles)
- Typeface: SF Pro Display / Text (closest free: **Inter / Inter Display**, tracking −0.5 to −1 %). Weight **medium (500)** for supers, semibold for names. Sentence case with a full stop for brand lines.
- Placement: centred, single line, 3-7 words; side-of-product placement only when the product fills one half.
- Size: cap height **3-7.5 % of H** (labels 3-4 %, supers 4.5-7.5 %, end titles 3-4.5 %). Huge text only as a deliberate device (Liquid Glass title 13 %, typed letters up to 14 %+ as the camera pushes in).
- No boxes, bars, outlines or heavy shadows. White on footage, dark slate (#15222f/#1f2d3d) on light; one keyword in a lighter tint (#7d9bb5) or a feature gradient.
- Motion: fade 0.3-0.6 s, in-place word build (~0.7 s per word), cross-dissolve between cards, light sweep across one word, RGB glitch only as a feature metaphor (AirPods).
- Campaign titles may use their own display face (MacBook Neo extended heavy grotesk; Nido flared serif in yellow) — only for the title, never for supers.
- Legal: grey 1.2-2 % H at the bottom, on the end card or under the claim.

## Colour
- Either **light and empty** (white/light grey sweep, black or white product, colour only from UI/feature) or **one controlled grade** (dark steel/teal, warm noir, storybook teal/brick, telenovela green) with **one product colour** as the accent (cherry iPhone 18 Pro, orange iPhone 17 Pro, citrus MacBook Neo).
- Saturated colour is reserved for the feature (rainbow AI frame, thermal, iridescent rings, gradient "Hello").
- Proof footage keeps its own look; vertical clips are pillarboxed on black or framed as windows on a clean canvas — never blurred-fill.

## Camera
Macro on sliders with rack focus; product held by fingertips entering from the frame edge (scale and thinness); top-down exploded parts; photoreal renders; real extreme proof; hands as performers (MacBook); classic film language in narrative ads (low angles, symmetric wides, crash zooms, POV from the grave).

## Transitions
**Hard cuts dominate.** Dissolves only between text cards. Hidden cuts in whips/hand passes; morphs for reveals (silhouette → product, padlock → logo, light → screen); fly-through rings (AirPods). No decorative wipes.

## Sound
- **−16 LUFS integrated** for every ad and launch film (−15.6 to −16.3); keynote ≈ −19 LUFS.
- Product spots: music only + one SFX per action. Launch films: VO + music + dense foley. Story ads: dialogue/song first, music drops for the joke.
- Drop-outs before hits: 0.2 s (AirPods 41.8 s), 0.5 s (iPhone 18 Pro 145.5 s), 0.8 s (Privacy 38.25 s), 1.0 s (Privacy 8.45 s before the song), 1.8 s (Dear Apple 115.8 s).
- Endings: logo or title held in silence (Air 3 s, iPhone 18 Pro 1.5 s, MacBook 0.7 s gap on the title wipe) or a single final sound (heartbeat, lock click).

## What makes it premium (across examples)
1. One idea per shot; nothing competes with it.
2. Restraint in text: ≤ 7 words, small, centred, no boxes.
3. Proof over claims: real footage, BTS rigs, the feature used as the technique.
4. A long breath shot before each fast run.
5. Controlled colour with one accent.
6. Sound designed per action; silence at the end.

## How to recreate with real footage + HTML/GSAP (Hyperframes)
**Tokens**
```css
:root{
  --bg-light:#f4f3f6; --bg-top:#d6d9dd; --ink:#1f2d3d; --ink-soft:#7d9bb5;
  --white:#ffffff; --legal:#8a8a8a; --black:#000;
  --font: "Inter Display","Inter","SF Pro Display",system-ui,sans-serif;
}
.super{font-family:var(--font);font-weight:500;letter-spacing:-0.01em;
  font-size:6.5vh; /* cap ≈ 4.6 % H; use 9.5vh for cap ≈ 7 % */
  position:absolute;inset:0;display:grid;place-items:center;text-align:center}
.super em{font-style:normal;color:var(--ink-soft)}
.label{font-size:4.8vh;line-height:1.15}            /* cap ≈ 3.5 % H, 2 lines */
.legal{font-size:1.6vh;color:var(--legal);position:absolute;bottom:6vh;width:100%;text-align:center}
.lightsweep{background:radial-gradient(120% 80% at 50% 60%,#f6f5f8 0%,#eeeef1 60%,var(--bg-top) 100%)}
```
**Timing**
- Product spot (30-50 s): card 1.5-1.8 s → product 1.5-3 s → card…; proof inserts 0.7 s each with a shutter SFX; total ~25-30 units; logo 2.5-3 s in silence.
- Launch film (2-3 min): VO-driven sections of 15-30 s; each opens with a 4-8 s hold, then 0.5-1.2 s cuts; no supers; end title 2.5 s on black.
- Story ad (30-60 s): 85-90 % story with no text; then 1-3 supers (1.2-2.5 s each); logo 1.5-3 s.
- Vertical series: 3.5-4.5 s shots, one dialogue line per shot, burned-in subtitles 2.5vh at y 71 %.

**Motion (GSAP)**
```js
// card in/out
tl.from(card,{opacity:0,duration:.45,ease:"power1.out"})
  .to(card,{opacity:0,duration:.35},"+=1.5");
// push transition: product rises, text leaves
tl.to(card,{yPercent:-120,duration:.6,ease:"power3.inOut"})
  .from(productClip,{yPercent:100,duration:.6,ease:"power3.inOut"},"<");
// in-place word build ("Privacy. That's iPhone.")
tl.to(".build span",{opacity:1,duration:.15,stagger:.7});
// crash zoom on real footage
tl.to(video,{scale:3,transformOrigin:"62% 40%",duration:.18,ease:"power4.in"});
```
**Grade presets (CSS on `<video>`)**
- Dark engineering: `filter:saturate(.75) contrast(1.08) brightness(.95)` + `#0d2a33` overlay 20 % soft-light; keep the product colour unmasked.
- Noir story: `saturate(.6) contrast(1.1)` + teal 18 % soft-light, letterbox bars 12.7vh.
- Light spot: no grade; white/grey sweep background, ≥ 45 % empty frame.

**Audio**
- Master to −16 LUFS integrated (true peak ≤ −1 dBTP); VO −16, music ducked 8-10 dB under VO.
- One SFX per visible action; cut all audio 0.2-0.8 s before the biggest hit; end on the logo in silence.
