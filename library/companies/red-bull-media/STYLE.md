# Red Bull Media House – athlete short documentaries (PROVISIONAL: 1 film measured)

Examples: `mira-rai-trail-runner` (The Way of the Wildcard, 2020, 15:59; first 300 s measured). Analysed Oct 2026. This folder is for documentary shorts; Red Bull's vertical action clips are in `companies/redbull`. Two more films were chosen (The Rise of Eileen Gu, 2026; The Armed Robber Turned Pro Triathlete, 2018) but YouTube blocked downloads on 20 Oct 2026; add them before treating this as the company style.

## The style in one line
A cinema-look (2.35:1) portrait of an unlikely athlete: one patient cold open, the hardest fact of the past as window-boxed archive, a small "presents" credit, a fast promise montage, a big series logo on an aerial, then interviews with subtitles and near-silent chapter breaks.

## Rules seen so far (with numbers)
1. Cold open = one locked 12.9 s take of the athlete today, room tone only (≈-20 dBFS).
2. Past as record: archive in a 4:3 window on black (56 % of frame width, full height of the 2.35:1 picture), 0.5–1.5 s flashes cut against the face today.
3. Credit "RED BULL MEDIA HOUSE / PRESENTS": tiny wide-tracked caps (≈1.7 % H, 0.3 em) over a running-feet POV.
4. Promise montage 9 cuts per 10 s, a 3.3 s black hold, an archive line, then the title.
5. Title on one 8.3 s aerial: heavy rounded slab caps (≈9 % H) + episode line in light wide-tracked caps.
6. Body: interviews 6–7 s, cutaways 2–4 s, lower thirds without a box (bold condensed name ≈3 % H), subtitles in the lower letterbox bar.
7. Chapter breaks = 8–11 s near-silence at -40…-48 dBFS.
