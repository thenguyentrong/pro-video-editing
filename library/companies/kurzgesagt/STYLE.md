## In one line
One continuous, glowing flat-vector universe narrated by a calm voice: the camera travels through it (pull-outs, zoom-throughs, morphs) so ideas change every 3–5 s while hard cuts come only every 10–14 s, and words on screen are rare and big.

## Sub-styles
1. **Space / scale** (`leave-solar-system`): dark indigo space, stars and galaxy doodles everywhere, bubble zoom-outs, timelines with pins and pill labels, a neon chapter title, recurring cartoon characters as viewer stand-ins. Has the most hard cuts (cut every ≈10 s, median 5.8 s).
2. **Inside the body** (`memories-stored`): purple-black ground, everything that "fires" glows, a recurring girl character, zoom-through into her head, white full-frame term cards with a tracked caps label, script chapter titles. The slowest (median 9.4 s, one 54.7 s shot).
3. **Futurism / history ladder** (`godlike-civilization`): each era in its own palette (space navy, desert sand, mint utopia), isometric tile dioramas that grow, numbered scale diagrams, comic speech-bubble gags, a white caps chapter title with an orange sub-line.

## Measured numbers (first 300 s)
- Hard cuts: ASL 10.0–13.6 s, median 5.8–9.4 s, 6.0–4.4 shots per minute; 0–10 % under 1 s (the only sub-second shots are a 0.37 s flash and sponsor stock), 57–93 % over 3 s.
- Longest single shots 24–55 s (46.0 / 54.7 / 30.8 s): the drawing animates and the camera moves inside them.
- By eye ≈1 new set-up (camera target, reveal, morph) every 3–5 s; ≈8 set-ups in the first 30 s of each film.
- Cuts per 10 s: 0–2 in the story; 4 only in the sponsor's stock montage.
- Cut-on-onset at chance (48–59 % vs 52–56 %): cuts follow the narration, not the music.
- Loudness −14.7 to −16.3 LUFS integrated, loudness range only 2.0–2.3 LU, true peak −2.3 to −2.8 dBTP: a very even, compressed mix.
- Voice median −19.6 to −21.6 dBFS (50 ms RMS), p10 −29.0 to −30.8 dBFS → the score sits only ≈9–10 dB under the voice and is always there (0–0.4 % of windows below −45 dBFS).
- Drop-outs are rare: 1.6 s digital silence between the merch spot and the story (4.4–6.0 s); 0.75 s dip before a chapter title (214.4 s) followed by the biggest rise of that film (+28.8 dB).
- Type on screen: chapter titles 1–2 per 300 s (45–58 s, 215–250 s); term/label cards ≈4 per 300 s; no subtitles.

## Hook (first 30 s)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Common pattern:
1. **0–1 s: narration starts on a moving picture.** No logo, no channel ident, no title. (The only exception is a short own-merch spot before the story, marked "Advertisement".)
2. **0–15 s: one camera journey, zero or one hard cut.** A push-in, zoom-through or slow pan shows the claim as a world (solar system inside a bubble, a memory inside a head, aliens eating planets).
3. **15–20 s: a character beat.** A recurring cartoon person reacts (shock marks, star eyes, a growing speech bubble). This is the human anchor and often the joke.
4. **24–43 s: the question.** Said once, plainly, over the "alternative" image.
5. **45–58 s: chapter title card** with a sound accent (or, in the futurism film, the story runs straight into chapter 1 without a card).
6. **Sponsor** never in the hook: mid-roll at ≈4:08 (29 % of runtime) with a joke segue, or outside the first 300 s.

## Look
- Ground: deep indigo/navy-black #0E0133, #100150, #0A022E, #010925. Violet mid-tones #3405B1, #6A0177, #6E05CA.
- Accents (saturated, used with glow): electric cyan #12F0FC / #0998D4, magenta #AA016A / #C96AB2, warm orange #E98208 / #F2B649, coral skin #EB6F47 / #F38477, mint #2DF7CF / #ABFAED.
- Flat shapes, no outlines on characters or objects; soft inner gradients on round things; bloom/glow on light sources, stars, neurons and UI; star specks and tiny galaxy doodles as texture; no grain, no letterbox.
- Characters: round heads, big eyes with highlights, flat skin colours (coral, orange), simple bodies; reused across films as viewer stand-ins. Animals and aliens are cute (frog scientist, goggled bird, octopus).
- Each chapter world can switch palette completely (space navy → desert sand → mint utopia) — the switch tells the viewer "new world".
- White full-frame "term cards" (#FDFDFD) are the only flat-slide moments, ≈2 s each.

## Type
- Chapter titles (one style per film): neon outline rounded caps (key line ≈14 % H, others ≈7 % H, words pop in one by one with overshoot), or cream retro script ≈10 % H on a glowing object, or white geometric caps ≈7.3 % H with an orange sub-line ≈3.8 % H. Hold ≈2.5–4.5 s.
- Term cards: tracked caps label in dark indigo #3A2A6A, cap ≈4 % H, under one illustration on white.
- Labels: white geometric sans ≈3 % H on hairline leader lines; pills (orange #F0602A, purple #4B2BD0) or a cyan box #17C8F0, cap ≈2.7–3.3 % H.
- One-word emphasis: a single word as a big slanted neon object ("SPEED", ≈18 % H) once per film at most.
- Free stand-ins (guesses): Outfit / Nunito Sans for labels, Fredoka for rounded titles, Pacifico for script.

## Sound
- One narrator, always on top; the score is continuous, ≈9–10 dB under.
- Dense small SFX: pop on every label, whoosh on every whip, sparkle on glows; rises of 17–29 dB on accents.
- Almost no silence. Drop to silence only to separate an ad from the story (1.6 s) or before a chapter title (0.75 s).
- Very even master: LRA ≈2 LU at −15 LUFS.

## Rules
1. Build one illustrated world per chapter and move the camera through it; hard cut only between worlds (every 7–14 s on average, up to 55 s).
2. Change the picture's idea every 3–5 s by camera target, reveal or morph, synced to the narration.
3. Start the narration within 0.5 s of the story's first frame; no logo or title in the first 45 s.
4. Put a recurring character's reaction in the first 20 s.
5. Pose the question at 24–43 s, title the first chapter at 45–58 s.
6. Words on screen: ≤1 label at a time, ≤4 term cards and ≤2 chapter titles per 5 min; no subtitles.
7. Dark indigo ground + 2–3 saturated accents with glow; switch palette only to mark a new world.
8. Score never stops; keep it 9–10 dB under the voice; SFX on every pop and move; master ≈−15 LUFS with ≈2 LU range.
9. Sponsor after the hook (≈4 min) with a joke segue, own-merch spots ≤6 s and labelled.

## Recipe (HTML/GSAP + SVG, all drawn — no footage needed)
Layer stack: gradient ground → star field (canvas or 150 SVG dots) → world group (SVG illustration) → glow layer (`filter: blur()` copies, `mix-blend-mode: screen`) → characters → labels → chapter title.
```css
:root{--ink:#0E0133;--deep:#100150;--violet:#3405B1;--cyan:#12F0FC;--mag:#C96AB2;--orange:#F2B649;--coral:#EB6F47;--mint:#2DF7CF;--card:#FDFDFD;--label:#3A2A6A}
.ground{background:radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 60%,#150369 0%,var(--ink) 60%,#07001F 100%)}
.glow{filter:drop-shadow(0 0 1.2vh currentColor) drop-shadow(0 0 3vh currentColor)}
.pill{font:600 2.7vh "Outfit";color:#fff;background:#F0602A;border-radius:999px;padding:.4vh 1.4vh}
.callout{font:500 3vh "Outfit";color:#fff;border-bottom:.2vh solid #fff;padding-bottom:.4vh}
.termcard{background:var(--card)} .termcard .lbl{font:700 4vh "Outfit";letter-spacing:.12em;text-transform:uppercase;color:var(--label)}
.neon{font:700 7vh "Fredoka";color:#2A1AA8;-webkit-text-stroke:.5vh #7FE3FF;text-shadow:0 0 2vh #4F7BFF}
.script{font:400 10vh "Pacifico";color:#FFF3C8;transform:rotate(-6deg)}
```
```js
// pull-out: the whole world shrinks into a bubble (5.5 s)
tl.fromTo("#world",{scale:4},{scale:1,duration:5.5,ease:"power2.inOut"},t)
  .fromTo("#bubble",{autoAlpha:0,scale:.9},{autoAlpha:1,scale:1,duration:.8},t+4.5);
// zoom-through: mask grows from a point on the character into the next scene
tl.fromTo("#portal",{attr:{r:0}},{attr:{r:2200},duration:3,ease:"power2.in"},t)
  .fromTo("#next",{scale:1.3},{scale:1,duration:1,ease:"power2.out"},t+3);
// idle life on every hold: slow drift + breathing glow
gsap.to("#cam",{x:"-=40",y:"-=12",duration:6,ease:"sine.inOut",yoyo:true,repeat:-1});
// chapter title: words pop in, 0.2 s apart
tl.from(".title .w",{scale:.3,autoAlpha:0,duration:.25,ease:"back.out(2.5)",stagger:.2},t2);
// pill label pop + SFX cue
tl.from(".pill",{scale:0,duration:.3,ease:"back.out(2)"},t3);
```
Timing template (first 60 s): 0–0.5 s voice starts on a moving world → 0–15 s one camera journey (push-in / zoom-through / pull-out), 0–1 cut → 15–20 s character reaction gag → 20–30 s the alternative image → 24–43 s the question → 45–58 s chapter title with a 0.5–0.75 s audio dip before and a hit on it → chapter 1 at a new camera target every 3–5 s, hard cut every 7–14 s.

## Caveats
- Only the first 300 s of each ≈14 min film were measured; later chapters and end cards (Patreon/shop) are not described.
- Hard-cut numbers understate the pace: most idea changes are camera moves or morphs; the "every 3–5 s" set-up rate is by eye from 1 fps contact sheets.
- `leave-solar-system` numbers include the 5.4 s merch spot and 52 s of sponsor read (stock live action, faster cuts); without them the story's ASL is higher.
- Fonts are guesses (the channel uses its own lettering); hex colours are averaged from 480p frames and can be off by a few steps.
- Music was measured for level only (continuous bed, ≈9–10 dB under the voice); its style and instruments are not described.
- Hook timings for the first line and the question come from caption timestamps (±0.5 s).
