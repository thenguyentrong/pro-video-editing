Purpose: trailer (first full trailer, 5 months before release)
One-sentence point: Doom is coming, every hero from every corner of the multiverse is being gathered, and Steve Rogers is back.

Reuse note: analysed on 19–20 Oct 2026 in an earlier analysis run; media already deleted, not downloaded again. ## Cut rhythm
- 65 shots, **ASL 2.20 s**, median 1.46 s. ASL by quarter **3.25 · 1.38 · 1.49 · 5.11 s**: slow – fast – fast – slow.
- 17% of shots under 1 s, 11% over 3 s. Longest: title shot 24.4 s (118.6, one continuous move into the end cards), 7.3 s (17.1), 6.3 s (0), 5.4 s (24.4, the logo). Shortest 0.33–0.46 s (53.8–56.6 s fight).
- Cuts per 10 s: 2 · 4 · 2 · 3 · 8 · 9 · 6 · 5 · 8 · 9 · 2 · 6 · 0 · 0 · 0. The montage runs 40–100 s at 5–9 cuts per 10 s (ASL ~1.4 s); the last 25 s has no cuts at all.
- 34% of cuts land on an audio onset vs. 19% by chance: the montage is cut to the score.

## Typography
- No taglines and no dialogue cards: all text is at the end (title, format, date, countdown).
- Title: "AVENGERS" in the classic chrome logo with green rim light (~half the picture height), "DOOMSDAY" in spaced green extended caps under it, tiny red "MARVEL STUDIOS" box above. It is built in 3D: the camera starts inside the letters (~120 s) and pulls back for ~5 s, then holds ~4.5 s.
- Date: condensed caps with chrome shading, wide tracking (~0.3 em), cap height ~3.3% H (coarse, from the sheet), typed one token per ~1 s, left-aligned start at x ≈ 16% that ends near centre.
- Countdown: monospaced-looking digits with colons, tiny unit labels (MONTHS DAYS HOURS MINUTES SECONDS) under each pair; a "THIS FILM IS NOT YET RATED" box below.

## Colour / grade
Very dark, low-key: most shots sit under 20% luma with one hot light source (window, fire, lightning). Colour per world: teal-steel for Doom, amber for X-Mansion fire, blue-white for Thor, purple for the mutant fight, warm sand for Wakanda. The end cards are green-black (Doom's colour). 2.39:1 bars throughout.

## Camera / shots
Slow push-ins and static held frames in the cold open; symmetrical wide tableaux for team shots (37–39 s hall, 67 s desert line-up); centred close-ups for each hero reveal (the "roll call" shots are 0.8–1.5 s each); a 24 s continuous CG camera move for the title.

## Transitions
Hard cuts in the montage; three long black holds (1.9 s, 1.6 s, 2.7 s) as chapter breaks; fades to and from black around them; the logo comes in on a bright burst. No whips.

## Sound
- Integrated −14.1 LUFS. Loudness by quarter (median M) −16.8 · −13.6 · −13.6 · −16.5.
- Score builds through the montage; loudest at 54.5 s (−8.6 LUFS M, the purple fight).
- **13 s of near-silence** 101–114 s (−10 → −45 LUFS) under the closing scene: no music bed, only the scene.
- Pre-title: 0.35 s gap at −51 dBFS (117.1 s), hit at 118.0 s (+28 dB, largest rise in the trailer), title cut 0.6 s later.
- The countdown ticks once per second; the sound fades to digital silence at 138 s.

## What makes it premium
- Patience: three single shots in the first 24 s, with long black between them, before any logo.
- The montage is a roll call of faces (each 0.8–1.5 s) but the trailer never explains anything with text.
- The biggest reveal (Steve Rogers) is played quietly, in a near-silent dialogue scene, not on a music hit.
- The title is a built 3D object in the film's world (Doom's green window), not text on black.
- A real countdown clock turns the end card into an event.

## Recipe (real footage + HTML/GSAP in Hyperframes)
1. Open with one 5–7 s held shot (slow push `scale 1.0→1.06`, `ease:"none"`), 1.9 s of black, a second 5–7 s shot, 1.6 s black, a third long shot, then the logo.
2. Montage: faces and worlds at 0.8–1.5 s per shot, cuts on score accents (aim for ~1/3 of cuts on onsets).
3. 2.7 s of black, then a 15 s quiet scene with music muted (`data-automation` volume to 0.02).
4. 0.35 s full mute, hit (+28 dB), cut to the title 0.6 s after the hit.
5. Title as a camera pull-back: logo PNG/SVG in a `perspective:1200px` stage, `scale 3.2→1, z -800→0` over 4 s `power2.out`, green rim light via `filter: drop-shadow(0 0 18px #4dff7a88)`; hold 4.5 s.
6. Typed date: one `<span>` per token, each `opacity 0→1` in 0.15 s at 1 s intervals, `font: 500 34px "Barlow Condensed"; letter-spacing:.3em; text-transform:uppercase`, then a live countdown (`setInterval` is not seek-safe in Hyperframes: compute the digits from the timeline time instead).
7. Letterbox 2.39:1, mix at −14 LUFS.
