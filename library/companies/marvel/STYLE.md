## Sub-styles

### 1. Character teaser (60–80 s)
- 6–8 shots of 3.5–8 s, then **one long take of ~21 s** as the payoff. ASL ~7 s; nothing under 1 s.
- No action, no VFX, no faces until late (face first seen at ~47 s of 80). Objects carry the story: ring, helmet, folded suit, baby.
- Locked-off static wides, low-angle detail inserts, a drone opener, one slow push-in.
- Text: one small line on black, built word by word (3 groups, 2.5 s apart, 0.75–1.0 s fades), then a date and a running **countdown clock**.
- Sound: sparse single strikes with 4–5 s decays; text lands on the strikes; a 6.5 s lull (−39 LUFS) before the reveal object.

### 2. Main trailer, classic (Spider-Man)
- Cold open 16 s of action → studio logos (17–21 s) → Act 1 world → "THIS JULY" card at 33% → Act 2 → Act 3 → silence → hit → title → **comedy button** (16 s) → tickets + date.
- Flat cutting: ASL 1.0–1.1 s in every quarter; 45% of shots under 1 s, 1% over 3 s; densest at 60–80 s (11–12 cuts per 10 s).

### 3. Main trailer, event (Doomsday)
- **Slow – fast – fast – slow**: quarters 3.3 · 1.4 · 1.5 · 5.1 s.
- Opens with three single long shots (6.3 s, 7.3 s, …) separated by 1.6–1.9 s of black, before the logo at 24 s.
- Montage = roll call of faces and worlds at 0.8–1.5 s per shot, cut to the score (34% of cuts on onsets vs. 19% chance).
- 2.7 s black, then a 16.6 s closing scene with the music gone (13 s at ~−45 LUFS); the biggest reveal (Steve Rogers) is played *quietly*.
- No tagline cards at all; text only at the end: 3D title, format card, typed date, countdown. The countdown replaces the button.

### 4. Final trailer (Fantastic Four)
- Flat ASL ~1.4 s, more story and family than action; humour beats in the first third.
- Tagline split over **two cards with footage between them** ("THEIR / GREATEST STRENGTH" … "IS / EACH OTHER"), followed by the longest shot of the trailer (8.3 s slow-motion hero shot at 85% of the runtime).
- Gets louder to the end (last-quarter median −12.5 LUFS vs. −15.5…−17.4 before); title cut on the musical peak, no pre-title silence, no button.
- Everything in the film's world: custom retro studio logo, TV-show framing, split screen, cards on the film's steel-blue gradient.

### 5. TV spot (30 s, Super Bowl)
- The full trailer arc compressed ~5×: slow open 8 s (logo + 2 empty wides) → question → 9 s montage (11 shots, ASL 0.82 s) → 1 s black → 3.3 s silent tableau (−35 dBFS) → **+30.7 dB hit** → logo + month (2.6 s).
- Cuts per 10 s: 3 · 9 · 5. Mixed hotter (−13.5 LUFS).
- The question is diegetic (a poster in the set); the studio logo turns to dust (story in the logo).

### 6. Vertical social (9:16)
- **Same edit, same mix**, re-framed: cut list within ±0.15 s of the 16:9 version, no captions, no new hook.
- Re-extracted from a taller master (more picture above/below than 2.39:1), subject-centred; wide ensembles lose their sides.
- End card recomposed: title ~79% of width at ~38% height, long lines left-aligned at x ≈ 12%, all text between 15% and 82% height.

## Measured ranges (across the six)
- **Body cutting:** ASL 1.1–1.4 s in trailers; montage sections 0.8–1.5 s per shot; shortest normal shots 0.33–0.5 s (flash frames 0.2 s).
- **Densest part:** 30–75% of the runtime (Spider-Man 39–52%, F4 49–77%, Doomsday 28–70%, spot 33–67%), 9–12 cuts per 10 s.
- **Endings slow down** in 4 of 5 trailers/spots: title 3.5–4.1 s, closing scenes 15–25 s, end cards 1.6–2.6 s. Exception: F4 keeps 1.2 s shots right into the title.
- **Studio logo:** after a 16–24 s cold open (11–17% of the runtime) in main trailers; first (1.4–2.9 s, customised) in final trailers and spots; absent in the teaser body.
- **Black holds:** 0.8–2.7 s, 1–3 per piece, at act breaks; fades of 0.5–0.8 s around them.
- **Long takes as payoff:** teaser 20.9 s, F4 8.3 s, Doomsday title move 24 s.

## Title cards: typography and timing
- **Few:** 2–5 text moments per piece. Doomsday has zero taglines.
- **Taglines:** 2–4 words, caps, very wide tracking (0.25–0.3 em), main line ~6.7% H with a small connector word (~3.5% H) on top; hold 1.1–1.8 s; hard cut in and out.
- **Date cards:** either big (Spider-Man "JULY 31" ~10% H, 1.6 s) or tiny and wide-tracked (Doomsday / teaser ~2.5–3.3% H) typed one token per ~1 s, then a countdown that ticks each second.
- **Title:** 3.5–4.1 s hold. Main word ~22% H (Spider-Man) or a ~43% H block (F4); always a small "MARVEL STUDIOS" above (~3% H). Entrances: fade from black 0.8 s (Spider-Man), hard cut with letters flickering into place over 1.5 s (F4), 3D camera pull-back over ~4 s (Doomsday).
- **Backgrounds:** black (Spider-Man, teaser), brand gradient (F4 steel blue #1d3247 → #3b5c7c), in-world 3D set (Doomsday's green window), a prop in the scene (Endgame poster).
- **Faces:** extended heavy caps (Spider-Man; free: Michroma), wide geometric caps (F4; free: Josefin Sans / Jost), humanist Title Case (teaser; free: Source Sans 3), condensed chrome caps (Doomsday; free: Barlow Condensed). Font names are guesses from frames.

## Colour and letterbox
- Every 16:9 piece is **2.39–2.41:1** (bars ~12.9% of the height each, top and bottom), including the end cards. The spot file itself is 1920×798. The vertical has no bars.
- Low-key images: most frames are dark with one hot practical source (window, fire, lightning). Colour is coded per world/character (teal-steel Doom, amber fire, blue-white Thor, purple mutants); F4 uses a retro teal/cream/orange palette. Light grain.
- The end card takes the villain's or film's colour (green for Doom, purple for Thanos-era Endgame, steel blue for F4).

## Sound structure
- **Mix:** −14.1 LUFS for trailers (−16.7 for the Sony-led Spider-Man trailer), −13.5 for the TV spot, −15.2 for the teaser.
- **Pre-title silence + hit:** 0.35 s at −51…−59 dBFS → hit +28…+31 dB → title cut 0.6–2.7 s after the hit (Spider-Man, Doomsday). The spot: 2 s at −35 dBFS → +30.7 dB hit *on* the end-card cut.
- **Quiet reveal:** the emotional or big reveal sits in near-silence: Doomsday 13 s at ~−45 LUFS, teaser 6.5 s lull at −39 LUFS, F4 1.5 s dip to −35 LUFS.
- **Small drop-outs:** 0.2–0.65 s before reveals inside the body (Spider-Man 37.5 / 48.1 / 76.0 s, F4 61.2 s before the villain).
- **Comic dead air:** 0.5–0.85 s gaps at −52…−58 dBFS before each joke in the button.
- **Beat sync:** only the event trailer and the spot are cut to the music (27–34% of cuts on onsets vs. 19% chance); the classic and final trailers cut to the action, not the beat.

## What makes it premium
1. Patience before branding: picture first, logo later; the teaser shows the face at ~59% of its runtime.
2. Strong contrast in shot length: 1.1 s bodies against 8–24 s held payoffs.
3. Silence is the loudest tool: every climax is set up by a measured drop-out.
4. Almost no text; the date and the title are the only words in most pieces.
5. The end card belongs to the film's world (3D set, brand gradient, countdown), not a generic template.

## Recipe: a 60–70 s Marvel-style piece with real footage + HTML/GSAP (Hyperframes)
Timeline for 66 s at 24 fps, 1920×1080 with a 2.39:1 letterbox (`.bars` 138 px top/bottom, on top of everything):

| Time | Beat | Edit / sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | cold open: 1–2 long shots (4–6 s), slow push `scale 1→1.06`, `ease:"none"` | drone bed, one strike |
| 8–9.5 s | black 1.5 s | the strike rings out |
| 9.5–11 s | brand sting (logo, or story-logo: letters break into dust) | hit |
| 11–24 s | Act 1: world, ASL ~1.4 s | music cue 1 |
| 24–25.5 s | tagline card 1 (2–3 words) | on a hit |
| 25.5–44 s | Act 2: densest part, ASL 0.8–1.0 s, 10–12 cuts per 10 s; cut ~1/3 on onsets | cue 2, building |
| 44–45.5 s | tagline card 2 | |
| 45.5–52 s | one held payoff shot 5–8 s (slow-motion or a quiet face) | music down to −35…−45 LUFS |
| 52–52.35 s | full mute 0.35 s | −60 dBFS |
| 52.35–55 s | hit +28…+31 dB, 2–3 shots of the biggest action | |
| 55–59 s | title 3.5–4 s | |
| 59–64 s | button (one comic beat, 0.5–0.8 s dead air before the line) or typed date + countdown | |
| 64–66 s | date card 1.6–2 s, fade to silence | |

CSS/GSAP values:
- Grade on every clip: `filter: contrast(1.08) saturate(.9) brightness(.92);` plus a vignette `radial-gradient(ellipse 75% 70% at 50% 50%, transparent 55%, rgba(0,0,0,.55))`.
- Tagline card: centred, main line `font: 600 72px/1 "Josefin Sans"; letter-spacing:.28em; text-transform:uppercase; color:#ece6d6;` (6.7% of 1080), connector word 38px above; background black or a brand gradient; hard cut in, hard cut out, hold 1.1–1.8 s.
- Teaser line: `font: 600 30px "Source Sans 3"; letter-spacing:.3em;` fixed left edge at 15.5% of the width, groups fade `opacity 0→1` + `color #8a8a8a→#fff` over 0.75 s `power1.out`, 2.5 s apart, on music strikes.
- Title: either fade from black (`opacity 0→1`, 0.8 s, `power1.out`) or a 3D pull-back (`perspective:1200px`, logo `scale 3.2→1`, `z -800→0`, 4 s `power2.out`, rim glow `drop-shadow(0 0 18px <brand>88)`).
- Typed date: one `<span>` per token, `opacity 0→1` in 0.15 s, 1 s apart; countdown digits computed from timeline time (seek-safe), one tick SFX per second.
- Sound: `data-automation` volume lanes for the 0.35 s mute (8 frames at 0) and the −40 dB quiet scene; master `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11`.
- Vertical version: same timeline, `object-fit:cover` with a keyframed `object-position` per shot; title at 80% width, 38% height; all text inside 15–82% of the height.
