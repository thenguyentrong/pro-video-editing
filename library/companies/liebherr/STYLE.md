## Sub-styles
1. **Event / group film** - 100 s, ASL ≈1.3 s, 2.35:1 letterbox, dawn opener of booms against blue-hour sky, 4-5 named leaders with 2.5-4.4 s soundbites, crowds, award, careers; flash-cut burst at ≈73 %; cube logo end card; very loud (-10 LUFS).
2. **Employer anthem** - ASL ≈1.0 s, 69 % of shots under 1 s, one VO voice asking and answering, huge white value words (≈13 % H) flashed on the cuts, subtitles inside the letterbox bar, teal-orange sunrise grade, faces to camera, 13.5 s white end card (claim -> URL -> logo).
3. **Customer case** - one buyer in PPE on his site, ASL ≈2.7 s flat across all quarters, white title box with model name + yellow logo strip, white name super, the favourite feature explained in operational terms and shown on the cab screen and in a real lift; ≈8 drone shots; -20.7 LUFS.

## Rules with numbers
- Machine scale first: low angles up booms, a person tiny on a boom, hook against the sun, aerial yards full of cranes. Every film has 6-10 drone shots.
- Brand frame is strict: white boxes, black text, one yellow accent (#FFD000-like), no gradients. Title box top-left/top-right ≈42-46 % H tall.
- Soundbites are short: 2.5-5 s per bite, b-roll inserts of 0.5-3 s between almost every sentence.
- Pace by genre: brand/recruiting 0.8-1.3 s ASL with 0.12-0.2 s flash frames; case study 2.7 s.
- Brand films end on 5-13 s of white card in silence or on a single hit.

## Typography system
- House grotesk (free: Barlow / Barlow Condensed). Titles 500-700 ≈4.2-5.5 % H in white boxes; name supers name 700 + role 400, each ≈2.6-4.5 % H, black on a white box, wipe in 0.35 s.
- Value words: Barlow Condensed 700 ≈13 % H white, no box, cut on/off with the picture.
- Subtitles burned into the lower letterbox bar ≈3.5 % H.

## How they show machines credibly
- Real customer sites and real fairs; famous landmarks in the background (harbour bridges) make places checkable.
- On-machine text is the data: counterweight stencils, boom lettering, the real cab UI with values; no graphic spec cards.
- Features explained by the user in his words, then shown in one real lift.
- Weak spots: no numbers on screen in any of the three; the event film shows a rendered machine only as a screen-in-shot (fine); loudness of the event film (-10 LUFS, +0.3 dBTP) and the anthem (+0.3 dBTP) clips.

## Lessons for a construction-tech startup
- For a case film, copy the SMC structure: who -> project challenge -> favourite feature in operational words -> show it on screen -> show it in the real job -> reliability/support -> sunset close. Add one number card and captions.
- For a recruiting or brand cut, the cheap premium tricks are: sunrise shoot, letterbox, faces looking into the lens, one value word per beat in huge condensed type.
- Keep the brand frame boring and consistent (white box + one accent strip) so the machine is the hero.
