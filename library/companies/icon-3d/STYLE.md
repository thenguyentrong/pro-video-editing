## Sub-styles
1. **Spec launch (render).** Grey, slow CGI camera moves, one spec per shot on a frosted card with a mono tag and dotted HUD guides, music only, ASL ≈3.5 s.
2. **Engineering documentary.** Field + design + leadership interviews, fast design-process cold open (7 cuts / 10 s), the real prototype print with people for scale, short unlabelled render "vision" passages (≈6 % of runtime), no supers.
3. **Project documentary.** Third-party TV news clips as the cold open, huge chapter title with the speaker in front of the letters, two-camera interviews in an empty printed room, drone grids of dozens of slabs, timelapse printing, residents and a crew celebration, 17 s final aerial.

## Rules with numbers
- Slow, calm pace for a construction robot: ASL 3.1-4.7 s, under 8 % of shots below 1 s; only the cold opens run faster (4-7 cuts per 10 s).
- Interview bites 3-9 s, partner/leader bites up to 22 s; b-roll inserts 1.5-4 s.
- Scale is always shown: HUD dimension lines in renders (27 ft, 100 ft, 40 ft); people, lifts and ladders next to printed walls in real footage; drone grids of many slabs.
- Logo: white on black at the end, 6 s.
- Music under everything, cuts on speech or camera moves, not on beats (sync at or below chance).

## Typography system
- Neo-grotesk caps (Helvetica Neue / Inter look) for titles: launch title ≈10.6 % cap height centred; documentary title 3 lines ≈5 % bottom-left; chapter title ≈10.4 % per line, left, behind the speaker.
- Monospaced caps (IBM Plex Mono / Space Mono) for HUD tags, corner labels and spec descriptions (≈1.4-2 %).
- Spec value: light grotesk caps ≈3.8 % on a frosted translucent card (white ≈22-25 % + blur).
- Name super (documentary only): bold caps ≈3.3 % + role caps ≈2 %, white, bottom-left, no box. The engineering doc has no supers at all.

## How ICON shows the machine and site credibly (and where it does not)
- Real proof = drone over the real print with people beside it, timelapse of printing, crew in full PPE, named partner executives, third-party press.
- Real process = sketch wall, CAD screens, scale model, field feedback told by field staff.
- Weak spots: the launch film is 100 % render without a label; renders sit inside the documentary unlabelled; spoken numbers are not shown as text; timelapses carry no speed label.

## Lessons for a construction-tech startup
- Make two films, not one: a render spec film (frosted cards, one number per shot) and a real-site film. Label renders ("Concept render") and timelapses ("Timelapse 20x").
- In the real film open with outside proof (press, partner logo, a named customer), then the problem told by field people, then the machine.
- Put a person next to every big printed or built thing; fly one drone reveal per chapter (6-17 s).
- A big chapter title layered behind the speaker is cheap with an AI matte and looks premium.
