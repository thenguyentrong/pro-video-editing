## In one line
A personal, loud, fast creator documentary: Johnny in a lit, object-filled set talks straight to you, and every claim is answered with something real on screen — a prop, a screen capture, a clip with a full citation, a recreation, a glowing map.

## Sub-styles
1. **Conversation essay** (`i-dont-get-democracy`): cinematic wordless open, then webcam conversation with his producer laid out on off-white paper, internet evidence (YouTube pages, his own past videos with view counts), talk-show and rally clips with subtitles + citation bars. Yellow typed key-phrase captions.
2. **Investigation with props + recreation** (`china-dirty-money`): physical evidence on the desk (cash stacks), undercover footage labelled as such, link-analysis diagrams, dark teal flow maps with photo pins, 2.39:1 actor recreations, webcam experts on black, burned-in yellow subtitles, huge outlined condensed numbers.
3. **Map documentary** (`us-cant-beat-iran`): narrator only; news cold open, then a textured antique-paper map world (hatched empires, octagon flag/portrait pins, glowing engraved-serif labels, persistent chapter tag), real antique maps filmed on a table, atlas flip montages and a "PERSIA" text match-cut at 4 frames per map; single animated map moves of 14–38 s.

## Measured numbers
- Talk-driven films: ASL 2.2 s, median 1.44–1.54 s; 24–27 % of shots under 1 s, 24 % over 3 s.
- Map film: ASL 1.43 s but median 0.42 s — two speeds only: flip montages at 4–6 cuts per second and single map moves of 13.9 / 18.2 / 37.6 s.
- Bursts of 8–11 cuts per 10 s, with single host shots allowed to run 6.5–11 s.
- Flash bursts of 3–5 cuts at 0.21–0.5 s (inserts of hands, keyboard; arrest recreation).
- Cuts land on onsets only at chance (40–48 % vs 41–52 %): cut on speech.
- Loud YouTube-native mix: −15.1 / −15.2 / −16.3 LUFS integrated, loudest −8…−10 LUFS momentary. Voice median −21…−22 dBFS (50 ms RMS), pauses 15–20 dB below → bed present but low.
- Map film keeps a fuller bed (p10 only 13 dB under the voice median, 1.3 % of windows below −45 dBFS) — music carries the long map moves.
- Designed silences: 1.6–3 s near-silence (−63…−69 dBFS) before the host's first line or a punchline, then +27…+36 dB jumps.

## How they show evidence
- **Physical props** in the host shot (cash pushed toward a wide lens, model ship, maps on every wall) — the set itself is research.
- **Screen captures as they are**: full YouTube pages (title, channel, likes), his own video cards with views and age, phone screens.
- **Clip + citation**: third-party clips keep their own look and get a one-line credit bar "Name, *Programme*, Month D, YYYY" bottom left.
- **Labelled raw footage**: "UNDERCOVER FOOTAGE" centred over fisheye hidden-camera clips with a scanline texture.
- **Investigation visuals**: link-analysis graph (people/places/relations), dark world map with glowing arcs and octagon photo pins "NAME / CITY".
- **Recreations** shot like a feature in 2.39:1 letterbox, clearly a different register from reporting.
- **Maps as the main evidence** (map film): one consistent map language for minutes; real atlases and antique engravings filmed physically under a moving light; museum artefacts as rounded cards on black.
- **Match-cut montage**: 12–14 scans of the same word ("PERSIA") or the same country, aligned to the same spot, 4–6 frames each, then hold on the one that matters.
- **Experts** as webcam frames, either on off-white paper (conversation) or on black with rounded corners (investigation).

## Captions & lower thirds
- Key-phrase captions: yellow #FFFF4C caps, Optima-like flared sans, ~3.8 % H, at ~77 % H, typed on letter by letter with the words.
- Subtitles (third-party clips; narration in the investigation film): yellow sentence case, ~3.8–4 % H, bottom centre (~94 % H), no box.
- Numbers/titles: tall condensed caps 10 % H, outline stroke, often behind the host (depth matte).
- Map labels: glowing white engraved serif caps (~5.5 % H) on hatched regions; pin tags in white condensed caps on black; a persistent chapter pill top centre "DATE RANGE • CHAPTER".
- Names: "ADAM / Producer" under a webcam frame, or white caps inside the frame lower left. No branded lower-third bars.

## Graphics restraint & colour
- Warm-vs-teal: host sets lit with warm practicals against teal window light; maps and recreations cold teal/navy; webcams neutral.
- Graphics are few but bold: one map style (dark ground, glow lines), one big-number style (outlined condensed), one caption colour (yellow).
- Map film palette: dark vignetted antique paper, muted empire fills with 45° hatching (brick red, slate blue, orange, yellow-green), white glow labels; light-leak flashes between worlds.
- Texture everywhere: scanlines on macro/undercover, paper grain on layouts, film halation on the set.

## Voice & music mix
- Voice loud and dry at the front (−15 LUFS integrated); bed 15–20 dB under.
- Silence as punctuation: 1.5–3 s drops before the first line, under a demo (practical sound only, 4.5 s), before a joke.
- Clip audio enters hard (+27…+31 dB in 50 ms).

## Rules
1. Put the evidence in the host's hands or on his desk when possible; a prop beats a graphic.
2. Every borrowed clip gets a citation bar (Name, *Programme*, date) and subtitles; every borrowed screen is shown whole enough to be recognised (page, title, views).
3. Pace: median shot ~1.5 s, bursts of 8–11 cuts per 10 s, but let the host run 6–11 s when he is the point.
4. Separate registers visually: host set (warm, 16:9), recreation (2.39:1, cold), maps (dark teal glow), raw footage (labelled, textured).
5. Captions are yellow and small (3.8 % H); only key phrases are typed on; full subtitles only for others' voices or long narration.
6. Maps get their own consistent world (paper texture, hatching, octagon pins, chapter pill) and long continuous camera moves (14–38 s); real printed maps are cut in as fast flip montages (0.17–0.4 s).
7. One huge typographic moment per act: outlined condensed caps at ~10 % H for a number or a chapter phrase, behind the host if possible.
8. Mix loud (−15…−16 LUFS) with 1.5–3 s silences before the key line; clips cut in at full level.
9. Show the process (producer webcam, "I don't get it", his past videos) — the honesty is part of the proof.

Timing template (first 150 s): 0–8 s raw/undercover or wordless cinematic open (8 cuts) → 1.5–2.5 s silence → host at desk with a prop (7–11 s) → evidence bursts (screens, clips with citation, maps) at ~1.5 s → one recreation or demo (letterbox or practical sound) → expert webcams with subtitles → big outlined number → host lands the point → chapter phrase behind host.

## Caveats
- The map film's cut counts were corrected by eye (match-cut montage at 107–109 s, dissolves at 102/105 s, light leak at 112 s).
- Fonts are guesses: the caption face reads as Optima-like (Belleza/Linux Biolinum are free stand-ins); the condensed face as Bebas/Anton-like.
- `i-dont-get-democracy` numbers cover only 90 s (reused data); its key frames come from a fresh download that may be offset by ~0.5 s around 32 s.
- I measured levels only; music style is not described.
