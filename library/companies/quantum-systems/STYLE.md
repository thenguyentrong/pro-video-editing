## Core idea
Datasheet honesty in a geometric sans. Every product appears with its numbers (span, weight, endurance) or with one named component and one line of benefit. Proof is native sensor footage and real UI with a caption that names the capability, anchored by dates and counts.

## House rules (measured)
- **Type:** geometric sans (closest free: Montserrat). Title bold + light pair (≈16 % H). Product name ≈4.5 % + descriptor ≈1.8 %. Spec table in 2 columns ≈2.8 %. Captions: bold label ≈3.5-4 % + one regular line ≈2-2.3 %, bottom-left (proof) or bottom-right (feature film). White, no boxes.
- **Data layer:** tiny monospaced telemetry columns (≈1.2 %), running timecode, split-screen panels on black with product cut-outs; a small product icon in the top-right corner on operational footage.
- **Rhythm by type:** feature reveal = one feature per ≈3.6 s cut on the beat (64 % of cuts on onsets); brand film = even 1.8 s pulse; proof = holds of 3-15 s on sensor footage.
- **Sound:** silence under the opening title (0-4 s) then the first hit; drop-out 0.5-1.7 s before the logo or the first product. Brand film mastered very loud (-8.9 LUFS); proof and CGI films -15.6 to -19.0 LUFS.

## How they make trust visible
- A timeline with real dates and a deployment count opens the proof film.
- Operational footage keeps its own overlays (target boxes, coordinates, stamps); captions say what the system did in one line.
- Specs with units on every product; real ground-station screens next to real soldiers.
- Real employees close the brand film.
- Weak spots: CGI from the feature film is reused in the proof film; long black text sections; a looped music bed.

## Recipe
1. Feature reveal: silent title 4 s; 9 features x 3.5 s, label right-bottom (700 4vh + 400 2.3vh); cut every 2 bars; 1.5 s drop-out; logo.
2. Brand film: calm VO ≈1.6 words/s (question -> contrast -> claim -> tagline); tagline super top-left 5vh; product name + spec table per product; real UI screens; employee grid; logo + tagline 6 s.
3. Proof compilation: dated timeline on black; section cards; native sensor footage with bottom-left captions held for the whole take; field tests with captions; closing two lines; logo.
