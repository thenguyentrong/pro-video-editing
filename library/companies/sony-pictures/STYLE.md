## Core idea
Sony's horror trailers are built from the **brand colour and silence**: one hard colour (pure red for Resident Evil, red title on ember black for The Bone Temple) carries every card, the title and the date, and the mixes are quieter than the blockbuster norm (−15.3 and −17.9 LUFS). Logos are allowed late (12–32 %), after a dialogue or atmosphere open. The 2.39:1 letterbox stays on cards too. Both long trailers end on a **silent poster card of ≈7 s** after the date.

- **Text**: 6–8 text moments in the long cuts; one or two words per card, very large (12–33 % H), in a heavy condensed or heavy grotesque face with a rough texture.
- **Pace**: the teaser is slow (ASL 2.5 s, a 16.7 s take); the main trailer is near the norm (1.47 s) and speeds up every quarter.
- **Talk**: 77 % (RE), 72 % (Bone Temple), ≈94 % (GOAT).

## Sub-styles
**A. Horror teaser, slow dread (Resident Evil).** A 26 s phone call in three shots; director credits in brand red over the long take; three pure-red tagline cards with black cracked type ≈30 % H per line; 2.5 s black before the title; −18 LUFS; 7 s silent poster.

**B. Horror main trailer, silence-driven (Bone Temple).** A 5.75 s near-silence at 21–25 %, a 1.3 s true silence (−72 dBFS) at 54 %, each followed by +42…+60 dB rises; a four-card tagline (one word per card, each card a different plate: ember black, burning ring, landscape); final hit +48.5 dB and a fire montage at 0.5 s per shot.

**C. Talent spot for animation (GOAT).** A real star and a real animal on a stage lit in the film's colours, intercut with animation; the joke gets the longest shot (6 s) and the only quiet; title at 82 %, date at the end.

## Recipe
1. Pick one brand colour; use it for every card, credit, title and date.
2. Open on story or talent; logos at 12–32 %.
3. Put the "who made it" credits inside a held shot.
4. Split the tagline into 3–4 cards of 1–2 words, 1.0–2.1 s each, shots between.
5. Horror: 3–6 s of near-silence early and 1–1.5 s before a scare, hits of +30 dB or more; master −15…−18 LUFS.
6. Title at 82–88 %, credits, date, then (optional) a silent poster card.
