# Patagonia – activist brand films (PROVISIONAL: 1 film measured)

Examples: `whats-next` (2:00, 2023, 50th-year brand film). Analysed Oct 2026. Two more films were chosen (The High Life, 2023, Patagonia Films; Too Old Not To, 2026, Patagonia Australia) but YouTube blocked downloads on 20 Oct 2026; add them before treating this as the company style.

## The style in one line
The brand's own documentary archive (surf, climbing, protest, repair) cut into one argument: the company has always done this, so the next step (protecting the planet) is credible. No product, no claim card.

## Rules seen so far (with numbers)
1. Archive first: 16 mm van-and-surfboards opener with the logo centred (cap ≈4 % H) for ≈2 s.
2. The activist turn sits inside the montage at ≈16 % of the runtime (a painted dam, a protest headband, a march), with no title card.
3. Text only in the world: banners, a kayak slogan on water, a patch, a handwritten line. No captions or lower thirds.
4. Fastest point late: 5 shots of 0.38–0.5 s at 86–88 % of the runtime, then slower again.
5. One quiet beat before the payoff: 0.9 s drop to -33 dBFS, the founder's face.
6. End line in two voices: bold sans "Next ▸ is" + italic serif phrase (≈4.5 % H, left at x ≈25 %), typed on and erased, the ▸ glyph becoming the logo.
