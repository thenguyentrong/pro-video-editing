# Patagonia – brand documentaries (2023–2026)

Examples: `whats-next` (2:00 activist brand film, 2023), `the-high-life` (Patagonia Films short doc, 2023, first 300 s of 15:30), `too-old-not-to` (Patagonia Australia portrait doc, 2026, first 300 s of 6:35). All on Patagonia's own YouTube channels, 16:9. Analysed Oct 2026.

## The style in one line
Real people outdoors, filmed close and without gloss; the brand appears as a small wordmark or a "Films" label and never shows a product; the argument (protect places, keep going, repair) is made by the people and the archive.

## Core idea
Three registers from one brand: a 2-minute archive anthem that states the mission, slow place films that let a community speak for 15 minutes, and warm portrait films with a hand-made type system. All three keep the product out and the brand tiny.

## Rules (with numbers)
1. **Brand mark small, once.** Centred wordmark ≈4–5 % H over the first footage (What's Next 0.9–3 s, Too Old Not To 6–7.5 s) or a "patagonia FILMS" card on black ≈3 % H (High Life 62–68 s).
2. **Open in the place.** First shots are long and wordless: High Life 9.0 / 8.7 / 11.4 s mountain takes, Too Old Not To 7.6 s underwater; 2–2.5 s of silence or ambience first.
3. **Title late or quiet.** High Life titles at 80 s (sentence-case bold sans ≈5 % H over a dark doorway); Too Old Not To at 13 s as hand-lettered stacked words (≈6 % H) with a script signature.
4. **Text in the world.** Real banners, a kayak slogan, handwriting (What's Next); hand-lettered acknowledgements, maps and kinetic words (Too Old Not To); subtitles for non-English speech (High Life).
5. **Interviews at home.** Kitchen, sofa, with family or memorabilia in frame; 6–12 s holds.
6. **Archive as history.** 16 mm and early video in the anthem; black-and-white prints on black (3–4 s) in the place film; medals and race numbers as objects in the portrait.
7. **Pace by register.** Anthem 1.45 s ASL with one 5-shot burst of 0.38–0.5 s at 86–88 %; portrait 2.3 s with a 9-cut hook; place doc 4.8 s with the first cut after 9 s.
8. **Loudness by register.** Docs -16 to -20 LUFS; the anthem is mastered hot (-9.7, +2 dBTP); do not copy that.

## Recipe (real footage + HTML/GSAP, 1920x1080)
- Anthem: 80–85 archive + modern clips at 1.2–2.0 s, protest turn at ≈16 %, burst at ≈86 %, end line "bold sans + italic serif" on a 6 s nature shot.
- Place doc: 2.5 s black, 4 long takes, first interview at ≈35 s, credits on black at ≈60 s, title at ≈80 s; body 3.5–5 s.
- Portrait doc: acknowledgement + wordmark on underwater footage, 9-cut hook with goggle flashes, hand-lettered title (`Permanent Marker` / `Caveat`), body 2.2–2.6 s, a hand-drawn map, kinetic handwritten words near the end.
