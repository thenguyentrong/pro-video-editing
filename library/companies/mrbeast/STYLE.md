## In one line
A loud, bright, never-still challenge film: the whole premise, the prize and the cast are on screen in the first 4–15 s, and from then on a number (day, calories, percent, N/100) is always visible and changes every minute, so there is always a reason to keep watching.

## Sub-styles
1. **Contestant endurance challenge** (`grocery-1m`): one built set (a real stocked store), a giant prize prop with a counting display, "DAY N" tag on every shot, calorie/dollar pills attached to objects, a new rule every 50–100 s, purple night scenes, B&W flashbacks to the earlier video. ASL 2.1 s, flat across the whole window.
2. **Multi-team race** (`extreme-places`): three teams in three biomes, colour-coded clothes = colour-coded graphics, triptych split screens, neon route lines and a neon prize label on the goal, race HUD with bars and percent every ~100 s, 3D diorama standings. ASL 2.6 s (slower, landscapes need time).
3. **Philanthropy montage** (`100-kids-wishes`): many short segments (5–40 s each) at landmarks, a counter "N/100" that grows big when it jumps, celebrity name labels in yellow comic caps, white glow flashes between places, a short emotional story before the fun. Fastest: ASL 1.7 s, 24 % of shots under 1 s.

## Measured numbers (first 300 s of each)
- ASL 1.74–2.59 s, median 1.37–2.12 s; 23–34 shots per minute. ASL per quarter stays inside ±0.6 s except one slow scene: there is no "slow middle".
- Cuts per 10 s: typically 3–8, peaks 8–10 (face burst, list inserts), and a 10 s window with 0–1 cuts appears once per film at most (the opener or one long reaction take).
- Longest shots: the opening walk-and-talk 8.7 s; a whiteout / jungle walk 8.5 s; a reaction take 10.9 s. Everything else under 6 s.
- Shortest: flash bursts of 0.10–0.43 s (9 faces in 1.8 s), list inserts 0.5–0.6 s, jump-cut stutters 0.13–0.33 s.
- Cuts on audio onsets 30–50 % vs 28–46 % chance: they cut on speech and reactions, not on the music beat.
- Very loud masters: −8.0 / −8.9 / −11.6 LUFS integrated (first 30 s: −7.8 / −9.6 / −9.0), LRA 3.6–6.8 LU, true peak +1.0 to +1.6 dBTP on the downloaded stream.
- Dense bed: 50 ms RMS median −11.3 / −13.6 / −17.2 dBFS, p10 only 9–11 dB lower; 0.0–0.3 % of windows under −45 dBFS.
- Drop-outs are short (0.3–0.7 s), 3–6 per 5 min, and each sits right before a reveal; re-entries +26 to +47 dB.

## Hook (first 30 s)
The common pattern, with the times measured in the three films:

|---|---|---|---|

| Prize / goal / count on screen | cash prop counts up to $1,000,000 at 5–7 s | neon goal outline 15–16 s, "$100,000" 16–19 s | face burst = "100" at 4.1–6.0 s; counter 0 → 1/100 at 22–23 s |
| Cast + scale shown in one image | four contestants in front of the prop at 5–8 s; high wides 12–22 s | triptych of 3 teams at 3.5–7.4 s; drone pull-backs + route lines 10–14 s | castle group at 0–4 s; creator video wall 33–47 s |
| Doubt / taunt / impossible | ≈22 s | ≈17–19 s | (emotional story instead, 6–17 s) |
| Clock starts | "DAY 1" tag at 28.8 s | countdown + start line 21–27.6 s | first wish done, counter at 1/100, 23 s |
| Cuts in 0–30 s | 14 | 9 (3 split screens) | 21 |
- No title card, no logo, no channel intro, no sponsor in the first 30 s (no sponsor read at all in any 300 s window studied).
- The first shot is either one long proof shot (8.7 s) or a wide that reads in 1 s, followed by the first fast section at 4–10 s.
- Short drop-outs (0.3 s) sit inside the hook before the first hit (11.95 s, 17.1 s).
- Re-hook at 30–50 s with a scale promise (bigger guests, a projection of how long it would take, each team's disadvantage), then a new rule, twist or standings every 50–100 s.

## Retention devices
- **Persistent game state**: "DAY N" (≈6 % H top-left), "N/100" (≈3.8 % H top-left), race HUD bars bottom-left. It never leaves the frame.
- **Numbers attached to things**: calorie pills on food, a dollar pill that ticks down, a neon prize over the helicopter, a counting display on the cash prop.
- **The jump**: when the number changes it grows (counter to ≈9.5 % H) or gets its own full-screen card (DAY card ≈25 % H) for about 1 s.
- **Standings / progress checks** every ~100 s (percent bars, diorama, under 1 % eaten).
- **Twist or bigger-than-expected payoff** each segment (spoiled food costs money; a meet-and-greet becomes an explosion).
- **Promise early, pay late**: a famous guest named at 39 s appears at 205 s.
- **Worlds you can read in one frame**: bright day, purple night, B&W past; one colour per team; one landmark per wish.

## Type
- Two families: (1) heavy wide/rounded sans for numbers and tags (chrome white-to-grey gradient with a dark outline, or white with shadow); (2) comic italic caps in lemon yellow with a black outline for names and shouts.
- Free stand-ins: Russo One / Archivo Black (chrome day tags), Montserrat ExtraBold (counters, HUD), Lilita One / Luckiest Guy (neon prize), Komika Axis / Bangers (yellow names, shout captions).
- Sizes: corner tag 3.8–6 % H; pill number ≈7 % H; prize label ≈8 % H; name label ≈7 % H at ≈19 % H from the top; shout caption ≈3 % H at ≈92 % H; full-screen day card ≈25 % H.
- Colours: lemon yellow ≈#F5FF10, neon green ≈#39FF14 (prize), calorie green ≈#3FA463, minus red, team cyan/green/orange; glows in the same colour.
- Motion: pop on with an overshoot (scale 0 → 1, 0.25 s, back-out), hold 1.5–4 s, cut off. No full subtitles; captions only on shouted words.

## Rules
1. State the premise in the first sentence over a picture of it; show the prize as an object with a number within 5–19 s.
2. Show the whole cast and the scale in one image before 15 s (group wide, triptych, drone pull-back).
3. Keep a game-state number on screen from the moment the clock starts (by 23–29 s) to the end.
4. Pace: ASL 1.7–2.6 s, median 1.4–2.1 s; no shot over ≈11 s, and only one or two over 8 s per 5 min.
5. Every 50–100 s something must change: a rule, a twist, standings, a new location or guest.
6. When a number changes, make it big for ≈1 s, then put it back in the corner.
7. Bed under everything; silence only as 0.3–0.7 s gaps right before a reveal, then a +30 to +47 dB hit.
8. Bright, saturated, high-key picture; separate worlds by colour (night purple, past B&W, team colours).
9. Use CG only for game information (counters, routes, standings, projections), never to fake the event itself.
10. Flash bursts (0.1–0.4 s shots) only once or twice per 5 min, for a list or a crowd of faces.

Timing template (first 60 s): 0–4/9 s premise over one wide or one walk-and-talk → prize number on screen by 5–19 s → cast/scale image by 15 s → taunt or "impossible" ≈17–22 s → clock starts (tag/counter/countdown) 21–29 s → first action at 1.5–2.5 s per shot → re-hook (scale promise, disadvantages, projection) 30–50 s → first rule/twist or standings by 90–105 s.
Mix: continuous music + SFX bed; 0.3–0.7 s gap before each reveal; the channel masters at −8 to −12 LUFS, but deliver at −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP (platforms turn loud uploads down; the stream's +1 dBTP overs would clip).

## Caveats
- Only the first 300 s of each film was measured; later acts (eliminations, endings) may cut differently.
- The scene detector missed many cuts in low-contrast jungle/snow shots and behind white flashes; all three cut lists were corrected by eye (details in each `metrics.json` under `manual_corrections`). Split screens count as one shot.
- Loudness and true peak come from the YouTube stream (decoded Opus), not the original master; overs above 0 dBTP may partly be codec overshoot.
- Fonts are guesses from 480p frames; colour hex values are sampled from compressed frames and marked ≈.
- I measured levels only; music style and SFX types are not described beyond what the levels and subtitle music marks show.
- No sponsor segment appeared in any of the three 300 s windows, so sponsor placement is not covered here.
