## Sub-styles
1. **Cinematic launch (Exosystem):** VO essay in three acts: heritage (gloves, tools) -> product + "it can dig" -> fear and eight safety layers -> legacy aerials. No text on screen; physical branding on the machine; cuts 5-8 per 10 s.
2. **Interview explainer (RPD 35):** two lab interviews + one site interview with white diagonal lower-thirds; spoken numbers; one animated line chart; one separated 22 s CGI exploded view in blue wireframe on black; numbered safety list next to a 14 s talking head.
3. **Workflow ad (Physical AI):** one worker's day as the spine, one VO line per step (safety zone, load plans, robots work, one person supervises, data, night work), 60 s, even 4-6 cuts per 10 s, one text label on the UI.

## House rules
- **Safety is always a dedicated block** (≈20 % of runtime): E-stop button pressed on camera, tape and warning signs, LEDs, numbered list of the eight layers, "zero incidents" spoken by a person in PPE.
- **Robots shown with people and vehicles** for scale and trust; the operator steps out of the cab or supervises from a truck.
- **Real UI on a laptop in a truck** (trench path, session config), never full-screen.
- **CGI only in its own block** (black background, blue wireframe) - except the 2026 data-scan glow composited on real piles.
- **Brand palette = machine palette:** black + yellow, blue for "AI/data"; persistent white "B" bug bottom-right ≈8 % H.
- Logo end on black, white condensed wordmark, 4-10 s with music tail.

## Typography system
- Lower third: white panel with a diagonal right edge, bottom-left ≈32 % W x 27 % H; name bold ≈3.3 % H, role ≈2.6 % H grey. Closest free: Inter / Manrope.
- Numbered list: yellow circle ≈7 % H with a black number + white semibold label ≈3.8 % H, centred in the empty half of a talking-head shot, one item per spoken word.
- Chart: white bold title ≈4 % H top-left, one yellow line, dotted for forecast.
- UI label: acronym bold + words regular, ≈7 % H font size, left.
- Logo: condensed bold "BUILT" ≈10 % H + spaced "ROBOTICS" ≈3 % (closest free: Oswald Bold).

## How they show the machine / site credibly
- Retrofit proof: a crane truck lifts the kit onto a stock excavator on site.
- Autonomy proof: empty cab, cab POV with no driver, one operator managing several machines from a truck.
- Numbers spoken by named people: 200 piles on board (≈30,000 lb), ≈100 piles/day manual vs 300+ robot, 18,000+ autonomous hours, zero robot-related incidents.
- Drone top-downs of trench lines and pile grids show scale and precision.
- Weak spots: no speed labels; the 2026 blue scan glow is unlabelled CGI on real footage.

## Lessons for a construction-tech startup
- Give safety its own block with one physical shot per safety layer and a person in PPE saying the record.
- Let named field staff say the numbers; put a white diagonal lower-third on them.
- Keep any CGI explainer in a separate black-background block; label visualisations on real footage.
- A 60 s "how to deploy" film built on one worker's day is cheap to shoot and answers buyers' real questions.
