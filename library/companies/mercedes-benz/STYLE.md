## Core idea
Mercedes films sell **belonging and calm**. The shared line "Welcome home." closes both the heritage and the launch film in a small serif. Pace depends on the tier: the mass-market launch cuts like a commercial (median 1.1 s), the heritage film moves fast with panels instead of cuts, and the top luxury tier (Maybach) never cuts under 1 s.

## Rules that repeat
- **Serif for the brand voice, sans for information.** Lines like "Welcome home." / "WELCOME TO BEYOND" in a light serif (≈3.3-3.8 % H), labels and captions in a light sans (≈2-4.8 % H).
- **Small type, centred or on an edge;** no boxes, no lower thirds. Legal/consumption line along the bottom (≈1.2 % H) whenever a current car is shown.
- **Slower with price:** median 1.12 s (GLC) → 1.64 s and 0 % under 1 s (Maybach).
- **The end is one long shot or black:** GLC's last road shot runs 11.5 s under two supers; Maybach dissolves and fades for 14 s into two logos on black.
- **Silence at the end:** 2.7 s under the Maybach star; 10 s silent web slate ("Visit us") on the YouTube versions of the heritage and launch films - do not copy that slate into a film.
- **Heritage as a catalogue:** every car on the same grey cyc, chapter words (VISIONARY, SAFETY, DESIGN ...) as rotated edge labels, sliding split-screen panels at one change every ≈1.35 s, a growing tile grid as finale.
- Loudness -13.1 to -16.0 LUFS; Maybach is the quietest.

## Recipe
1. Pick the tier: commercial (median ≈1.1 s, dialogue), heritage (panels, one change per 1-1.5 s, continuous narrator), luxury (no shot < 1 s, no voice, 2.0:1 letterbox).
2. One stage or one world for all shots (grey cyc, or one invented landscape).
3. Type: serif `Cormorant Garamond` 3.3-3.8vh for brand lines (caps + .12em tracking for luxury, sentence case for "Welcome home."); `Inter Light` caps for labels.
4. End: one long shot with the line, then logo on black and 2-3 s silence.
