Purpose: explainer (civics/data, series episode), producer on camera + experts + a congressman

- Every legal or historical claim has its paper: the bill, the Constitution clause, the census form, the 1920s newspaper, each with only the key phrase highlighted.
- Paper physics (crumple, unroll, drop) gives documents weight without fake 3D.
- A real congressman filmed properly next to webcam experts — mixed production levels used honestly.

Recipe (real footage + HTML/GSAP):
- Paper ground: `#E7E7E7` + a creased-paper texture PNG at 60 % opacity (`mix-blend-mode:multiply`).
- Hemicycle: compute seats on concentric arcs (rows = 8–10, dot r = 0.8 % H); colour `#6C7FFF` / `#F52563` / grey `#6D6D6F` for vacancies; year label `italic 600 3.5vh 'Source Serif 4'` under the arc; per year `gsap.to(dots,{scale:1,stagger:{each:.004,from:'center'},duration:.3})` and swap the year every 1 s.
- Dot-matrix number: draw numerals on a 5×7 grid with `<circle>`s, `#794EC8`, collapse with `gsap.to(dots,{x:cx,y:cy,duration:.6,ease:'power3.in',stagger:.002})`.

- Webcam card: `border-radius:2.2vh; border:.6vh solid #fff; box-shadow:0 .5vh 2vh rgba(0,0,0,.15); width:80vw` on the paper ground.
