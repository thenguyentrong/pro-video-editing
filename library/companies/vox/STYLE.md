## In one line
A calm, voice-led explainer that proves each claim with a real source (scan, study, headline, archive, news clip) and spends its motion budget on one or two long hand-made graphics, with lemon yellow as the only "look here" colour.

## Sub-styles
1. **Host-led history/politics** (`one-china`): producer on camera in a real office, archive newsreel, real object scans (passports, a CIA 1949 map) on graph paper, remote expert. Archive bursts of 0.75–1.5 s between 8–20 s graphic or talking shots.
2. **Narrator + primary documents** (`ambulance-crisis`): no host; drone b-roll, the actual study map and report pages with highlighter, flat vector "mechanism" graphics, webcam experts, typed chapter questions. Fast open (ASL ~2 s for 40 s).
3. **Science from the news** (`super-el-nino`): other outlets' clips and headlines prove "it is happening", stock nature footage, one 18.6 s animated flat map for the mechanism, data globe from a science agency, print ephemera for word origins. Slowest cut (median 4.2 s, 3 % of shots < 1 s).
4. **Data/civics series on paper** (`house-too-small`): producer on camera, a grey paper world for every graphic, hemicycle seat charts animated across years, the bill and the Constitution clause re-set on torn paper strips that unroll, 1920s newspaper macros with one word highlighted, a series title card. Busiest of the four (median 1.5 s) because history is told with 1–2 s archive and print shots.

## Measured numbers (first 150 s)
- ASL 2.2–5.0 s, median 1.5–4.2 s. 3–19 % of shots under 1 s, 21–73 % over 3 s. Cuts per 10 s peak at 7–8 (montage), typical 1–4.
- Three of four films have one continuous animated graphic of 18.6–19.9 s; the data episode uses several 7–9 s paper/chart graphics instead. Object/document shots hold 6–13 s.
- Cuts sit on sentence boundaries, not beats: cut-on-onset = chance in all four (48–61 % vs 51–61 %).
- Logo or series sting 3.4–4.7 s at 35–80 s, then a sound drop of 1.0–2.15 s (−12…−30 → −41…−56 LUFS) and a hard re-entry (+28…+45 LU) on the next shot. Same in all four.
- Loudness varies a lot: −14.1 to −27.0 LUFS integrated (three of four at −14…−20). Voice median −18…−34 dBFS (50 ms RMS); pauses (p10) sit 13–15 dB under the voice median → bed low or absent.

## How they show evidence
- **The primary source on screen**: the real study map with its own title and "Author, et al. | Month Year" citation; the report's "Prepared by" page; the executive-summary page zoomed so it runs off frame, one sentence highlighted. The study author then appears on webcam.
- **Objects on a neutral ground**: scanned passports in a row on off-white graph paper (#FAFBF6), yellow outline box drawn around the word that matters.
- **Old maps as base layers**: a scanned 1949 CIA map, re-tinted (one region red, sea pale blue), credited "CIA, 1949" in the corner, with portraits and label tags placed on it.
- **Other outlets as witnesses**: NBC, Al Jazeera, CBC, Fox clips kept with their original chyrons; CNN and Vox headline screenshots with highlighter. A small source label top left.
- **Data in a familiar form**: the hemicycle seat chart (blue #6C7FFF / red #F52563 dots, grey vacancies) animated year by year; hero numbers in dot-matrix numerals.
- **Paper mechanics for legal text**: the key sentence of a bill or the Constitution re-set large in serif on a torn strip that unrolls over the original page, highlight on the phrase, then it rolls back up. 1920s newspaper macros with ink bleed and one word highlighted, source tag top left.
- **Archive** is desaturated and lifted (grey, low contrast) so yellow captions read; it comes in bursts of 4–6 cuts.
- **Mechanism graphics** are flat, 2-colour-plus-accent, and move inside one long shot (clock that counts response time; trade-wind loops reversing into a warm plume).

## Captions & lower thirds
- No running subtitles. Only keyword captions: 1–4 words in heavy grotesque caps, lemon yellow #FEFF0F, cap height 6–10 % H, centred, no box, cut on with the spoken word, held 1.5–3 s.
- Typed questions as chapter cards: white heavy caps ~6.3 % H with a blinking text cursor, over dark footage or a dark navy (#213745) graphic.
- Lower third: thin yellow vertical rule (~11 % H tall) at ~12.5 % W, bold serif name ~5–5.5 % H, small sans role ~2.5 % H, typed on letter by letter, ~4 s hold, cut off. Same for host, producer and experts.
- Bug: white serif "Vox" wordmark top right (~8 % H) on footage, swapped for "PRESENTED BY <sponsor>" in sponsored parts.

## Graphics restraint & colour
- One accent: yellow (#FEFF0F / #FFFF00) for keywords, highlighter and outline boxes. Never arrows, emoji or stickers.
- Category colours are learned once and kept (PRC red #C2455A vs ROC green #257343; target-time yellow vs late pink #F1627E).
- Grounds: grey creased paper #E7E7E7, off-white graph paper #FAFBF6, pale ice-blue grid #E1F6F7, mint halftone ocean, or a flat royal blue #2362A9. Maps use a 5-step single-hue ramp.
- Source/credit text on every borrowed image or map, small, in a corner.

## Voice & music mix
- Voice carries everything; bed 13–15 dB under the voice median, or near-silent pauses (El Niño: 16 % of 50 ms windows below −45 dBFS).
- Designed silence after the logo sting (1–2 s), and 0.15–0.6 s true gaps before experts.
- Third-party clips and archive bring their own sound for ~1 s (rises of 30–44 dB in 50 ms windows), then duck.

## Rules
1. Prove each claim with the primary source on screen (study map + citation, report page, original headline, original news chyron) — then show the person behind it.
2. Spend motion on one long animated graphic per ~2.5 min (18–20 s, continuous, no cuts inside); keep everything else as plain cuts.
3. Yellow #FEFF0F is the only highlight colour: 1–4 keyword caps (6–10 % H) or a highlighter on the exact spoken words. One idea per caption.
4. Pace: median shot 2.2–4.2 s; archive/news montage bursts of 4–7 cuts per 10 s, then 8–20 s holds for graphics and experts. Cut on the sentence, not the beat.
5. Logo/series sting at 35–80 s, followed by 1–2 s of near-silence and a hard re-entry on the next shot.
6. Lower third = yellow rule + serif name + sans role, typed on, 4 s. Remote experts appear as honest webcam frames.
7. Credit every borrowed image in a quiet corner label (2.4 % H), keep other outlets' branding visible.
8. Keep the voice on top: bed ≥ 13 dB under the voice median; integrated −14…−20 LUFS (one science film sat at −27, too quiet to copy).

## Recipe: real footage + HTML/GSAP (Hyperframes)
Layer stack (bottom → top): footage/scan/graphic ground → grade layer (`filter: grayscale(1) contrast(.8) brightness(1.15)` for archive only) → evidence card (screenshot/scan) → highlight/outline SVG → keyword caption → lower third → corner credit + bug.

```css
:root{--vox-yellow:#FEFF0F;--paper:#FAFBF6;--grid:#DFE6DF;--red:#C2455A;--green:#257343;--navy:#213745}
.paper{background:var(--paper);background-image:linear-gradient(var(--grid) 1px,transparent 1px),linear-gradient(90deg,var(--grid) 1px,transparent 1px);background-size:1.6vh 1.6vh}
.kw{font:900 8.8vh/1.05 'Archivo Black',sans-serif;color:var(--vox-yellow);text-transform:uppercase;text-align:center}
.typed{font:900 6.3vh 'Archivo Black';color:#fff;text-transform:uppercase}
.lt{position:absolute;left:12.5vw;bottom:18vh;border-left:3px solid var(--vox-yellow);padding-left:1vw}
.lt .name{font:700 5.5vh 'Source Serif 4',serif;color:#fff}.lt .role{font:500 2.5vh Inter;color:#fff}
.credit{position:absolute;right:3vw;bottom:3vh;font:500 2.4vh Inter;color:#111}
.hl{background:#FFFF00;mix-blend-mode:multiply;transform-origin:left}
```
```js
// highlighter on spoken words
tl.fromTo('.hl',{scaleX:0},{scaleX:1,duration:0.9,ease:'power1.inOut'}, tWord);
// outline box around an object word
tl.fromTo('#box',{strokeDashoffset:len},{strokeDashoffset:0,duration:0.5,ease:'power2.inOut'}, t);
// typed chapter question with cursor
tl.from('.typed .ch',{opacity:0,duration:0.01,stagger:0.045}, t);
tl.to('.cursor',{opacity:0,repeat:-1,yoyo:true,duration:0.4}, t);
// lower third type-on, 4 s hold, hard off
tl.from('.lt .ch',{opacity:0,stagger:0.04,duration:0.01}, t).set('.lt',{autoAlpha:0}, t+4);
// object strip on graph paper, then land on the key object
tl.to('.strip',{x:'-62vw',duration:9,ease:'none'},0).to('.last',{scale:1.25,duration:1.2,ease:'power2.inOut'},9);
```
Timing template for a 150 s opening: 0–13 s object or "news proves it" cold open → host/narrator sets the question → 4–6 archive or news cuts with yellow keywords → logo sting ~3.5 s + 1–2 s silence + real sound → one 18–20 s animated mechanism graphic → expert (webcam, lower third) → primary document with highlighter → expert.

## Caveats
- Typefaces are guesses (Vox's own faces); Archivo Black / Inter / Source Serif 4 are the closest free stand-ins I chose by eye.
- I measured levels, not timbre: music style and SFX type are inferred from level jumps, not heard.
- Loudness differs between episodes (−14 to −27 LUFS); treat the bed-to-voice gap (13–15 dB) as the stable house rule, not the absolute level.
- Ambulance numbers exclude a 38 s sponsor read (72.9–111.2 s); raw numbers are in its metrics.json.
- Only the first 150 s of each film are analysed; later acts may cut differently.
