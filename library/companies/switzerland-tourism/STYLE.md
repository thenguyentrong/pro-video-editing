## Core idea
A comedy short with famous people, where the joke always turns into the product benefit. The country is never sold with a plain scenery montage: scenery is a film on a laptop, a film on a cinema screen, or the background of a shoot that goes wrong. The stars argue; the landscape wins. Each film ends on the same two-step promise: "When you need [benefit]" → "You need Switzerland." → logo.

## Rules with numbers
- **Length 90–130 s**; dialogue-led; cuts follow lines and reactions (cuts on onsets = chance, 37–49 %).
- **ASL 1.4–1.7 s** for the dialogue films; 3.1 s when the film uses long takes (9.8 s dolly, 15.4 s walk-and-talk oner).
- **Densest 10 s at 55–75 % of the runtime** (9–10 cuts / 10 s): the scenery or gag montage.
- **A cold open of 1.4–4.2 s** that is either the star alone or the "film-in-the-film".
- **End block 7.4–11.6 s** on one aerial plate (or an aerial montage with ≈1.2 s dissolves): tagline 1 (≈3 s), tagline 2 (≈2–3 s), logo (≈2.5 s). Optional 2.6 s post-logo button.
- **Two lighting worlds** for the stars (dark/cool vs golden/warm) or a dark cinema room vs a bright screen.
- **Type**: 2021–22 soft bold serif taglines (≈6 % H, white, centred, hard cuts); 2025 clean sans system with a red-cross wordmark, lower thirds (name 3.8 % / role 2.5 % H) and a 3 % H bug top-right.
- **Loudness** varies widely (−17.4 to −9.0 LUFS); use −14 to −16 for your own master.

## Recipe (a 90 s star tourism film)
1. Write the joke as a conflict about the product benefit (no drama, autumn too perfect, someone upstaged by the view).
2. 0–4 s: cold open on one star, still and dark, or on the scenery as "a film".
3. 4–55 s: dialogue at ASL ≈1.5 s; every 3rd–4th shot shows scenery inside the story (screen, window, background).
4. 55–70 s: one scenery or gag montage at ≈1.0–1.1 s per shot.
5. 70–80 s: the turn; let one shot run long (4–10 s).
6. Last 9 s: one aerial plate, tagline 1 at +0.7 s, tagline 2 at +3.7 s, logo at +6.7 s; add a 2–3 s button after the logo if there is a last laugh.
7. Grade two worlds; real landmarks only; dialogue on top, score under, master −14 LUFS.
