## In one sentence
Sony films are cinematic first and technical second: moody, filmic lifestyle (night bokeh, golden hour, teal shadows) or pure dark CGI carries the mood, and each technical claim gets one clean, disciplined card - a bold keyword or number with small tracked text.

## Sub-styles

### 1. Chaptered phone film (Xperia)
- ≈10 features in 190 s; each = cinematic human scene + real UI on the phone held in frame + one spec card on graphite ("Approx. 4x ..." with the number bold 7.5 % H, the rest 3.8 % H tracked).
- Calm narrator (≈85 words/min); 0.5-1.85 s music drops before features; ORIGINAL vs AI side-by-sides.

### 2. Brand concept film (Alpha)
- Two words as the whole idea ("Redefine basic", bold 6.7 % H, black on off-white) shown at start and end.
- Fast montage of young photographers (ASL ≈1.4 s, 0.2 s flashes) cut against bright white-studio CGI of sensor, processor and an exploded camera; AF tracking as thin white boxes and a pose skeleton.
- Sparse poetic VO, ambient score, cuts not on the beat.

### 3. Hardware reveal trailer (PlayStation)
- 20 s of dark grazing-light macros with small caps titles (7 % H) fading in one by one, a rainbow light reveal on a +29 dB hit, three CGI tech metaphors with titles inside the scene (8 % H caps), product on a rainbow light floor.
- 100 % CGI, music only, pace slows down to the end (2.3 → 4.0 s per quarter).

## Measured ranges
- Film ASL ≈1.4-3.6 s; Alpha and Xperia have fast bursts (12-13 cuts / 10 s) inside calmer films; PlayStation stays at 1-4 cuts / 10 s.
- Loudness −14.0 to −15.7 LUFS (moderate, never Samsung-loud). Xperia peaks at +0.5 dBTP.
- Drops before features (Xperia 0.55-1.85 s), silences around logos (Alpha 1.1 s and 2.15 s).

## Typography
- Sony SST (proprietary). Free stand-ins: **Noto Sans** / **Source Sans 3** (Xperia, Alpha). PlayStation uses heavy wide caps: **Montserrat ExtraBold** / **Archivo ExtraBold**.
- Pattern: one bold keyword or number + light/regular tracked text (Xperia "AI" 9.4 % H over "Camera Assistant" 3.8 % H).
- Legal micro-text ≈1.2-1.5 % H on claims.

## Colour
- Xperia: low-key cinema (mean luma 0.25) with warm practicals and magenta-cyan CGI.
- Alpha: teal-green cool lifestyle vs high-key off-white CGI.
- PlayStation: near-black (luma 0.19) → spectral rainbow → PlayStation blue.

## Camera
- Long lenses, shallow focus, golden hour and night bokeh; extreme close-ups (eyes, fingers on shutter); product macros under raking light; continuous CGI moves (sensor grows, pixels assemble).

## Transitions
- Mostly hard cuts; in-shot CGI morphs; one-frame white flashes and whips (Alpha); one white light flash (PlayStation); match cuts on round shapes (eye ↔ lens).

## Sound
- Narrator for phones, sparse poetry for cameras, music only for consoles. Moderate masters, drops before new chapters, hits +17-29 dB on reveals.

## Recipe
- Grade lifestyle like a camera brand (teal shadows, warm skin, bokeh), keep CGI clean and bright (Alpha) or dark and spectral (PlayStation).
- One spec card per feature: `number 7.5vh bold` + `line 3.8vh regular, letter-spacing .1em`, centred at 25 % H on graphite.
- Openers: one 12-15 s continuous move (Alpha) or 20 s of dark macros with three small caps titles (PlayStation).
- Mix ≈ −14 to −15.5 LUFS; true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
