# Warner Bros. trailer style (studio craft)

Three official Warner Bros. trailers, measured at 480p (cuts2 + eye-checked verify pairs, EBU R128 in 0.5 s bins, 50 ms RMS):

| Example | Content length | Shots | ASL / median | ASL by quarter | < 1 s / > 3 s | LUFS | Aspect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dune: Part Two, Trailer 3 (2023) | 159.5 s | 112 | 1.42 / 1.29 s | 1.38 · 1.48 · 1.42 · 1.29 | 30 % / 6 % | -14.5 | 1.90:1 |
| Dune: Part Three, Trailer (2026) | 167.7 s | 122 | 1.37 / 1.19 s | 1.82 · 1.16 · 1.27 · 1.27 | 36 % / 5 % | -14.3 | 2.20:1 |
| One Battle After Another, Trailer 2 (2025) | 141.7 s | 123 | 1.15 / 0.96 s | 1.48 · 0.98 · 0.93 · 1.27 | 52 % / 2 % | -14.8 | 1.86:1 |

Warner does not have one house look: each film gets its own type world (the director's brand). What is shared is the skeleton and the restraint.

## Shared skeleton (all three, % of content length)
| Beat | Dune 2 | Dune 3 | OBAA | Rule |

| Densest 10 s | 133-143 s (83-90 %) | 142-152 s (85-91 %) | 114-124 s (80-87 %) | peak cutting very late |
| Hard stop before title | 0.8 s black + 2.6 s lone wide | 0.8 s silence + face + hit on black | 5.9 s held wide, music dropped | one stop, 1.5-6 s |

| After the title | cast/billing 5 s | cast/billing 6.3 s | 7.4 s comic button + billing 2.75 s | end block 5-10 s |

Compared with our Marvel anchors (Spider-Man BND: ASL 1.14, quarters 1.07/1.10/1.02/1.33; Doomsday: ASL 2.20, quarters 3.25/1.38/1.49/5.11, densest at 50-65 % and slowing for the last 15-25 %): Warner keeps the ASL almost flat from start to end (quarter ratio max/min 1.15 for Dune 2, 1.57 for Dune 3, 1.59 for OBAA vs 3.7 for Doomsday), puts the densest cutting much later (80-91 %), and then stops once, hard. The breathing comes from held scale shots and black beats inside the body, not from a slow intro and a slow ending.

## Sub-styles

### A. Epic event trailer (Dune: Part Two, Dune: Part Three)
- Pace: ASL 1.37-1.42 s, median 1.19-1.29 s, 30-36 % of shots under 1 s, 5-6 % over 3 s. Opening 0-31 s slower (ASL 1.42-1.97), final act fastest (1.08-1.14).
- Scale shots: every 20-30 s one extreme wide with a tiny figure (subject < 10 % of frame height), held 2.5-3.7 s while everything around it cuts at ~1.2 s.
- Black beats: 5 (Dune 2) to 10 (Dune 3) dips to black of 0.4-1.7 s, each followed by a fade-up or a hit. They work as chapter breaks.
- Cards: 3 per trailer + title. Wide extended geometric caps (Dune face; closest free match a guess: Michroma), cap height only 2.5-2.9 % H (small line 1.9 %), tracking ~0.35 em, centred at 49-50 % H, 2-4 words. Card background is a branded "world", not black: Part Two = radial orange glow (#E2761E bottom centre to #0D0101 edges) with peach-gold text #FFB386/#FFD89F; Part Three = blurred teal-left/amber-right anamorphic haze with dust specks and a cyan flare streak, muted gold text #A8946E-#C2AC83. In: hard cut, letters brighten with a light sweep (0.2 s, Part Two) or resolve with a flicker (0.6-0.8 s, Part Three). Out: the next shot dissolves in UNDER the text over 0.6-0.9 s. Total on screen 1.3-2.4 s.
- Title: thin-stroke DUNE logo 6-10 % H with an anamorphic flare through the last letter, subtitle PART TWO/THREE 1.9-2.1 % H fades in 0.8 s later; hold 3.4-3.8 s. Cast block 2.3-2.5 % H; in Part Three the names add one line at a time.
- Colour: letterbox constant per trailer (1.90:1 or 2.20:1, no IMAX ratio jumps in these cuts). Desert haze with lifted blacks, low-key interiors, one or two "colour chapters" (Part Two: B&W infrared arena 70.8-75.1; Part Three: full-red frames 68.6 and 73.4, teal-blue interiors 85-91).
- Sound: -14.3/-14.5 LUFS integrated; a steady crescendo of +4.7..+5.2 LU from Q1 to Q4 (median momentary). Quiet dialogue openings (-20 dBFS in Part Two, -25..-27 dBFS in Part Three for the first 6 s). Hits of +16..+24 dB on cuts out of black and on the WB logo. Cuts barely follow audio onsets (11-24 % vs 14-16 % chance): the edit follows long score phrases. Two ways to land the title: on the decay of the last impact (Part Two, no hit, -22..-27 dBFS) or after a real silence (Part Three: 0.8 s at -72 dBFS, a face, then +38.6 dB to -8.7 dBFS on the cut to black, title fades up 0.1 s later).

### B. Auteur action-comedy trailer (One Battle After Another)
- Pace: faster (ASL 1.15, median 0.96, 52 % under 1 s, only 2 % over 3 s), but bookended by two long holds: a 6.4 s hook and a 5.9 s hold before the title.
- Cards: full-frame pure black with one heavy condensed face (chamfered corners, rough edges; closest free match a guess: Big Shoulders Display Black or Anton), cream #EAEECD, HUGE: 16.4-17.3 % H per line for 2-line cards, 22.4 % H for the title, centred (title left-aligned stair-step at ~12/21/32 % W). No animation: every card and every title word appears and leaves on a hard cut. Taglines 0.91-1.42 s; names 0.67-0.75 s; title 2.71 s; billing 2.75 s. 12 cards in total including 6 names.
- Cast run as a rhythm peak: card/shot/card/shot every 17 frames (0.709 s) for 8 s at 80-86 % of runtime.
- Title built word by word: ONE BATTLE / AFTER / ANOTHER at 0.71 s steps, then a 7.4 s comic button after the title.
- Colour: VistaVision 1.86:1, natural film grain, warm tungsten and neon interiors, sodium-teal nights, pastel desert; cards never coloured.
- Sound: -14.8 LUFS; Q medians -18.9 / -15.0 / -12.7 / -13.2. Eight short silences (0.15-0.7 s) used as comic timing, each followed by a big rise (+46 dB, +60 dB). The title takes the loudest 0.5 s of the trailer right after the music drops out (0.7 s at -44 dBFS) inside the held shot.

## Sound structure (summary)
- Integrated -14.3 to -14.8 LUFS (all three within 0.5 LU).
- Quiet start: the first 6-12 s peak 5-15 dB under the body (dialogue, room tone).
- Build by quarters: +4.7 to +6.2 LU from Q1 median to Q3/Q4 median.
- Hits around the logo: Dune 2 +23.6 dB exactly on the logo cut; Dune 3 +16 dB 1.9 s before it; OBAA no measured hit (flat logo on a hard cut after a 0.35 s gap at 27.35 s). Biggest hit either at the title (OBAA, Dune 3) or nowhere near it (Dune 2 lands the title on decay).
- Silence: epic trailers keep the bed continuous and use black-picture valleys (Dune 2 75-83 s down to -46 dBFS) or one true silence before the end (Dune 3, 0.8 s at -72 dBFS); the comedy trailer uses many short silences (8 gaps, 0.15-0.7 s) as joke timing.

## What makes it premium
- Restraint with text: 3-4 cards in the epics, tiny and wide-tracked; or, in the comedy, few words but huge and blunt. Never medium.
- Scale held: the widest images get 2.5-3.7 s while the body cuts at 1.1-1.4 s.
- Flat pace + rising score: the edit does not speed up; the music does (+5 LU), and the cutting peaks only in the last 10-20 %.
- One hard stop before the title (black, silence, or a held wide), never a gradual slowdown.
- A unique card world per film (glow, haze, black), consistent across every card, logo and end block.

## Recipe (real footage + Hyperframes/GSAP)

Common timeline for a 60-70 s cut (scaled from the measured % positions):
- 0-9 s cold open: one 4-5 s held shot under quiet dialogue/room tone (-25 dBFS), then 4-5 shots at ~1.5 s.
- ~9-14 s studio/brand logo on a hit (+20 dB), 1.2-1.6 s.
- 21-24 s "FROM ..." card; 37-40 s date card; 42-46 s tagline.
- Body ASL 1.1-1.4 s, flat. One extreme wide (2.5-3.5 s) every ~12 s; one black beat (0.5-0.9 s) every ~15 s (epic) or one 0.3-0.7 s silence before every joke/hit (comedy).
- Densest 6-8 s at 82-90 % (e.g. 53-59 s): ASL ~0.7 s or a card/shot alternation on the beat grid.
- Stop: 0.8 s black + silence, or a 4-6 s held wide with the music muted for 0.7 s.
- Title at 91-94 % (59-62 s): hit on the cut, title hold 2.7-3.8 s; end block or a 4-6 s button.
- Master -14 LUFS integrated (`loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=11`); bed automated to rise ~+5 LU from first to last quarter.

.glow{position:absolute;inset:0;background:radial-gradient(ellipse 80% 90% at 50% 100%,#E2761E 0%,#A23113 45%,#2C080A 80%,#0D0101 100%)}
.txt{position:absolute;top:50%;left:0;right:0;transform:translateY(-50%);text-align:center;font-family:Michroma;color:#FFC08F;text-shadow:0 0 12px rgba(255,170,110,.35)}

GSAP: letters `from {opacity:0}` with `stagger:{each:.03, from:"random"}, ease:"steps(3)"`, total 0.6 s; hold 1.0 s; the next shot's wrapper `opacity 0→1` over 0.8 s `ease:"none"` starting 0.4 s before the card ends (overlap so the text sits on the picture); text `opacity→0` in the last 0.3 s. Teal/amber variant: replace `.glow` with two radial gradients (teal rgba(40,120,120,.55) at 25 % 45 %, amber rgba(190,120,40,.55) at 80 % 75 %) over #0d1416, add 30-40 dust dots drifting `y:-8` and a 2 px cyan streak at the left edge.

Comedy card (sub-style B), Hyperframes: full-frame `#000` sections, Big Shoulders Display 900 or Anton, `#EAEECD`, `font-size:180px; line-height:1.0; letter-spacing:0; text-transform:uppercase; text-align:center`. No tweens; only `data-start`/`data-duration`. Names 0.709 s each alternating with 0.709 s shots (17 frames at 23.976 fps). Title: three `div`s at 242 px, `text-align:left`, `left:12% / 21% / 32%`, shown with `tl.set(el,{autoAlpha:1}, t + i*0.71)`.

Footage: real footage only (licensed stock or own shoot): for A, desert/landscape wides with tiny people, low-key faces, macro hands/sand; for B, actor close-ups, car-mount and dashboard shots, symmetrical interior wides. Grade A `filter: sepia(.18) saturate(.8) contrast(.95)`; grade B `contrast(1.05) saturate(1.05) sepia(.06)` + grain at .10.
