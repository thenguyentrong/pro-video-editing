What makes it premium
- Scale is shown, not told: 10+ extreme wides with tiny figures, each held 2.5-3.7 s while the rest cuts at ~1.3 s.
- Text is tiny (2.5-2.9 % H), wide-tracked and rare (3 cards + title), on a branded glow, and the film dissolves in under it.
- The edit does not race: flat ASL across quarters (1.29-1.48), so the music's crescendo (+4.7 LU Q1 to Q4) carries the build.
- One-colour cards (orange glow) and a black-and-white sequence give strong visual "chapters".
- The end is quiet: black, a lone rider, the title on the decay.
