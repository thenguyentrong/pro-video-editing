# Introducing Digit 5  (Agility Robotics, 2026-09-15, 1:10, 16:9, YouTube @AgilityRobotics)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oyq9BOwK5XI
Purpose: launch film (new hardware generation, brand-level)
One-sentence point: Digit 5 is Agility's first humanoid built to work safely next to people without cages, made at scale for real industry.

## Structure
- 0-12.7 people first (10 shots): city crowds, a woman on a platform, a manager's portrait to camera, floor plans, workers in hair nets. No robot yet.
- 12.7-26.6 industry montage (14 shots, many 0.25-0.6 s): machine parts, conveyors, a worker at a desk, a teal robot elbow macro, a factory hall, a quality worker smiling to camera.
- 26.6-29.5 reveal: Digit 5 rack-focus in a white studio (longest mid-film shot, 3.0 s), biggest loudness rise of the film here (+26.4 dB at 27.05 s).
- 29.5-49.7 product: Digit in a big hall (wide-angle low shot), CAD wireframe, screwdriver insert, robot head close, two Digits handling blue totes on a clean set, a code screen, hip/leg low angle, a running Digit with motion blur.
- 49.7-56.6 vision: a translucent blue "hologram" Digit composited into real places (warehouse, grocery store, office, data centre).
- 56.6-65.0 production: hands assembling, a chip drop, RoboFab-like line with people moving barriers next to Digit, face display close-up.
- 65.0-70.4 logo: grey symbol on light grey, then "Agility" wordmark on black (3.4 s).

## Typography
- No on-screen text until the logo. Wordmark "Agility" ≈12 % H (cap-height ≈10 %), light grey #D8D8D4 on black, centred; symbol shown alone first on light grey.

## Colour/grade
- Brand teal-green (#1BB89A-like) robot against cool grey/white studio and warm documentary interiors; mint-blue for the CAD and hologram graphics. Clean, high-end commercial grade.

## Camera/shots
- Documentary portraits looking into the lens, macro product shots with shallow focus, wide-angle low shots of Digit, whip pans, motion-blur running shot.
- Real vs CGI: the hologram Digit is CGI composited into real plates; the CAD view is a render; the very clean two-robot tote scene (≈38 s) looks rendered; the studio rack-focus shots look photographic. The film does not say which is which; the YouTube description says "Prototype. Features and design are subject to change."

## Transitions

## Sound
- Voice-over almost wall to wall (speech detected 0.8-65.5 s, ≈112 words, ≈1.7 words/s) on a driving electronic track. Integrated -14.4 LUFS, LRA 3.2 LU (very compressed), true peak -1.4 dBTP. No drop-outs. Cuts on onsets 35 % vs 37 % chance: cut to picture rhythm, not to the beat.

## What makes it premium / where it is weak
- People first: workers, managers and city life before the robot, so the robot answers a human need.
- One brand colour (teal) carried by the robot, the CAD and the hologram.
- Fast, flat 1.2 s rhythm with a clear reveal beat.
- Weak as proof: no task is shown longer than ≈3 s, no speed or autonomy label, real and CGI shots are not separated.

- 70-93 %: factory and people building it.
- Logo: symbol 1 s on light grey, wordmark 3 s on black, ≈12 % H.
