## Core idea
Hardware as a serious object, proof as dated events. The reveal film sells one machine like an industrial product (one colour of light, giant name, labels written like a spec sheet). The proof film sells a date and a count of days, and shows the human operator pressing the real button before the machine moves.

## House rules (measured)
- **Type = a technical grotesk** (closest free: Inter Tight / Inter), white on black or dark picture, no boxes. Three sizes only: giant title (≈13-35 % of frame height), labels ≈4.5 % caps, data text ≈1-2 % caps.
- **Spec-sheet layouts:** labels top-left with a numbered list; role words spread across the bottom edge; tiny metadata blocks in a 3-column grid under the title card. Text resolves with a character scramble (≈0.3-0.5 s) or word by word in two columns.
- **Two-column text cards** on black for context (date, customer, task), ≈4.5 % of picture height, words arriving one by one, held 4-5 s.
- **Picture:** reveal = near-black (mean luma 0.115) with one saturated key colour; proof = real, desaturated, filmic, 2.39:1 letterbox.
- **Sound:** a 0.5-0.9 s drop-out before the biggest reveal, then the loudest hit of the film (+29 to +41 dB rise). Loud masters (-10.7 to -13.4 LUFS, true peak above 0 dBTP).
- **Rhythm:** reveal ASL 2.8 s with 0.25 s light-streak bursts as section wipes; manifesto ASL 1.55 s cut on music onsets (47 % vs 29 % chance); doc ASL 1.73 s with the proof shot as the longest take (9.26 s take-off roll).

## How they make trust visible
- Real radio traffic as the cold open; the operator's screen and the touchscreen takeoff-confirm button before the take-off.
- Dates on screen (contract date, first-flight date) and a day count as the main claim.
- Test footage where the effect and the target share one wide frame.
- The team's reaction in a black-and-white epilogue after the logo.
- Weak spot: the reveal film never says whether it is render or photography, and shows no environment or operation.

## Recipe
1. Reveal: one dark set, one coloured key, 6-8 sections of 3-7 s; giant cropped title at the start, spec labels top-left, bottom role row; 0.5 s drop-out, hero hit, logo.
2. Proof: cold open with real control audio 8-25 s; two-column text cards on black; build montage 0.5-1 s per shot; operator + button 2-3 s; the proof take 8-10 s uncut; claim super with a number of days; logo; quiet epilogue.
3. Manifesto: 14-16 real clips of 1.2-1.8 s, a VO creed of 6-8 short lines at ≈2 words/s, title card with the slogan in the last 4 s.
