## In one line
Show the proof and where it came from: a real reporter at a real desk, white-box serif type, one annotation colour, every clip credited, long holds on the evidence, and silence instead of music when it matters.

## Sub-styles
1. **Visual Investigations (VI)** — reporter-led method film. The reporter sits at his NYT desk or in the newsroom; satellite screen recordings (cursor visible), white boxes and outlines, comparison montages, official/UGC/CCTV clips with credits, court documents with yellow highlighter. Slow: ASL ~5 s, median ~3 s, 0 % of shots under 1 s, proof held 14–28 s.
2. **Opinion video essay** — art-directed argument. A physical metaphor (3D goggles = public data), glitch/RGB-split for "bad", warm stock for "good", archive collage, typed dot-matrix text, real government web pages as evidence, a persistent "T | OPINION" bug, title card late (82 s). Faster: ASL 2.7 s, 31 % of shots under 1 s.
3. **Graphics-desk vertical Short** — a named graphics reporter on a white seamless set + native 9:16 charts that explain their own encoding; captions on every phrase; loud social mix (-14 LUFS).

## How they show evidence
- **Satellite**: screen recording of the reporter's tool (cursor visible) or full-frame stills; slow zoom to the target, white square or white polygon outline (2 px, soft shadow), label in white; pull back to show context.
- **Comparison montage**: the same white box + serif place label on 3–4 places, date top-left, imagery credit bottom-left, ~2–2.5 s each.
- **Human cost**: victims' photos as a grid over the darkened (~40 %) satellite map.
- **Third-party footage**: scaled to ~85 % of frame height on pure black, credit bottom-left on every clip ("U.S. Central Command, via DVIDS", "IRIB TV via Agence France-Presse"); corporate promo material gets "PROMOTIONAL FOOTAGE" top-centre.
- **Multi-source sync**: CCTV + phone videos in a grid; one panel scales up to full frame for the key moment (~0.75 s move).
- **Documents**: the reporter handles the printed filing at his desk, then the scanned pages full-frame on black with a flat yellow highlighter bar on the exact sentence; the rest blurred.
- **Maps**: NYT basemap (pale cream/sage), one fill (park green), two line colours (crimson road, blue barriers), orange diamond markers with black leaders, direct labels, locator pull-out; "Source: … / Graphic: The New York Times" bottom-left.
- **Opinion**: real government pages ("Page not found", Federal Register notice) and agency logos, glitched; forms double-exposed over photos.
- **Short**: dot-range chart built for vertical; zoom to one row, arrows explain "Earnings range" and "Median earnings", then show all rows; dim others to ~25 %; source note always under the chart.

## Captions & lower thirds
- Lower third = white rectangle, name in bold serif (Cheltenham-like) ~3 % H, role in tracked caps sans ~1.4–1.7 % H ("VISUAL INVESTIGATIONS REPORTER", "NEW YORK TIMES GRAPHICS REPORTER"). Placed in the empty space next to the subject (left at x 6 %, or right of the head), held ~4–5 s, simple fade.
- Titles = white boxes, one per line, bold serif ~3.5 % H (Short) to ~5.4 % H (VI), with a small caps label box above ("VISUAL INVESTIGATIONS"); in the Short the title types on letter by letter.
- 16:9 YouTube pieces carry no burned-in subtitles. The Short captions every phrase: white sans 600 ~2.1 % H on a tight ~45 % black box at y ≈ 77 %, 3–6 words, hard swaps.
- Opinion: persistent bug top-left "T | OPINION" (~2.5 % H serif caps).

## Graphics restraint & colour
- One annotation colour on footage: white. Yellow only as a document highlighter.
- Maps/charts: 1 fill + 1–2 line colours + 1 marker colour; everything else greys. Short: mint bar (~#A8D5C6) + slate dot (~#5B7A8C) only.
- Footage is not stylised (VI): neutral office light, natural satellite. The Opinion desk is the exception: deliberate RGB split, scan lines, iridescent 3D.
- No logo stings except: Opinion title card (82 s), NYT wordmark on the Short's last second.

## Voice & music mix
- VI is voice-led and quiet: -17 to -20 LUFS integrated. The Iran piece drops to true silence between phrases (32 gaps, down to -71 dBFS; 1.95 s of silence after the victims' photos, 1.8 s before the explosion clip) and lets source sound peak (+52 dB rocket launch). Big Bend keeps a very quiet bed (-40…-48 dBFS in pauses).
- Opinion keeps a continuous designed bed ~15–20 LU under the voice, glitch SFX hits +23…+34 dB, and pulls out for 3 s before the title card.
- Short: -14.2 LUFS, flat (median -13.3 in every quarter), continuous bed, no hits.

## Rules
1. Credit every external visual on screen: ~1.5 % H white text bottom-left (x 3 %, y 96 %); add "PROMOTIONAL FOOTAGE" (bold caps ~3 % H, top-centre) on corporate/military promo material; maps and charts carry "Source: … / Graphic: …" at ~1.3 % H.
2. Annotate with white only: 2 px box/outline (+ soft shadow), serif label ~3.3 % H under it; reuse the identical box for every comparison (~2.2 s per place).
3. Show the method: real desk or newsroom, 3 angles (wide, close, OTS on screens), cursor-visible screen recordings, finger on the monitor, paper in hand.
4. Hold proof long: evidence/graphic shots 14–28 s; overall VI ASL ~5 s, median ~3 s, no shot under 1 s.
5. Type system = white boxes + bold serif + tracked caps sans; titles one box per line (~5.4 % H), lower thirds ~3 % H name / ~1.5 % H role, placed in the subject's open space.
6. Maps: pale basemap, 1 fill + ≤ 2 line colours + orange diamond markers with leaders, direct labels, locator pull-out; yellow highlighter only for document sentences.
7. Let silence work in investigations (gaps to < -60 dBFS around the hardest images), keep the integrated level around -17…-20 LUFS; Shorts go to -14 LUFS with a constant bed.
8. Vertical explainers: native 9:16 charts, explain the encoding first (arrow + 2-word label), then compare 2 named examples with exact numbers; captions on every phrase (2.1 % H, 45 % black box, y 77 %).

## Recipe: real footage + HTML/GSAP (Hyperframes)
- **Layer stack (16:9 VI)**: footage/satellite (full-bleed) → optional dim `#000` at 40 % → annotation SVG (`stroke:#fff; stroke-width:.19vh; filter:drop-shadow(0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.6))`) → labels → credit line → (title/lower-third boxes).
- **Satellite zoom**: still `scale 1 → 2.2` over 10 s `power1.inOut`; target box `from {scale:3, opacity:0}` 0.8 s `power2.out`; pull back `scale 2.2 → 1` 4 s.
- **Comparison card**: box 34 % W x 50 % H centred; label `400 3.3vh "Source Serif 4"` white under it; date `400 2.7vh "Libre Franklin"` top 4 % left 3 %; credit `400 1.5vh` bottom 4 % left 3 %; hard cut every 2.2 s.
- **Title (white boxes)**: `.line{display:inline-block;background:#fff;padding:.15em .35em;font:700 5.4vh/1.15 "Source Serif 4";color:#111}`; label `600 2.5vh "Libre Franklin"; letter-spacing:.08em; uppercase`; reveal each line `clipPath: inset(0 100% 0 0) → inset(0 0 0 0)` 0.4 s `power2.out`, stagger 0.12 s; hold 5 s.
- **Lower third**: `background:#fff; padding:1.2vh 1.6vh`; name `700 4vh "Source Serif 4" #222`; role `500 1.6vh "Libre Franklin"; letter-spacing:.08em; uppercase`; fade 0.3 s; hold 4–5 s.
- **External clip**: `height:85%` centred on `#000`; credit always on; promo tag top-centre `700 3vh`.
- **Map**: basemap image; polygon `#8CC063`; route `stroke:#A3122E; stroke-width:.45vh` drawn via `strokeDashoffset` 3–4 s `power1.inOut`; barrier `#3D5BA9`; markers rotated squares `#F05A23` (1.4 % H) + 1 px black leader `scaleY 0 → 1` 0.3 s; labels `700 2.8vh`.
- **Document**: page on `#000` at 95 % W; scroll `y` 0.8 s `power2.inOut`; highlight `background:#FFE600` `scaleX 0 → 1, transformOrigin:left` 0.6 s; blur the rest with a masked `filter:blur(2px)` copy.
- **Opinion glitch** (only for opinion pieces): RGB-split copies offset ±1.2 % with `steps(1)` jitter for 0.3–0.5 s, scan-line overlay `repeating-linear-gradient(0deg, rgba(0,0,0,.25) 0 1px, transparent 1px 3px)`.
- **Short (1080 x 1920)**: captions `600 40px "Libre Franklin"; background:rgba(0,0,0,.45); top:77%`; chart bg `#F2F2F2`, bar `#A8D5C6`, dot `#5B7A8C` (3 px white stroke); zoom chart `scale 1 → 2.2` 1.2 s `power2.inOut`; arrow path draw 0.5 s; dim others `opacity:.25` 0.4 s.
- **Mix**: VI voice -16…-18 LUFS short-term, no bed under the hardest evidence, source SFX ~6 LU over voice; Short voice -13…-14 LUFS with bed ~-30 LUFS, master -14 LUFS.

## Caveats
- Fonts are identified by eye from 480p frames (Cheltenham/Franklin assumed; free stand-ins suggested). Hex colours are sampled by eye, ±10 %.
- Music presence is inferred from loudness floors in pauses (no source separation); "no bed" means pauses fall below -50…-70 dBFS.
- Only the first 150 s of each 16:9 piece was analysed; later chapters may use other devices.
- Animated graphics (zooms, scale-ups, highlight moves) are counted as one shot; the visual change rate inside them is higher than the ASL suggests.
