## In one sentence
Meta shows what the wearer sees: the person stays in their real place (a sunny room, a sofa, a city street) and the product's view appears life-size in that place or through the lens - with tiny, calm type and no spec cards.

## Sub-styles

### 1. Human demo documentary (Be My Eyes)
- A real person, three small real tasks, each shown fully (problem → wake phrase → a volunteer answers → done), then an emotional payoff that is not about the product (salsa night).
- The glasses' view appears as a 9:16 phone inset (≈25 % W) on a blurred copy of the POV.
- No text until the end; interview voice + real dialogue; −14.8 LUFS.

### 2. Warm product announcement (Meta VR Glasses)
- Apple-like dark warm macros bookend four everyday uses (sofa cinema, cafe, airport, plane); the virtual screen is composited life-size into the real room.
- Pace curve slow-fast-slow (4.3 / 2.4 / 2.0 / 4.3 s per quarter), no shot under 1.2 s.
- Tiny tracked caps (1.9-2.1 % H); end card "SEE HOW IT FEELS" 4.6 % H on a bronze gradient. Music only, −16.7 LUFS.

### 3. Statement teaser (Meta Ray-Ban Display)
- Five short sentence-case statements (cap ≈4.2 % H, no full stop) on black or over the product; through-the-lens POV with a dark round lens rim; one man in a city.
- Slow first quarter (4.7 s), then 0.3-1 s montage; two 1-1.4 s black beats as act breaks. Music only, −14.0 LUFS.

## Measured ranges
- ASL 1.26-3.19 s; median 0.83-2.46 s; the documentary and announcement never cut faster than 1 s (0-2 % short shots).
- Loudness −14.0 to −16.7 LUFS (Google/Apple-like, not Samsung-loud).
- Text: 1.9-4.6 % H, always calm; no numbers on screen.

## Typography
- Optimistic (proprietary). Free stand-in: **Figtree** or **Inter** (Medium for statements, Regular for small lines).
- Statements: sentence case, no full stop, centred, soft blur-fade in ≈0.5 s.
- Labels: tracked caps (+0.15-0.25 em), 1.9-2.1 % H, beside the subject.
- End: "A Community Voices story" style grey tracked line ≈4.4 % H on white + logo, or a two-line tracked caps card on brand gradient.

## Colour
- One warm world per film: daylight cream and green (documentary), bronze-brown low key (VR), cool city + hot accents → sunset (teaser).
- Product renders end on soft gradients (bronze, sky blue-to-peach) or white.

## Camera
- People first: long lenses, shallow focus, handheld or slow slides. Through-the-lens POV and life-size composites instead of UI screenshots. Product macros only to open and close.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts, slow fades, black beats; flash only once (light burst into the lens). No whips or graphic wipes.

## Sound
- Music-only for product films; real voices (interview + live dialogue) for human stories; source music (a record, a salsa band) can become the soundtrack.
- −14 to −17 LUFS; silence at the very start (3.3 s) or on the logo.

## Recipe
- Pick one person and 3-4 everyday places. For each: wide → hands → face → what the product shows (life-size composite, lens POV with a dark circular rim, or a 9:16 phone inset).
- Type: Figtree/Inter, statements 4.2 % H, labels 2 % H tracked caps, end card 4.6 % H.
- Pace: 2.5-3.2 s average for stories, 1.2 s for teasers with a slow first 10 s; black beats of 1-1.4 s between acts.
- Mix at ≈ −15 LUFS; keep real voices and room sound.
