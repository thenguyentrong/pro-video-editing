# Chipotle – social style (YouTube Shorts / TikTok, 2025-2026)

Examples: `red-chimichurri-process` (58.3 s employee-creator explainer, 74 k on YT), `drooling-6s` (6.1 s promo cutdown). Both 9:16, analysed Oct 2026. Chipotle's own TikTok is bigger; these are the YouTube cross-posts.

## The style in one line
Food content that looks like a fan or an employee made it on a phone: real hands, real sun, captions in TikTok's own styles, and the sauce as the hero.

## Hook
- A face + the product in 2 s (chef holding the sauce, then a macro drip), or the food action itself with the offer already on screen.

## Rules to copy
1. Let staff be creators (name + job in the first sentence).
2. Food macro of the hero ingredient within 2 s.
3. Cutdowns: one repeated action, one static offer line, cut on the bar.
4. Promo codes and legal in the post caption, never in the video.
5. End on a question to the viewer or mid-action (loop).

## What makes a 6 s cutdown work (seen here and in Target `no-tricks-40-off-6s`)
- 3-4 beats of 1.5-2 s, each beat one idea; the offer is either on screen the whole time (Chipotle) or arrives as the last beat (Target).
- Readable on mute in under 1 s: huge type (Target, 7-15 % H) or a short static card (Chipotle, 6 words).
- The brand device is the transition or the background (Target bullseye rings), or the product is (sauce).
- Loud music master for sound-on placements (-6.5 LUFS) or quiet for muted autoplay (-24 LUFS); never VO in 6 s.
