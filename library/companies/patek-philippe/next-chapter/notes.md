# The next chapter of the Generations campaign  (Patek Philippe, 2019-11-05, 0:37, 16:9, YouTube @PatekPhilippeGeneva)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OXJcOdn9Jow
Purpose: luxury social/TV spot (short Generations chapter) with product end
One-sentence point: A father and his sons share small daily moments; the watch is part of the family.

## Structure
- 0-2.5 opens with the logo: cross + "PATEK PHILIPPE GENEVE" + "BEGIN YOUR OWN TRADITION" over a B&W sky and a stone cornice.
- 2.5-27.3 B&W story in one place (a villa with a courtyard): a boy runs into his father's arms, hides under his coat in the rain, roses, father and two boys run down a hall, a toy car on the floor, the father's hand with the watch on the boy's shoulder, a doorway, the boy looks up.
- 27.28-37 colour product card (9.8 s): the gold chronograph on a pale grey table; the campaign line in tracked caps at the left (28-32 s), then the logo + claim at the left (33-37 s).
