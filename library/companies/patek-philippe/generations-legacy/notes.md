# Patek Philippe "Generations" film: the legacy  (Patek Philippe, 2015-02-12, 1:56, 16:9, YouTube @PatekPhilippeGeneva)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_y0y_XeS0fM
Purpose: luxury heritage brand film (long version of the "Generations" campaign), no voice
One-sentence point: A watch is passed from parent to child in five cities; the owner only keeps it for the next generation.

## Structure (five vignettes, all black-and-white)
- 0-17 **Geneva**: a lakeside garden (4.3 s opening wide), a boy running on a lawn, father and son on a jetty, a hug.
- 17-36 **Shanghai**: a bright office with skyline, father and son at the window, a hand on the son's shoulder with the watch on the wrist.
- 36-59 **Tokyo**: a Japanese garden and house, mother and daughter; the daughter receives the watch (48-49 s), the mother is moved.
- 59-79 **New York**: a woman at a night window, a child's bare feet on a wooden floor, a family dinner, mother and daughter hug.
- 79-96.5 **London**: father, son in school uniform, the front door, a walk to a black taxi, father and son holding hands with the watch in frame (92-93 s).
- 96.5-115.7 **end card (19.2 s)**: silver horizontal gradient. The campaign line fades in as two serif lines (line 1 ≈97 s, line 2 ≈101 s), then dissolves (≈106.5 s) to the Calatrava cross logo + "PATEK PHILIPPE GENEVE" + "Begin your own tradition.", held ≈9 s.

## Cut rhythm
- 45 shots in 115.7 s (the fade to the end card at 96.5 s was verified by eye and added). Film part 0-96.5 s: 44 shots, ASL ≈2.19 s.
- Whole film: ASL 2.57 s, median 2.0 s, only 7 % under 1 s, 27 % over 3 s, 23 shots per minute.
- ASL per quarter 2.41 / 2.07 / 1.70 / 5.79 s (the end card is the last quarter).
- Cuts per 10 s: 3, 5, 4, 3, 6, 4, 4, 6, 6, 2, 0, 0. No acceleration: the pace is even and slow.
- Longest 19.2 s (end card), 4.32 s (opening), 4.16 s (74.4). Shortest 0.76 s.

## Typography
- Book serif (Patek uses a Garamond-style face; closest free: EB Garamond), sentence case, dark grey on silver, cap height ≈3.3 % H, two lines centred at y ≈47-53 %. Fades only.
- Logo card: Calatrava cross + "PATEK PHILIPPE" in a narrow sans caps ≈3 % H, "GENEVE" smaller, then the claim in serif ≈2 % H.

## Colour/grade
- Full black-and-white, soft and bright: lifted whites, open shadows, gentle contrast, no grain; looks like a photo book. The end card is a silver gradient (light centre band, darker top and bottom).

## Camera/shots
- Lifestyle photography in motion: wides of beautiful homes, medium two-shots of parent and child, a few close-ups of the watch on a wrist (always worn, never presented). Slow dolly or locked; real-time, almost no slow motion.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts in the vignettes; fade to the end card; fades and a cross-dissolve on the card.

## Sound
- No voice. A piano/strings score, continuous. Integrated -12.6 LUFS, LRA 9.4 LU, true peak +0.1 dBTP. No drop-outs; small rises (+17 to +21 dB) at 85-89 s in the last vignette.

## What makes it premium
- Restraint: B&W, slow cuts (median 2 s), no voice, no product shot in the film.
- The watch is a detail inside a human moment.
- The end card is long (19 s) and the line does all the selling.

## Recipe
- 4-5 vignettes of 15-20 s, each with 6-9 shots of 1.5-4 s; one wrist close-up per vignette.
- Grade: `filter: grayscale(1) brightness(1.08) contrast(.9)`.
- End card: `background: linear-gradient(#8c8c8c, #e9e9e9 45%, #e9e9e9 55%, #8c8c8c)`; serif lines `font: 400 3.3vh 'EB Garamond'; color:#333`; line 1 fade 1 s, line 2 4 s later, hold 5 s, cross-dissolve 1 s to the logo, hold 8-9 s.
