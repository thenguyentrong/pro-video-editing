## Core idea
An animation trailer sells **characters and jokes**, not a world or a threat. Pixar opens on a gag or a scene, lets dialogue carry the cut (speech in 81–94 % of the runtime), uses the studio's own past ("from the studio that brought you …") as the credibility card, and keeps the frame bright and full (no letterbox in the main trailer). Silence and drop-outs, the main tools of live-action trailers, are almost absent: the only real quiet is a story beat (a night scene, a held face). The teaser trusts a single scene: one 24.5 s locked take.

| Silence | none | one 0.4 s drop-out in the fight | none | no pre-title silence |

| After the title | date only | date + 3.6 s black | call to action | short |

- **Cut to the talk**: cuts on onsets at or below chance in the long cuts (TS5 36 % vs 39 %), but every slapstick cut lands on a hit (Gatto fight 58 % vs 29 %).
- **Loudness**: both channel uploads at −14.1 LUFS, LRA 6.3–6.9 LU (an even, dialogue-led mix); the regional spot upload sits at −21.3 LUFS and is not a reference for level.
- **Picture**: saturated, bright, mostly high-key; dark scenes are lit by one warm or coloured practical (a bulb, a screen).

## Sub-styles
**A. Animation main trailer (Toy Story 5).** Comic open → conflict (the new rival) → logos at 18 % → comedy acts at ASL 1.2–1.6 s → a quiet night beat with a 5.3 s close-up → date at 53 % → action → tagline split over three cards → 6.6 s comic button → title at 96 %.

**B. Scene teaser (Gatto).** One establishing shot with the studio claim, one 24.5 s locked dialogue scene, 10 s of slapstick at 1.0–1.4 s per shot with 0.25 s black flashes, title with a character gag, date. ASL 4.5 s, the slowest piece in the trailer set.

**C. Animation TV spot (Toy Story 5 "Showdown Event").** ASL 0.77 s, 82 % of shots under 1 s, a tagline built word by word on three black cards between bursts of 4–6 shots, the villain held 2.6 s, title with character cut-outs, call to action.

## Recipe
1. Open on a joke or a full scene; no threat, no silence.
2. Studio credit early: the studio's past hits, or logos at ≈18 %.
3. Body ASL 1.2–1.6 s, cut on dialogue; give jokes 2–3 s holds.
4. One quiet, held character beat near the middle.
5. Cards: humanist or condensed bold caps, white on black, two-size stack, 0.7–2.0 s; split the tagline over three cards.
6. Title late (76–96 %), animated with a character if possible; master −14 LUFS, LRA ≈6–7 LU.
