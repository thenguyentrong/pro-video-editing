## Core idea
Warm, human proof of autonomy. The machine is shown doing the whole job in the real world (clinic, store, front yard) with ordinary people around it. Numbers are few and big, and each is tied to real footage. Abstract graphics (globe, moon) are used only to make a number tangible, then the film returns to real life.

## House rules (measured)
- **Type:** one big number (≈20 % H, grotesk 700) or 2-3 number supers (≈5 % H, centred, warm gold or white). Condensed bold for titles and comparison lines (closest free: Barlow Condensed 600-700). Real process footage carries no text.
- **Rhythm:** ASL 2.1-3.0 s, but built as long-short-long: a long held proof shot (6.6 s delivery; 17 s drop) inside faster montages (1-1.5 s shots).
- **Process order:** phone -> store -> dock -> load -> take-off -> flight -> delivery -> return; the delivery is the longest take.
- **Picture:** bright, warm, saturated daylight; white hardware with brand red; end cards on white or brand red.
- **Sound:** VO only in the milestone film (≈1.9 words/s); otherwise music. Music pulled to silence inside the held proof shot (≈1-2.5 s) and brought back with a hit. Masters -13.7 to -20.0 LUFS.

## How they make trust visible
- Complete real jobs, unbroken where it matters (the drop and the delivery in one take).
- Real app UI and real staff (high-vis vests, store, clinic) as part of the chain.
- Customer phone videos in vertical frames: the public as witnesses.
- Safety stated as a distance flown, then translated into comparisons.

## Recipe
1. Process demo: one job in 8 steps, 3-7 s each, title only (Barlow Condensed 700 7vh, right half), cut on every second music hit (≈2.5 s), solid brand-colour end card.
2. Milestone: giant number with rolling digits over a fast POV, two 5-6 s comparison graphics, 12-15 real impact shots of 1-2 s, photo-card collage into the logo.
3. Montage: two 10 s montages around one 15-17 s uncut proof shot with a music drop; three number supers max.
