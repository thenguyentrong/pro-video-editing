## Sub-styles
1. **Robot / product launch (Jaibot):** brand-voice VO, no on-screen text, 6-7 cuts per 10 s on music onsets, problem shot before product, 2.5 s black + 1.1 s silence, reveal on the hit, product name filmed as lettering on the machine.
2. **Platform launch (Nuron):** no VO, one claim pattern ("PERFORMANCE / OF CORDED", "... WITHOUT COMPROMISE") as white bold caps that stay over 2-6 fast site shots; one CGI block for the range; designed data graphics; red chevron wipes; hashtag end card.
3. **Employer film (Life at HQ):** VO over drone geography, lifestyle and campus, staff in red; no supers; red line wipe to logo.

## House rules
- **Brand red in every frame:** tool, case, helmet, robot, tablet frame, staff jackets. Grade stays neutral so the red pops.
- **Real PPE on every worker** (hi-vis, helmet, gloves, glasses) - the films look like safe, real sites.
- **Hands and dust close-ups:** gloved hands slamming batteries, drill bits in concrete, sparks; low angles, shallow focus.
- **Logo end:** thin red diagonal line or chevron wipe to white, red Hilti box centred ≈9 % of width, hold 2-3 s.
- **Loudness:** music-only launch ≈-13 LUFS; VO films ≈-19 LUFS.

## Typography system
- Claim type (Nuron): neo-grotesk bold caps, white, no box, left-aligned at x ≈5 %; keyword ≈12-13 % H, qualifier ≈8-9 % or ≈4 %; blur/slide in 0.3-0.4 s; holds 3-9 s over cutting footage. Closest free: Archivo Bold / Inter Bold.
- Data icons: white line pictograms ≈8 % H + labels ≈5 % sentence case, right side, popped in one by one.
- Hashtag end: stacked caps ≈13 % per line on a red chevron band.
- Robot launch and recruiting films: no added text at all.

## How they show the machine / site
- BIM link: monitor close-up of the 3D model -> the robot's own tablet with the floor plan -> the robot drilling. Story = data to robot.
- Pain shot first (a worker drilling overhead in dust), then the robot doing the same task.
- Weak spot: no complete work cycle, no speed or autonomy label, no customer name, no numbers. The launch films sell vision, not proof.

## Lessons for a construction-tech startup
- Copy the reveal device: pain shot -> 2.5 s black with 1 s silence -> machine on the hit.
- Copy the claim system: one repeated sentence pattern, keyword big, qualifier small, type held over 2-6 site close-ups.
- Do better than Hilti on proof: add one full real-speed cycle (8-15 s), one number card and a named site.
