## In one line
Cinematic business documentary: 2-second b-roll on a continuous score, clean boxless interviews, Bloomberg's own newsroom (TV ticker, headline cards) as evidence, and data drawn onto real places rather than slides.

## Sub-styles
1. **Originals explainer (Germany)** — neutral cool b-roll from factories and cities, one interview set (an empty concrete warehouse) for every speaker, Bloomberg TV clip with live "INTRADAY" box and red ticker, Bloomberg News headline cards with byline/date/QR, charts composited onto aerial ground and a steel container, a chapter title with an audio breath.
2. **Tech-thriller explainer (India)** — dark teal/black grade, macro screens, data-centre dollies, abstract renders, a 10 x 3-frame stutter montage, a document "focus band" on the regulator's number, staged metaphor (empty chair under a spotlight).
3. **Bloomberg Investigates (Myanmar)** — first-person field film led by a named senior editor (self-filmed in second 1), observational handheld, UGC and other broadcasters' chyrons kept raw, subtitles for local voices, dark slate 3D maps with crimson areas, series ident at 68 s and a silver episode title at 95 s.

## How they show evidence
- **Own newsroom first**: a Bloomberg TV clip with its live market box ("INTRADAY Volkswagen 71.28 ▼1.66 2.28 %") and ticker headline; Bloomberg News headline cards (wordmark, 3-line bold headline, byline | date, QR "Scan to read the full article") over darkened footage.
- **Charts in the world**: bars and lines composited flat onto real surfaces (top-down truck yard, weathered steel container) with title + "Source: …" line; the line draws while the time axis scrolls (25 s in one shot); event labels in italic ("Labor Market Reforms", "Global Financial Crisis").
- **Document focus band**: regulator text on black, all blurred/darkened except one band; the key phrase ("combined $33 billion over the four years") in a light box; "Source: Securities and Exchange Board of India" top-left.
- **Maps (Investigates)**: dark slate-textured 3D country shape, controlled areas in deep crimson, one italic label with leader line + dotted underline.
- **Third-party and UGC**: shown full-frame with the source's own branding (WION chyrons, KineMaster watermark, militia insignia) — not re-dressed.
- **Archive**: old film inset with rounded corners on black (Deutsche Mark), archive trading-pit film.
- **Metaphor shots**: an empty chair under a top light in front of servers, a stutter montage of phones = "millions of traders".

## Captions & lower thirds
- Lower thirds: no box, white type. Originals: name bold ~3.5–4.2 % H, role regular ~3 % H; revealed by a red/orange-to-white gradient sweep (~0.6 s), surname or affiliation briefly in brand orange-red; placed on the subject's open side (left x 10 % or right-aligned x 90 %), y 72–84 % H, held 3–4 s. Investigates: name in bold CAPS ~4.5 % H, role ~3.5 % H, simple fade.
- Subtitles (foreign speech only): white 500 ~4 % H, centred, ≤ 2 lines, y 80–90 % H, shadow, no box.
- Chapter titles: white bold/semibold sans ~5–6.5 % H, centred over a wide landscape/aerial, fade in/out ~0.5 s, held 3–7 s.
- Persistent bug: white Bloomberg "B" icon top-right (x 94 %, y 9 %) on every frame.

## Graphics restraint & colour
- One accent family: Bloomberg yellow (~#E8C33F) bars, crimson (~#D0263A / ~#8E1B2C on dark maps) lines and areas, orange/yellow event dots; everything else white, grey or the surface itself.
- Grades are naturalistic and slightly desaturated; India pushes teal/black for "machines" and keeps interviews warm; no letterbox, no grain, no added colour boxes on footage.
- Graphics appear 2–3 times per 150 s, each held long (13–25 s), never as rapid slides.

## Voice & music mix
- A score is always on: none of the three has any gap below -50 dBFS; pauses only fall to -28…-44 LUFS.
- Integrated -16.6 to -19.6 LUFS; flat by quarter (spread ≤ 1.5 LU).
- Structure through dips, not hits: 0.5–2 s dips before new sections, then a +15…+28 dB rise; one big breath at a chapter title (Germany: 7.5 s at -44 LUFS).
- Swell into branding (Myanmar loudest moment -12.5 LUFS right before the series ident).
- Cutting is on picture and story, not on beats (onset-sync at or below chance in all three).

## Rules
1. Keep the shot length at ~2 s median (ASL 2.2–2.6 s); use sub-second shots only as a device (cold-open flashes of 6 frames, a 10 x 3-frame stutter montage), and hold charts/metaphors 8–25 s.
2. Use your own newsroom as evidence: a TV clip with its live ticker, and headline cards = wordmark (~4 % H) + 3-line bold headline (~7 % H) + byline | date (~3 % H) + QR, over footage darkened ~50 %.
3. Put charts on real surfaces (aerial ground, walls, containers), draw lines over 20–25 s while the axis scrolls, always show a title (~3.5 % H) and "Source: …" (~1.5 % H).
4. One accent family (yellow #E8C33F / crimson #D0263A / orange dots) on neutral footage; no coloured boxes behind text.
5. Lower thirds without boxes, white, name bold ~4 % H + role ~3 % H, placed in the subject's open space; brand colour only as a 0.6 s gradient reveal.
6. Show the key number from a document with a focus band: blur 3 px + darken to ~35 % everywhere except one line, box the phrase, source label top-left.
7. Keep a continuous score (nothing below -50 dBFS), integrated -17 ± 2 LUFS; mark sections with 0.5–2 s dips and give chapter titles a 5–7 s breath.
8. Investigations: first-person reporter on screen in the first 2 s, raw UGC/third-party branding left visible, subtitles 4 % H without box, series ident after the human-cost beat (~70 s), episode title ~95 s.

## Recipe: real footage + HTML/GSAP (Hyperframes)
- **Layer stack**: footage → (for cards) `filter: blur(2px) brightness(.5)` copy → chart SVG or text → white "B"-style bug top-right (`top:6%; right:4%; height:5%`).
- **Headline card**: centred column; wordmark `700 4vh "Inter"`; headline `700 7vh/1.05 "Inter"`, max 3 lines, white, reveal with a colour wipe (`background:linear-gradient(90deg,#fff 50%,#F5A623 50%); background-size:200%; -webkit-background-clip:text; color:transparent`, `backgroundPosition 100% → 0%` 0.6 s `power2.out`); byline `300 3vh #bbb`; QR 12 % H at top 6 % left 5 % + `1.5vh` caption. Hold 2.5–3 s.
- **TV-ticker evidence**: insert the broadcast clip full-frame, no extra graphics; 2–2.5 s.
- **Chart on a surface**: static top-down or wall plate; SVG with `mix-blend-mode:multiply` (dark ink) or normal (white ink) + 4 % noise; title `700 3.5vh`, source `400 1.5vh`; bars `#E8C33F` `scaleY 0 → 1, transformOrigin:bottom` 0.4 s `power2.out`, stagger 0.25 s; line `stroke:#D0263A; stroke-width:.8vh` via `strokeDashoffset` over 20–25 s `ease:"none"` with the inner group translating left at the same speed; event dot `scale 0 → 1` 0.25 s `back.out(2)`, italic label `italic 400 2.2vh` fade 0.3 s.
- **Focus band**: page on `#000`; back copy `filter: blur(3px) brightness(.35)`; front copy `mask-image: linear-gradient(transparent 38%, #000 44%, #000 56%, transparent 62%)`; phrase box `background:#e9e9e9; color:#111` `scaleX 0 → 1, transformOrigin:left` 0.5 s `power2.inOut`; page push `scale 1 → 1.05` 4 s `none`; source `600 2.2vh` top 7 % left 6 %.
- **Lower third**: no box; name `700 4vh "Inter"` (Investigates: uppercase `4.5vh`), role `400 3vh`; gradient-sweep reveal (`#fff → #ff3b5c → #ff8a00 → #fff`, `backgroundPosition 100% → 0%` 0.6 s `power2.out`); `left:10%` or `right:10%; text-align:right`; `top:74–78%`; hold 3–4 s; fade 0.3 s.
- **Chapter title**: `700 6vh` white, centred at 50 % over a 6–7 s drone/landscape shot, fade in at +1.5 s, out 0.5 s before the cut; duck the score to ~-44 LUFS for 5–7 s.
- **Dark map (Investigates)**: `#1d2226` + noise background, country shape with slate texture, areas `#8E1B2C`; push `scale 1 → 1.12` 6 s `none`; label `italic 500 3.5vh` with leader `scaleX 0 → 1` 0.5 s and dotted underline.
- **Stutter montage**: 8–12 clips of the same action, 3 frames each (0.125 s at 24 fps), hard cuts, end on a 1 s hold.
- **Subtitles**: `500 4vh "Inter"; text-shadow:0 1px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`; `bottom:10%`; ≤ 2 lines.
- **Mix**: voice -15…-17 LUFS short-term, score continuous 10–16 LU under, master ≈ -17 LUFS integrated; dips 0.5–2 s to ~-40 LUFS before sections, then +20 dB.

## Caveats
- Bloomberg's typeface is proprietary; "Inter" is a stand-in. Colours sampled by eye from 480p frames (±10 %).
- The orange-to-white colour wipes on headline cards and lower thirds are inferred from single frames caught mid-animation; exact timing is a guess.
- Score presence is inferred from loudness floors (no stem separation).
- Only the first 150 s of each film analysed; the Investigates film runs 19 min and later chapters may differ.
