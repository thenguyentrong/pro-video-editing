## The core idea
Nothing films look like the product: monochrome, transparent, a little retro-technical, with one red dot of colour. The film is built around ONE idea told without words (a game, a fake world, a noise you can switch off, an insect lifting the case), and the brand voice comes in only at the end, as tiny lowercase dot-matrix cards on black. Text is a system with three voices - dot-matrix for names, a light serif for statements, a typewriter mono for specs - and it is always small. CMF is the loud younger sibling: same dot DNA, but one saturated colour and big captions.

## Sub-styles

**1. Feature-as-plot spot** (Phone (3): Come to Play)
- The feature IS the story device (Glyph Matrix plays spin-the-bottle at dinner). Zero on-screen text, zero logo.
- A-B-A-C loop: feature macro -> face -> feature -> face; shots shrink 1.2 s -> 0.56 s, then a hard 0.4 s silence before the payoff.
- Warm cinematic live action (R-B +18, median luma 56), hard cut to a cold 3D product render for the last 5 s.

**2. Typographic product tour** (Phone (3a) launch film)
- 3D renders on light-grey or charcoal studio gradients; light/dark = the concept and the colour script (diptych opens and closes the film).
- "Flank labels": product dead centre, one dot-matrix word left and one right on the centre line (x 5-13 % and 83-95 %).
- Serif statement cards on black ("Our most advanced camera systems", "Your second memory", "Designed in London"); mono spec callouts with 1 px dotted leader lines.
- Calm, even pace: ASL 3.1 s in each of the first three quarters; morphs inside the render (lens stack -> PCB -> screen) between hard cuts.

**3. Editorial concept film** (Headphone (1), Ear (3))
- An art-directed set is the metaphor: a test lab with giant photo backdrops (forest, moon, city) = you choose your world; a dark hall of fans, horns and old test machines = noise.
- Fashion casting, tableau wides with flat staging, low-angle CUs, product macros. Real footage, no CG.
- Metronome cut around 2 s (Headphone: 1.12-4.56 s, only 4 % under 1 s); muted grade (Headphone R-B -20, saturation 20 %; Ear (3) median luma 32).
- Sound design carries the feature: Ear (3) gates the noise to -46 dBFS for ~1 s twice when she speaks into the case; Headphone dips the music 10-15 dB around the finger-on-control macro (my reading).
- Bookended by dot cards: NOTHING(R) at 0 s, then name -> claim -> NOTHING(R) -> nothing.tech at the end, 1-2 s each.

**4. One-shot deadpan teaser** (Ear (2) black)
- One locked-off 10 s take on white seamless (luma 222, saturation 3 %), one physical gag (a black beetle lifts the black case), then 2 s "ear(2) black" + 2 s "nothing.tech". No cut in the action.

**5. CMF pop spot** (CMF Phone 2 Pro)
- One saturated set colour (orange-red, saturation 79 %, R-B +150), white neon line, talent dressed in the same colour.
- Lyric captions: big dot-matrix caps (9-11 % H), bottom-centre, built word by word on the sung words ("CALL" / "CALL ME" / "CALL ME FOCUSED").
- Graphic transitions: iris/portal mask with cuts inside the mask, 3 x 0.28 s colour-swap cuts, whip pans, zoom-through into the chip. Logo bug top-right all film.

## Measured ranges (6 films)
- ASL 1.61-3.64 s (live-action Nothing films 1.66-2.08 s; render tour 3.64 s; teaser = one take). Median 1.24-2.40 s.
- Shots under 1 s: 4-12 % in the editorial films, 30-33 % in the fast spots (Phone (3), CMF). Over 3 s: 8-17 % (live action), 42 % (render tour).
- Longest shot is usually the END (product render 5.0 s, end cards 4-8 s) or the feature moment (Ear (3) call CU 5.8 s).
- Cards: Nothing end sequence = 3-4 black cards of 1.0-2.2 s each, hard cuts. Text block heights: dot-matrix 3.4-5 % H (Ndot lowercase names, NOTHING(R) ~4 %), serif 5.2-5.8 % H (incl. descenders), mono spec cap height ~1.9 % with 3.4 % line pitch. CMF captions 9-11 % H.
- Text on picture: none in 4 of 5 Nothing films; only the render tour puts labels over picture. CMF puts captions on most shots.
- Loudness -12.2 to -15.4 LUFS integrated. Beat sync weak (cuts on onsets 13-47 % vs 10-45 % chance; only Ear (3) clearly above chance: 23 % vs 10 %).
- Grade: from very dark (Ear (3) 89 % of frames < 40 luma) to very bright (teaser 71 % > 170). Saturation low (3-20 % median) except the warm restaurant (48 %) and CMF (79 %).

## Typography
- **Dot-matrix** (Nothing's Ndot; free match: Doto, DotGothic16): product names in lowercase with brackets and no space - "phone(3a)", "headphone(1)", "ear(2) black", "nothing.tech"; brand "NOTHING(R)" in caps. 3.4-5 % H, centred on black, or as flank labels. Animation: hard cut in/out, or a per-letter scramble/decode (phone(3a) title, ~2 s).
- **Light serif with a typewriter flavour** (free match: Cutive, IBM Plex Serif Light): sentence-case statements and claims, white on pure black, centred, 1 line, 2-5 words, 1.6-2 s, words may appear in 2 steps.
- **Typewriter mono** (free match: Cutive Mono, IBM Plex Mono): spec lists, 1-4 lines, left-aligned, joined to the product with 1 px dotted leader lines.
- CMF: heavy round-dot display caps + wide-tracked mono caps labels top-left + "cmf by NOTHING" bug top-right.

## Colour
- Base = black, white, cool greys (the products are white/black/transparent). One accent: the product's red dot/square, a red chip glow, a blue shirt, a turquoise top.
- Two-world contrasts are common: warm story vs cold render (Phone (3)), light vs dark studio (Phone (3a)).
- No letterbox, no film grain, clean digital.

## Camera
- Real footage for stories: product macros on textured surfaces, tight face CUs with shallow focus, symmetric tableau wides, top-down tabletop. Mostly locked or slow push; handheld only for release moments (dance).
- CG for spec films: slow orbits, exploded views, screen-side renders, pixel-mosaic resolves.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts almost everywhere (Phone (3): 17/17). Inside renders: object-continuity morphs, a push/slide, one fade through black. No whips, flashes or glitch effects in Nothing proper (CMF uses whips, iris masks and colour-swap cuts).

## Sound
- No narrator in any of the six films. Music-led, levelled beds (Phone (3a) holds -11 to -13 LUFS-M for 75 s).
- Silence marks the feature: Phone (3) 0.4 s at -72 dBFS before the payoff; Ear (3) two ~1 s noise drop-outs; Phone (3a) a 5 s dip before the chip reveal, then the loudest moment.
- Logo cards in silence or a decaying tail (Phone (3a) cards fully silent, teaser decays to -30).

## What makes it premium (across examples)
- One idea per film, told visually; the title of the video does the explaining.
- Tiny lowercase type with a strict 3-voice system; never a big headline over picture.
- Monochrome discipline with one accent colour.
- Calm metronome editing (~2 s live action, ~3 s renders), long final holds.
- Silence as punctuation at the feature moment.

## How to recreate with real footage + HTML/GSAP (Hyperframes)
1. Tokens: `--ink:#000; --paper:#fff; --grey-l:#e9edf0; --grey-d:#1a1f24; --accent:#d71921` (bright Nothing red; the red square samples #9f1222 in the shaded render); fonts `Doto` (500), `IBM Plex Serif` (300) or `Cutive`, `IBM Plex Mono` (400).
2. End sequence (black): `phone(3a)` Doto 3.8vh 2.0 s -> claim serif 5vh 1.6 s -> `NOTHING(R)` Doto caps 3.8vh 1.2 s -> `nothing.tech` 1.2 s. Use `tl.set(el,{autoAlpha:1},t)` / `tl.set(el,{autoAlpha:0},t+hold)` - hard cuts, no tweens. Optionally the same NOTHING(R) card at 0-1 s.
3. Dot-matrix decode: per letter, 4 random glyphs from `"!#%&*+=?/0123456789abcdef"` at 40 ms steps then lock; stagger 0.05 s per letter (`gsap.timeline` with `onUpdate` writing `textContent`; seek-safe because each step is time-based).
4. Flank label shot: product image centred at 70 % H on `linear-gradient(180deg,#eef1f3,#c7d0d7)`; two Doto spans at `left:5%` and `right:5%`, `top:50%`, 3.4vh, colour #111; decode in, hold 2.5 s.
5. Spec callout: mono 1.9vh cap, `line-height:1.8`, SVG leader `stroke-dasharray:1 3` drawn by `strokeDashoffset` 0.6 s `power1.out`.
6. Edit live action at a ~2 s pulse (1.6-2.4 s), shorten to 0.6 s only for a tension loop, end on a 4-5 s product hold. Grade to one temperature per world (warm R-B +15 or cool R-B -20), saturation <= 20 % outside the accent.
7. Sound: no VO; one levelled music bed; cut to silence for 0.3-1 s at the feature moment, hit on the reveal; let the logo cards play quiet.
8. CMF variant: `background:#e6350d`, captions `font:900 10.5vh Doto; text-transform:uppercase` bottom-centre, one word per sung word via `tl.set`; whip = `x: 0 -> -120vw` with `filter: blur(18px)` over 0.25 s on both outgoing and incoming layers.
