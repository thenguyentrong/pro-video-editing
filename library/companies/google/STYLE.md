## In one sentence
Google sells the person, not the spec: a human moment (a missing dad in the photo, a fan at a concert, a dad's job interview, two phones stuck in a box) is solved by a Pixel feature, told with warmth or a joke, in a soft one-colour world with friendly sentence-case type - and once a year it flips into a loud, colour-blocked kinetic-type recap.

## Sub-styles

### 1. Editorial kinetic recap (Made by Google "Full Reveal")
- Flat colour cards (coral #FD6A58, maroon, periwinkle #6F80FF, navy, blush #ECCCC8, sage #879665, lime, lilac) with ONE giant word per card: condensed caps (cap 28-100% H, cropped by the frame) or extended heavy caps (line 16-23% H); the phone stands in for a letter ("DR[phone]PPED", "WR[phone]P").
- Product renders sit on a card of their own colour; event-stage reactions (host, guests) are the human layer.
- ASL 1.4 s, 37% of shots < 1 s, 71% of cuts on music onsets; micro-silences 0.15-0.5 s right before each section card.

### 2. Calm hero spot ("Meet Pixel 11 Pro")
- One colour world (coral-pink gradient + rose-gold phone), product macros with very shallow focus, one short sentence per card in sentence case with a full stop (cap ~3.5% H), one real-life proof per feature, black close "Ask more of your phone." -> name card -> G.
- ASL ~2.1 s, flat quarters (1.5-1.9), music only, -16 LUFS. This is Google's most Apple-like register - but in colour.

### 3. Human feature demo (Add Me, Starstruck)
- One problem, one feature, real people in real time; a locked-off take (6.7 s) to show the effect happening, or a performance story cut on lyrics.
- Deadpan joke VO ("I'll stop saying photo now.") or a song whose lyrics match the plot; supers build line by line.
- ASL 2.2-3.8 s, few cuts (2-5 per 10 s).

### 4. Narrative / comedy (Dream Job, #BestPhonesForever)
- Dialogue is the spine (person + Gemini, or Pixel + iPhone + Gemini); the montage illustrates each line with a double meaning.
- Comic timing in silence: 0.65-1.5 s dead air before punchlines; emotional peak after a 2.45 s silence.
- Montage accelerates (down to 0.17 s flash cuts) and then holds 5-9 s for the payoff; type only at the very end.

## Measured ranges
- ASL 1.4 s (recap) / ~2.1 s (hero + vertical) / 1.8-2.5 s (narrative) / 3.75 s (feature demo). Medians 1.1-3.7 s.
- Pace curve: recap fast-slow-fast (1.09 / 1.80 / 1.61 / 1.18); narratives slow down hard at the end (last quarter 2.8-5.8 s).
- Loudness: -13.5 to -16.5 LUFS for music ads (Apple-like), -23.7 LUFS for the dialogue comedy.
- Silences used: 0.15-0.5 s before cards (recap), 0.65-1.5 s comic beats, 1.6 s black after a question, 2.45 s before the payoff line.
- Empty frames: 8-25% of samples (median empty share 21-56%).

## Typography
- Google Sans (proprietary; a variable "Google Sans Flex" may be available on Google Fonts - check before use; otherwise DM Sans / Outfit).
- Hero / feature ads: sentence case WITH a full stop ("Get more done. Be more present.", "Ask more of your phone.", "Video and photos in one tap."), regular weight, white or warm grey, centred, cap 2.5-6% H, 4-7 words, lines built one sentence/line at a time, text changes by a 0.3 s blur dissolve.
- Name card: "Google [phone] Pixel 11 Pro" split left/right of the product (cap ~6-9% H), small "with ✦ Gemini Intelligence" under the name; vertical version stacks name above the phone.
- Gemini word: blue-to-pink gradient text, often arriving last (slide + blur in).
- Recap register: condensed caps cropped by the frame (cap up to 100% H) and extended heavy caps (16-23% H), flat-colour type on flat-colour cards, repeated-word walls that scroll. Free stand-ins: Anton / Bebas Neue (condensed), Archivo Black at wide width or Unbounded (extended).

## Colour
- One hero colour per campaign, product colour = background colour (2026: coral/rose-gold; 2024: porcelain on off-white #F5F3EF).
- Recap: 6-8 flat brand colours, bright and warm, no black world.
- Lifestyle and narrative: natural, filmic, soft contrast; warm tungsten interiors, cool exteriors; real media textures (camcorder REC overlay, phone video, Google Photos cards).
- Closes: off-white + multicolour G + "GoogleStore.com" (2024-25) or black + G (2026).

## Camera
- Real people first: handheld, shallow focus, natural light, faces and hands in close-up; locked-off tripod wides for compositing tricks; screen POV for UI.
- Product macros with shallow depth of field on colour (camera bar, curved glass), slow slides; product often just an object in the room.
- CGI used as live-action (#BestPhonesForever: lens flares, vignette, practical light sources).

## Transitions
- Mostly hard cuts. Specials: product-edge wipes, body wipes (a person crossing the lens hides a cut), blur text swaps, type as the transition (words scroll/scale off, phone becomes a letter), black beats.
- No light flashes, no fly-throughs - the opposite of Samsung.

## Sound
- Music-led ads at -14 to -16.5 LUFS; recap cut tightly on a percussive track; hero spots: instrumental, one hit per feature (+16 dB every 3-5 s).
- Voice: deadpan comedy narrator, character dialogue, Gemini's voice as a character; licensed songs whose lyrics tell the story.
- Silence is a tool: comic beats, cut-to-black after a question, a 2.45 s stop before the emotional payoff.

## How Google differs from Apple's restraint
| | Apple | Google Pixel |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | the product, on black | the person and their moment; product can stay in the background |
| Colour | black/white + product colour | one warm brand colour fills the frame (coral, porcelain); recap = 8 flat colours |
| Type | cap ~4% H statements, 2-5 words, no punctuation | sentence case WITH full stops (friendly, spoken), similar small size in hero spots - but giant cropped one-word cards (20-100% H) in recaps |
| Tone | serious, aspirational | humour, warmth, self-aware jokes, the rival iPhone as a friend character |
| Voice | rarely any | deadpan narrator, dialogue, Gemini as a speaking character |
| Sound | -16 LUFS, music never drops | same level in ads, but silence used as comic/emotional timing (0.65-2.45 s) |
| Pace | ASL 1.0-1.4 s flat | 1.4 s only in the recap; 2-3.8 s in demos; narratives accelerate then hold 5-9 s |
| Similar | - | name split around the product, black close, one move per shot in hero spots |

## The 8 rules
1. Start from a human problem, solve it with one feature, end on the feeling - not on a spec list.
2. One colour world per piece; the product colour is the background colour (or 6-8 flat colours in a recap).
3. Type: Google Sans-style, sentence case with a full stop, 4-7 words, cap 2.5-6% H, built one line at a time; swap text with a 0.3 s blur dissolve.
4. Pace by genre: recap ASL ~1.4 s on the beat; hero spot ~2.1 s flat; feature demo 2.2-3.8 s with one long locked-off take; narrative ~1.8 s, accelerating to 0.2-0.3 s flash cuts, then 5-9 s holds.
5. Use humour and dialogue; leave 0.65-1.5 s of dead air before a punchline and ~2.5 s of silence before the emotional payoff.
6. Show the feature working on real people in real time with the real UI - one action, no clutter.
7. Recap mode: one giant word per flat-colour card, the product replaces a letter, micro-silence (0.15-0.5 s) before each card, cut 70% on onsets.
8. Close the same way every time: line ("Ask more of your phone.") -> name card split around the product with "with ✦ Gemini" arriving last in gradient -> G logo; mix ads at -14 to -16.5 LUFS.

## Recipe (real footage + HTML/GSAP, Hyperframes)
- Hero spot: background `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 45%, #F0C3BF, #D98F88)` (or #F5F3EF off-white), product macros (85-100 mm, f/2), one real-life proof clip per feature. Sentence card: DM Sans 400, cap 3.5% H, white, centred; `from {opacity: 0}` 0.4 s; sentence 2 appended after 0.6 s; swap with `filter: blur(0 -> 10px)` out / reverse in, 0.3 s, overlap 0.15 s. ASL 2.0 s; 30 s = 14 shots.
- Recap cards: full-bleed flat colour, one word; condensed `font: 400 100vh/0.82 'Anton'; letter-spacing: -0.02em` cropped, or extended `font: 900 22vh 'Archivo'; font-stretch: 125%`. Product PNG as a letter: `y: 100% -> 0`, 0.35 s `power4.out`. Word wall: 4-5 repeated lines, `y: 0 -> -50%` over 1 s linear. Cut type cards at 0.25-0.6 s on beats, product 1.2-2 s, reactions 3-5 s; drop the music 0.2 s before each new section card.
- Narrative: record dialogue first; cut on line starts; montage ASL 1.5 s, a 2 s flash run (0.2-0.3 s shots), then 5-6 s holds; archive overlays (camcorder "REC" + date in VT323, pillar-boxed vertical clips, photo cards with dates); 2-2.5 s silence before the payoff line.
- Name card: product centred at 45% H, "Google" left and the model right (DM Sans 400, cap 6-9% H, #3C4043 on light / warm grey on black); "with ✦ Gemini" line 3% H with `background: linear-gradient(90deg,#4285F4,#C76BD3); -webkit-background-clip: text; color: transparent`, sliding in `x: 40 -> 0, filter: blur(8px) -> 0` over 0.5 s.
- Close: black 1.2-1.5 s with "Ask more of your phone." (cap ~6% H, #E8A496 on black), name card 2-2.5 s, G logo 0.5-2.5 s. Master ads at -15 LUFS, dialogue comedy at -20 to -24 LUFS.
