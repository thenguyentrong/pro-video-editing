# Dream Job | Google Pixel SB Commercial 2025  (Google, 6 Feb 2025, 2:05 (125.1 s), 16:9, YouTube @madebygoogle; Super Bowl 2025 campaign, this is the 2:05 YouTube version)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-7e6g11BJc0
Purpose: ad (narrative brand film for Pixel 9 + Gemini Live)
One-sentence point: a dad rehearses a job interview with Gemini Live, and every answer ("long hours with a small team", "quick decisions and negotiating") is illustrated by ~18 years of raising his daughter (camcorder date 2007) - the job he is proudest of.

## Structure
- 0-14.6 s setup: fridge with photo-booth strips, dad in a quiet kitchen talking to his phone (Gemini Live UI). VO/dialogue: "So, at my last job..." Gemini: "tell me about the job that taught you the most."
- 14.6-16.2 s: 1.6 s black hold ("Hmm... okay"), then warm piano enters at 16.2 s.
- 16.2-48 s early years: camcorder home video with REC overlay and "MAR 2 2007" date (20-23 s), night feeds + baby monitor, lunch bags, the phone on the bathroom counter ("try rephrasing to sound more confident"), toddler eating dog food, bedtime books ("One more book. - Two more.").
- 48-67 s school years: Halloween cat costume, Google Photos memory cards dated 2010 / 2018 (54-56 s), a pillar-boxed vertical phone video of dad and daughter dancing (60-63 s), Google search "explain bullying to a child" (66 s).
- 68-87 s teen years: driving lesson, a fender-bender, a fight, an "I'M SORRY" note under a door (77 s), winter bleachers, the old dog dying (81-83 s).
- 88-90.5 s: flash montage of faces (baby, smiling child, graduation cap) - shots 0.17-0.33 s.
- 91-111 s climax: graduation hall, college move-in, dad in the car, daughter runs back, long hug (103-111 s). 106.1-108.55 s: music stops (2.45 s near silence), one piano note.
- 112-116 s: Gemini: "That's a great answer. Sounds like you're ready."
- 116-122.4 s: desk, Pixel 9 on the table, a child's hand, sticky note "good luck dad!"; super "Google Pixel 9 with Gemini Live" (Gemini fades in last); interviewer audio "Hey James, thanks for joining us!" fades out.
- 122.4-125.1 s: Google "G" + "GoogleStore.com" on off-white.

## Cut rhythm (cuts2 + 2 soft transitions added after checking by eye)
- 70 shots, ASL 1.79 s, median 1.56 s. Quarters 1.96 / 1.49 / 1.25 / 2.84 - the montage speeds up, then the ending slows right down.
- 20% < 1 s, 13% > 3 s. Longest 6.1 s (116.3 s desk + super), 5.3 s (100.7 s, dad in the car before the hug), 4.6 s (opening). Shortest 0.17 s x2 (90.3 / 90.5 s, flash montage).
- Densest 80-100 s (8-9 cuts / 10 s); last 25 s only 3 cuts / 10 s.
- Cuts on onsets 42% vs 28% chance (cut to dialogue lines and piano accents).

## Typography
- Almost none: one end super, Google Sans regular, white, centred over the product, cap ~5% H, one line, 7 words; "Gemini" arrives with a soft gradient fade. Diegetic text does the rest (sticky notes, search bar, photo dates).

## Colour / grade
- Filmic, soft contrast, warm tungsten interiors, cold winter blue exteriors, dark night scenes; mixed media (camcorder video, phone video, Google Photos cards) feel like a real family archive.
- Only 8% of samples >= 70% empty (people fill the frame).

## Camera / shots
- Handheld, shallow focus, natural light, lots of close-ups of hands and faces; product only as an object in the room (phone on counters, desk). One top-down crash shot.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts; one 1.6 s cut to black after the first question; dialogue bridges every jump in time (the interview audio runs over the montage).

## Sound
- Two layers: the interview dialogue (dad + Gemini voice) as the spine, a warm piano that grows into a full emotional track. Integrated -13.5 LUFS, loudest -9.4 at 80 s.
- Silence: 14.75 s (0.5 s at -70 dBFS) and the big one 106.1-108.55 s (2.45 s at -72 dBFS) just before Gemini's verdict, then +45 dB return.
- Diegetic bits sit in the mix: "One more book", the crash, laughs.

## What makes it premium
- A universal story (parenting = the hardest job) with the product as the quiet enabler, not the hero.
- Dialogue + montage: every line of the interview gets a picture that turns it funny or sad.
- The 2.45 s silence before "That's a great answer" is the emotional peak.
- Real-life media textures (camcorder, phone video, Photos memories) make it feel true.
