Purpose: launch film (recap / sizzle of the Made by Google 2026 event: Pixel 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro Fold, Watch 5, Buds Pro 2)
One-sentence point: everything launched today in two minutes - told as a loud, colour-blocked kinetic-type magazine, with event-stage reactions as the "people" layer.

## Typography
- Three registers (one variable family pushed to extremes is my guess - plausibly Google Sans Flex; not verified):
  1. Condensed display caps: "DROPPED", "REDEFINED", "HELPS YOU", "PASSWORDS", "WRAP", "VOICE", "EMERGENCY DETECTION". Cap 28-100% H, often bleeding off the frame; the phone replaces a letter.
  2. Extended heavy caps: "INTRODUCING" (line 16% H), "BETTER" x3 (21% H each), "MOMENTS" (20% H), "VOICE TYPING" (2 x 22% H), "ACCURATE" (23% H), "GPS".
  3. Google Sans regular/medium for names and the lockup: "Made / by / Google" stacked, 3 lines ~13% H each; product supers ~6% H.
- Flat colour type on flat colour cards (coral on maroon, navy on periwinkle, white on coral, dark green on sage). Repeated-word walls that scroll (MATTER, HILIGHT).
- Words per card 1 (sometimes 2); hold 0.3-1.2 s. Free stand-ins: Anton / Bebas Neue (condensed), Archivo Black at width 125 or Unbounded (extended), Google Sans -> DM Sans/Outfit.

## Colour / grade
- Card palette (measured): coral #FD6A58, maroon ~#4A0A14, periwinkle #6F80FF, navy #050943, blush pink #ECCCC8, sage #879665, lime/chartreuse, lilac. Phones match the cards (coral phone on pink, green Fold on sage).

## Camera / shots
- ~40% type cards, ~30% product renders/macros on matching colour, ~20% event stage footage (host, guests, reactions), ~10% UI and lifestyle. Product moves: slow rotations and slides; type does the motion.

## Transitions
- Mostly hard cuts on beats; the type itself is the transition (words slide/scroll off, a word scales to full frame, the phone becomes a letter). Soft: 5 (type scrolls and the voice-typing word drop).

## Sound
- Upbeat percussive track + narrator VO + talent sound bites ("That's amazing. I like that.", "Incroyable!", "Are you kidding me?").
- Integrated -16.5 LUFS (same level as Apple films). Drop-out 44-45 s (-16 -> -28 LUFS) for the silent-phone demo, then back +14 LU.
- Micro-silences (0.15-0.5 s, -50 to -63 dBFS) right before section cards: 31.55 s (0.5 s, before the host + HILIGHT), 51.5 s (MATTER), 60.9 s (VOICE TYPING), 100.2-106.5 s (four stops on the PASSWORDS / PHOTOS / EMAILS word cards). Each is followed by a hit on the card.
- Biggest rises: +38 dB at 61.15 s ("VOICE TYPING"), +28.8 dB at 107.2 s. Loudest 71.5 s (studio laugh + music).
- Ends: music out 121 s, logo in silence.

## What makes it premium
- A strict editorial system: 3 type registers, 8 flat colours, product colour = card colour.
- The phone interacts with the type (replaces a letter), so type and product feel designed together.
- Real people reacting (stage clips) keep the hype human and funny.
- Very fast but every card has one word - readable at 0.5 s.

## Recipe (real footage + HTML/GSAP)
- Cards: full-bleed `background` from the 8-colour list; one word per card; condensed caps at `font-size: 60vh; line-height: .85; letter-spacing: -0.02em` cropped by the frame, or extended caps 18-22% H.
- Letter swap: set the word with a transparent letter span and place a product PNG in its box; slide the PNG `y: 100% -> 0` 0.35 s `power4.out`.
- Word walls: repeat the word 4-5 lines, animate the stack `y: 0 -> -50%` over 1 s `none` (scroll), cut on the next beat.
- Cut on the beat: 0.25-0.6 s for type cards, 1.2-2 s product, 3-5 s for demos/reactions.
- Voice typing look: words `opacity 0, y -40 -> 0, rotate -6 -> 0` staggered 0.12 s over a blue pill (#4F5BEF -> #6E7BFF gradient).
- End: "THAT'S" (white on coral 0.6 s) -> "WRAP" full-height condensed with the phone as the A (0.8 s) -> lockup 1.5 s -> logo on black 2.5 s. Mix at -16 LUFS.
