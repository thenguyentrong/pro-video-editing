# Starship's Tenth Flight Test  (SpaceX, 2025-09-29, 2:14, 16:9, YouTube @SpaceX)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rcd_SQZDlnk
Purpose: field-proof recap (flight-test highlight film)
One-sentence point: one flight test, told in order from pad to splashdown, entirely with real cameras and real call-outs.

## Structure (no narrator; real mission audio + music)
- 0.0-9.9 hook: the stacked vehicle on the tower at sunset, wide then closer silhouettes.
- 9.9-17.0 people: aerial of the site, spectators, launch control room through windows, an engineer on a headset. Countdown audio (11.5-18).
- 17.0-36 launch: top-down tower view, engine ignition from a camera inside the engine bay, tower-top fish-eye, long-lens lift-off, smoke, control room windows.
- 36-64 ascent and separation: long-range tracking, onboard views of engines, stage separation, the booster turning back, the booster landing area from the shore.
- 64-90 in space: crowd cheering at the factory, onboard tank and payload-bay cameras, satellite deployment, more cheering.
- 90-120 re-entry: engine relight (onboard), plasma on the heat shield, flaps, engineers at consoles.
- 120.0-134.4 ending: splashdown in the ocean, then black, then a fast tease of the next flight with two small words ("up next" type super) and the flight number.
