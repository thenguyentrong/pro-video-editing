# Meet Act II of Arc Browser | A browser that browses for you  (The Browser Company, 2024-02-01, 860.4 s, 16:9, YouTube live premiere)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WIeJF3kL5ng
Purpose: launch film, founder-led (a pre-recorded "event" that announces a new product direction and four features)
One-sentence point: The browser should do the browsing for you, and these four features are the first steps.

Structure: beats with timestamps (hook, build, turn, payoff, CTA/end card)
- 0-46 s (0-5 %) cold open: the CEO in a black-void interview set, lit from one side, speaking straight to the lens with long pauses. A blue film-poster title card (4.7-8.3 s), two small serif lines on black (17-22 s), a behind-the-scenes shot of lights and crew (37 s).
- 46-119 s (5-14 %) set-up: "a Browser Company production" in small serif on a dark diner, then a team member at a laptop and the first "browsing for you" demo.
- 119.6-235 s (14-27 %) chapter 1: blue chapter card (feature name in huge caps + a serif subline), then a diner booth where the CEO demos to team members and guests, cut with screen inserts and reactions.
- 236-299 s (27-35 %) the history interlude: a teal-graded short skit with an actor, serif captions on picture, intercut with archive web pages of early search and ads.
- 299.9-548 s (35-64 %) chapter 2: card, diner demo, screen recordings, a warp-tunnel graphic, then a tabletop metaphor with coloured wooden blocks and archive 1990s home pages.
- 549.5-654 s (64-76 %) chapter 3 (mobile): card, a street walk-and-talk with the CEO, phone macros in hand, blocks again.
- 654.6-760 s (76-88 %) chapter 4: card, diner demo, screen inserts, an archive photo, quiet diner atmosphere and a "final hour of filming" caption.
- 760-853 s (88-99 %) summary: the CEO in the diner at night; each feature returns as a serif lower third with its status ("available today", "coming soon"); reactions, phone shots, a clapperboard, back to the black interview set.
- 853.6-860 s (99-100 %) logo mark on black, then the URL.

Typography: typeface (or closest free match), weight, case, size as % of frame height, tracking, placement, words per card, hold time, animation in/out

- Opening title: film-poster pastiche on the same blue: mixed widths (condensed and wide caps of different sizes, 6-15 % H), small "production picture" and "copyright MMXXIV" credits left and right with emblems, a serif line at the bottom.
- Serif captions on picture or black: small (cap ≈2-3 % H), cream or white, centred, sentence case, 2-6 words.
- Summary lower thirds: same serif, cream, cap ≈4 % H, centred at ≈91 % H, "Feature — status" with an em dash, hold 4-8 s.
- Screen inserts carry their own UI type; no captions for speech.

Colour/grade: palette, contrast, grain, letterbox

- Diner: warm, slightly green-yellow film look (#4b4435, #a2ae8d), soft contrast, light grain; night summary is amber and red bokeh (#482818, #7c6d3c).
- Skit: teal monochrome (#136151, #1a8067). Cards: one flat royal blue, the only saturated graphic colour.

Camera/shots: macro, product on black/white, people, handheld, drone, screen recordings, 3D renders; moves
- Documentary coverage: locked interview on black; handheld two- and three-shots in the diner booth; over-the-shoulder laptop and phone macros (the real screen filmed, often tilted); clean full-screen recordings for detail; a street walk-and-talk; tabletop block close-ups; archive stills. Behind-the-scenes shots (crew, lights, clapperboard) are left in on purpose.

Transitions: hard cut / match cut / morph / mask / whip / dissolve, and how often
- Almost all hard cuts. The chapter cards act as act breaks. One graphic warp tunnel (≈464 s). No wipes or flashes.

Sound: music style, voice yes/no, SFX density, drop-outs, biggest hit, loudness
- Speech for ≈661 s of 860 s (≈77 %, ≈3.9 words/s, measured with a speech detector, not transcribed): the CEO to camera and in conversation, plus team members and guests in the demos.
- The cold open has no music bed: 20 silences of 0.35-2.05 s between lines in the first 36 s. The pause is the tension.
- Music and stings come in with the montages; biggest rises +33.7 dB (213 s) and +31.9 dB (243.7 s) in chapter 1 and the skit. Loudest 0.5 s at 402.7 s.
- Integrated −17.3 LUFS, LRA 10.6 LU, true peak +1.2 dBTP (over the usual −1 dBTP ceiling).

What makes it premium (3-5 bullets)
- It is built like a documentary episode, not a keynote: interview, observational demos, archive, a skit, BTS.
- The founder carries the argument (why browsers must change) before any feature is named.
- One strict graphic system: royal blue cards with wide caps + a classic serif, used only at act breaks.
- Real reactions from real people at a table make the demos believable.
- Silence in the cold open: a bare voice on black for 36 s.

Recipe: how to recreate the look with real footage + HTML/GSAP (Hyperframes), concrete values
- 10-15 min for an event-style launch: 5 % cold open (founder on black), 9 % set-up, 3-4 chapters of 13-30 % each, one 7-8 % history/metaphor interlude, 10 % summary with status lower thirds, 1 % logo.
- Shoot: founder on black (one soft key 45°, no fill, 50-85 mm); a real location for demos with 2 handheld cameras; over-the-shoulder screen shots; one tabletop metaphor with physical objects.
- Chapter card: `background:#0f3ff7`; words `800 200px "Archivo"` with `font-stretch:125%` (or Unbounded 800), colour #f3f0dc, line-height 0.9, centred; build with `tl.set` per word at +0.35 s steps; subline `400 44px "Sorts Mill Goudy"` at y 78 %; hold to 4 s; hard cut.
- Lower third: `400 40px "Sorts Mill Goudy"` #f3f0dc, centred at y 91 %, "Feature — Status", `autoAlpha` 0.3 s, hold 5 s.
- Sound: no music under the cold open; music only in montages; master −16 to −17 LUFS with a −1 dBTP limiter.
- Stock queries (for interludes): "vintage diner booth night", "wooden toy blocks tabletop close up", "1990s computer screen old website", "dark corridor teal light walking".
