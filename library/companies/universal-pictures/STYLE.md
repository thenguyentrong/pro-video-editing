## Core idea
Universal lets the filmmaker's brand lead. Both films are sold as "an event by a director": a director card is the first text in every piece, the picture is held longer than in any other studio set in the library (ASL 2.3–2.8 s in the long cuts), and the card system is built from one idea of the film (engraved slate letters for an epic; silver "declassified" letters for a secrets thriller). The studio logo is either first and plain (Nolan) or missing at the start (Spielberg).

## Shared rules (with numbers)
| Beat | Odyssey | DD teaser | DD spot | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First thing seen | MPA band + studio and production logos (0–14.8 s) | black 0.17 s, then story | a news "interruption" at full level | either logos first and plain, or no logo at all |
| Director card | 52.6 s (46 %) | 44.6 s (35 %) | 9.9 s (16 %) | one "FROM [director]" card, 1.9–4.7 s |
| Month card | "THIS SUMMER" 74 % | "THIS SUMMER" 61 % | "THIS JUNE" 36 % | month or season only, until the end card |
| Title | 91 % | 92 % | 91 % | **title at 91–92 %** in all three |
| After title | date 2.0 s + billing 1.75 s | 3 credit cards × 1.0 s + date 1.5 s | 1 credit 1.75 s + date 1.2 s | short end block, no comic button |
| Held images | first two shots 6.8 + 6.5 s | newsroom take 7.8 s, crop-circle aerial 4.4 s | 4.5 s dialogue, 3.2 s aerial | at least one 4–8 s hold per piece |

- **Text is rare**: 4–6 text moments per piece, 1–3 words each.
- **Cards hold long** in the long cuts: 3.8–6.0 s (Odyssey), 2.5–5.0 s (teaser); the spot shortens them to 1.7–2.4 s.
- **Loudness**: all three at −13.7 to −14.0 LUFS and hot true peaks (+0.2 to +0.9 dBTP; do not copy).
- **Silence is used sparingly**: no pre-title silence in any of the three. The Odyssey stops with a 3 s fall on black (−11 → −23 dBFS); the teaser uses two 0.4 s drop-outs (to −51…−54 dBFS) each followed by a +37…+39 dB hit.
- **Talk**: Odyssey ≈1 s of detected speech (music-led), the teaser 78 %, the spot 86 %.

## Sub-styles
**A. Auteur epic (The Odyssey).** Logos first, then two 6.5–6.8 s holds; ASL never under 2 s per quarter; 8 black holds of 0.8–3.9 s as chapter breaks; one long score crescendo from −30 to −11 dBFS RMS; wide-tracked geometric caps (Montserrat-like) in dark slate (#252a40) on black, title ≈11 % H with ≈1 em tracking, faded in and held 6 s.

**B. Mystery teaser (Disclosure Day teaser).** No opening logo, a 17 s quiet dialogue open, one long uncomfortable scene (the broken broadcast) instead of a montage, a "declassified" card reveal (letters fill in as vertical slices over 1.8–2.7 s, then a soft glow), condensed silver caps at 21–24 % H on full-frame black, cuts on dialogue not on the beat (24 % on onsets vs 33 % chance).

**C. Event TV spot (Disclosure Day Big Game).** Same cards and skeleton compressed 2×: hook inside the medium (breaking news), director card at 16 %, month card at 36 %, a 15 s montage at ASL ≈1.0 s cut to the music (37 % on onsets vs 26 %), two slow shots and the biggest hit, title at 91 %.

## Recipe
1. Decide the director/brand claim and put it on one card between 15 % and 46 %.
2. Hold the opening images 4–7 s; keep the long-cut ASL near 2.3–2.8 s.
3. Build every card from one idea of the film (engraved, declassified, etc.) and keep it for the whole campaign (teaser → spot).
4. Month card, then title at 91–92 %, then a 3–5 s end block (credits 1.0 s each, date 1.2–2.0 s).
5. Master −14 LUFS, true peak −1 dBTP.
