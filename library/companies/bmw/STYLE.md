## Core idea
BMW anthems ask or tell **one thing about the driver's feeling** ("sheer driving pleasure") and prove it with the brand's own history. The past is never archive footage in the launch film: it is restaged as cinema, era by era, and the old characters ride in the new car.

## Rules that repeat
- **One verbal device:** a question repeated as on-screen text ("HERE?", "WHO ARE YOU?"), or a 7-line narrator over 100 s. Text and voice say the same word.
- **Text as an object in the space:** the repeated word changes size (≈4-11 % H) and position per shot, set into the scene; bold red caps (≈7.5-8 % H) in the fashion film.
- **Letterbox for launches** (2.2:1 and 2.35:1), full 16:9 for the TV anthem.
- **Black hold before the new car:** 3.56 s (iX3), 1.12 s with a 0.45-s silence (Vision M NEXT). The product appears from black with a light sweep.
- **Even pace, no beat cutting:** ASL per quarter stays within 1.1-1.7 s; cuts on onsets are below chance (8-11 % vs 13-20 %).
- **Loudness rises with the future:** iX3 -19.9 LUFS before the black hold, -14.1 after (+5.8 LU).
- **End:** product name in the sky (≈6.3 % H, off-centre), roundel + claim on the last shot, 1.8-2.5 s black/silence. The 45-s anthem ends on a single continuous 9-s crane shot with two "100 %" claim lines.
- Sans only (BMW corporate sans → Inter / Manrope), light with wide tracking for questions, semibold for product lines.

## Recipe
1. Choose the device: a one-word question in text + voice, or eras restaged (4-5 eras of 8-15 s, each its own grade).
2. Before the product: black hold 1-3.5 s; reveal from darkness with a light sweep 2 s.
3. Raise the mix ≈+6 LU for the product part.
4. Close with name, logo, 2 s silence. For a 45-s TV cut, end on one 8-9 s moving shot with two claim lines.
