# The Soul of BMW.  (BMW, 2024-09-09, 0:45, 16:9, YouTube @BMW)
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-8UPg6hyDBg
Purpose: brand anthem (heritage + electric range), 45 s TV length
One-sentence point: Where is the soul of BMW - in the race cars, the designs, the people? It is all here, now 100 % electric.

## Structure
- 0-2.5 a car in silhouette at dusk; "THE SOUL OF BMW" in thin spaced caps (1-3 s).
- 2.5-19 **the question device**: one word, "HERE?", appears in every shot, each time in a different size and place, set into the picture: over a race car, a drifting coupé on a pier, a car hanging off a building (film stunt), a designer at sketches, a concept car's hood, an SUV in water, a toy, a painted art car, the headquarters at night, a classic coupé from above. The voice asks the same question. Shots 0.3-1.3 s, a new decade each time.
- 19-21 dark transition through a shadow.
- 21-36 **the answer**: real drivers smiling in today's electric cars on a coastal road; "ALL" (29 s) and "HERE" (30 s) as two words split across two shots.
- 35.8-45 one continuous crane shot that rises from a red car to a formation of electric BMWs; "100% DRIVING PLEASURE" then "100% ELECTRIC"; roundel + i logo on the sky.
