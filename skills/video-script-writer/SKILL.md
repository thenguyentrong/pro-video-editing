# Video script writer

Load the `professional-video-editing` skill (story order in `references/cutting-craft.md`) and read the project's `brief.md` and `style.md` first.

## Steps
1. **One sentence** a stranger can repeat. Put it at the top of `script.md`.
2. **Argument chain:** hook → tension/question → evidence → turn → payoff → call to action. Each beat is one claim + the proof that will be on screen. Test: read the beats with "so / but / that's why" between them; if a link is missing, the film will not connect.
3. **Budget:** voice ≈ 2.5 words/s; leave 0.5–1 s after key lines and room for the style's silence (trailer: 0.35–2 s before the turn). 20 s ≈ 35–45 words, 30 s ≈ 55–65, 60 s ≈ 120–140.
4. **On-screen text:** follow the style file (e.g. trailer 4–6 cards of 1–4 words; Apple 2–5 words per card; social 2–4-word caption chunks). Text says what the voice does not, or repeats only the key line.
5. **Shot intention per beat:** what the shot must show and why ("prove the robot is real and Chinese-made"), not a file name. The selectors find the material.
6. **Facts:** every number and claim has a source next to it in the script. No invented quotes. Product promises are written as promises.
7. **Cutdowns:** when asked, write the 30/15/6 s versions by removing beats, not by speeding up.

## Output format (`script.md`)
```
ONE SENTENCE: ...
| # | t (s) | beat | voice line | on screen | shot intention | source |
```
Plus total words, estimated duration and the place of the turn (seconds) for the music selector.

## Quality bar
Short, concrete words; names said plainly; no slogans without proof; one key point per 30 s.
