# Apple precision
**Use it for:** product spots, launch films, feature films for software or hardware, "proof" ads where a story shows what a device can do, calm brand pieces where the product or the brand line comes last; premium non-Apple hardware (audio, cameras, wearables, design objects) with the warmer variant F. **Not for:** loud spec-heavy launches (see `tech-launch-loud.md`), warm comedy or family stories where the person is the hero (see `human-demo-warm.md`), news or explainer videos, anything that needs many words on screen.

## Signature
One thing at a time. Each shot or card holds one idea, and nothing competes with it. The frame is either light and empty (white or light-grey sweep, often 46-65 % of frames mostly empty) or held in one controlled grade with one product colour as the only accent. Text is small, calm, centred, with no boxes, and it often appears only at the end. Hard cuts do almost all the work. Proof beats claims: real footage shot on the device, behind-the-scenes rig inserts, the feature used as the film technique. Each fast run starts with a long "breath" shot, sound drops out for a moment before the biggest hit, and the logo or title ends in silence.

## Structure
**A. Product spot (≈45 s)**
- 0-5 %: small ident or product name (fade or RGB glitch in).
- 4-15 %: hook. One card, then the product enters from the frame edge, held by fingertips.
- 12-85 %: feature run as card / product / card. Each card is one feature. Proof inserts (sample photos) are fast; cards are slow.
- 75-94 %: payoff. Colourways, product in the case, product upright.
- 94-100 %: logo, music already gone (0.2-3 s of silence).

**B. Narrated launch film (2-4 min)**
- 0-5 %: one long breath shot (4-9.5 s) with the opening line of VO.
- 5-88 %: sections of 15-30 s. Either a cause-and-effect chain (chip leads to heat, heat to cooling, cooling to battery) or a tour (feature by feature, each as a physical trick). Each section opens with a 4-8 s hold, then cuts at 0.5-1.2 s.
- ≈85 %: a 0.5 s suck-out, then the biggest sound and picture hit.
- 88-98 %: recap + bookend (the VO returns to the opening line).
- Last 1.5-8 %: product name small on black, or the wordmark on the product; sound already out.

**D. Story / proof ad (30-60 s; up to ~3 min for testimonials)**
- 0-60 %: story with no text and no product as hero. Cinema grammar.
- ≈63-70 %: the turn. Music drops for a spoken joke or a gag, then slams back.
- Last 3-27 %: supers and the product (often only the last 10 %). Brand line 1.2-2.5 s.
- Last 2-5 %: logo on black (1-3 s), sometimes a partner logo first.

## Picture
- **Subjects:** the product alone (renders, macros), one pair of hands on a white table, real extreme proof shot on the device (moon, caves, surf, night sky), actors in a cinema-style story, real people's found footage framed on a clean canvas.
- **Camera moves:** slow sliders with rack focus on macros; product rises from the bottom edge; slow rotations; top-down exploded parts; locked 3/4 overhead for hands; slow dolly on a presenter; crash zooms inside shots in comedy (the zoom itself is the gag). No handheld in product spots.
- **Lens / shot sizes:** macro and extreme close-up for the product; symmetric wides and low angles in story ads; fisheye close to the subject in music cuts; wide + medium two-camera set-up for a presenter.
- **Grade:** either light and neutral (sweep #f3f3f5-#f4f3f6 with a darker grey top band ≈#d1d5d9), or one controlled look: dark steel/teal low saturation, cool noir with one warm prop, warm "storybook" with teal-green and brick, warm interior vs. cool exterior for music cuts, deep green telenovela-noir for vertical drama. Saturated colour is saved for the feature (rainbow AI edge, thermal, iridescent rings).
- **Texture:** clean, no grain in product films; light grain in music cuts. Found footage keeps its own look.
- **Aspect:** 16:9. Story ads may use a 2.39:1 letterbox inside 16:9 or a full 4:3 frame. Vertical clips sit pillarboxed on black or as windows on a canvas, never with a blurred fill.

## Type
- **Typeface:** SF Pro Display / Text. Closest free: **Inter Display / Inter**. Campaign titles may use their own display face (an extended heavy grotesk; a flared serif), only for the title, never for supers.
- **Weight:** medium 500 for supers and cards; semibold 600 for names. Avoid light or thin weights for statements. Web type spec for big headlines: weight 600, line-height 1.05, tracking −0.015 em; small eyebrow text slightly open, +0.007 em (craft note, not a film measurement).
- **Case:** sentence case. Brand lines end with a full stop.
- **Tracking:** −0.5 to −1 % on supers.
- **Size:** cap 3-7.5 % H. Labels 3-4 %, supers 4.5-7.5 %, end titles 3-4.5 %, legal 1.2-2 % H in grey. Big type only as a device: 9 % hero words, 13 % keynote title, typed letters up to 14 % as the camera pushes in. For Inter, font-size ≈ cap / 0.73: cap 3 % → 4.2vh, cap 4.5 % → 6-6.5vh, cap 7 % → 9.5vh.
- **Placement:** centred at y ≈ 47-50 %, one line. Two-line labels go in the empty half next to the product (x 65-85 %). Keynote lower third: two lines, right-aligned at x ≈ 95 %, y 81-91 %, no box.
- **Colour:** dark slate #15222f / #1f2d3d on light, white on footage. One keyword in a lighter blue-grey #7d9bb5, or a pink-to-purple gradient for an AI feature.
- **Animation in:** fade 0.3-0.6 s, `power1.out`. No slide on cards.
- **Animation out:** cross-dissolve to the next card 0.3-0.5 s, or the product pushes the card up (`yPercent: -120`, 0.6 s, `power3.inOut`).
- **Word build:** each word appears in its final place (the line does not re-centre), ≈0.7 s per word.
- **Light sweep** on one keyword: gradient text, `background-position` over ≈0.8 s.
- **Word bursts** (craft note): short one- or two-syllable words, one at a time, 5-7 frames each, ending in a sudden stop on a clean lockup.
- **Premium non-Apple type†:** neo-grotesk (Inter) for lines and labels, or a light geometric sans (closest free: Jost / Outfit Light) for model names in wide-tracked caps (+0.08 em, cap ≈ 4 % H, alone on black). Warm brands set one keyword per line in a light italic serif (closest free: Instrument Serif Italic), built ≈ 0.5-0.8 s after the sans part. Edition names: italic serif under a sans product name. Spec labels sit low (y ≈ 85 %, cap ≈ 4.6 % + 3.5 % sub-line) and stay for the whole shot.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts dominate. Product films: 15-21 hard cuts in 43-49 s; launch film: 143 cuts in 172 s with no dissolves seen.
- Cross-dissolves only between text cards (0.3-0.5 s). In the iPhone Air spot, 7 of 27 changes are dissolves or vertical pushes.
- Hidden cuts in whips and hand passes (hands film: colour swaps every ≈2-3 s with almost no visible cut).
- Morphs for reveals: black silhouette becomes the product, padlock becomes the logo, light becomes the screen. Fly-through rings (zoom through a ring into the next shot).
- One black flash under 0.5 s at most. No decorative wipes, no flash frames as habit.
- Premium non-Apple†: the camera move is often the transition (one 67 s CG take through a cut-away house; parts fly in and lock during an orbit); fades through black (0.4-0.6 s) join product macros in dark films; a printed photo on black works as a "shutter" after footage of the photographer.

## Sound
- **Music:** product spots = music only, one track, cuts partly on beats. Launch films = music under VO. Story ads = the song or score is a character; music stops for the punchline.
- **VO:** none in product spots. Launch films: one voice, second person ("you start with...") or friendly first-person plural. No feature supers when VO is present.
- **SFX:** one SFX per visible action (shutter on each proof photo, case click, charging tone, feature tone). Launch films have dense foley (knocks, snaps, clatter, whooshes).
- **Mix:** −16 LUFS integrated (−15.6 to −16.3), true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Keynote speech ≈ −19 LUFS. VO at −16, music ducked 8-10 dB under VO.
- **Silence:** 0.2-1.8 s drop-outs before the biggest hit or the joke. End on the logo in silence, or on one last sound (heartbeat, lock click, a laugh).
- **Premium non-Apple†:** 8 of 12 films are music only; the 4 VO films use one calm voice wall-to-wall and almost no supers (one word, a title, or tiny call-out labels). Logos sit in 1.7-5.9 s of silence in 6 of 12. A long spec film stops the music for 0.3-0.45 s at each new section. Uploaded masters range −9.0 to −23.8 LUFS; master at −14 to −16 anyway.

## Do / Don't
**Do**
- One idea per shot or card. If a frame has two ideas, split it.
- Keep ≤ 7 words per card (≤ 6 per shot as a check), small, centred, no box.
- Leave ≥ 45 % of the frame empty in product spots.
- Open every fast section with a long breath shot (4-9 s).
- Show proof: real footage, the device in its rig, the feature as the technique.
- One grade, one accent colour (the product colour).
- Cut all sound 0.2-0.8 s before the biggest hit; end in silence.
- Let light reveal a dark product (a light band crossing the body, 4-10 s shots) before any text or cut rhythm starts.†
- When the brand has a mark colour (a red dot), make it the only colour in the film, even in black and white.†
- Match each colourway to a world of the same colour, one world per colourway.†
- In long technical films: one label per shot, low in frame, and a 0.3-0.45 s music stop to open each section.†

**Don't (what makes a copy look cheap)**
- Text boxes, bars, outlines, drop shadows, bold weights, ALL CAPS supers.
- Many colours, colour grading on the product colour, saturated colour outside the feature.
- Supers over the whole film when there is a narrator.
- Decorative wipes, zoom transitions, light leaks between every shot.
- Blurred-fill backgrounds behind vertical clips.
- Fake UI built from grey boxes. Show real UI, one action at a time, no VO over the UI moment.
- A logo with music still playing under it.
- A white sweep for a warm, body-worn product: use home materials (linen, oak, satin) and one accent colour instead.†
- Re-colouring matte black/white products to fit the grade; colour comes from the world around them.†
- Copying brand-upload loudness (−9 or −24 LUFS) instead of mastering to −14 to −16 LUFS.†

## Recipe
1. **Pick the sub-style** (A spot, B launch, D story, vertical). Write one sentence that the film must say.
2. **Footage (licensed stock or your own):**
   - Spot: product on white or light-grey sweep, macro slider moves, fingertips holding the product, 3-5 sample photos/videos as proof.
   - Launch (dark): macro of metal edges, exploded parts top-down, lab/clean-room people, extreme outdoor proof (surf, caves, night sky), one long opening landscape.
   - Launch (bright): one pair of hands on a white table, locked 3/4 overhead, 24 fps, 1/50 shutter; plan a hand pass or whip every 3-5 s to hide a cut.
   - Story: actors, symmetric wides, one visual metaphor that needs no words.
   - Stock search queries: "white cyclorama studio empty", "product macro slider rack focus", "hands on white table top down", "smartphone macro dark background", "dancer silhouette white background", "earthrise space footage", "surfer big wave slow motion", "cave abseil headlamp", "milky way time lapse", "symmetrical library interior", "noir warehouse detective", "found footage doorbell camera" (only with clear licence).
   - Premium non-Apple (F)†: "aluminium grinding sparks close up", "white gloves assembling product", "empty white warehouse wide", "camera lens element macro", "leaf shadow on beige wall", "linen curtain morning light", "oak table macro sunlight", "infinity pool blue towel", "clay tennis court shadow", "starling murmuration dusk", "black and white street photographer".
3. **Background tokens:** `--bg-light:#f4f3f6; --bg-top:#d6d9dd; --ink:#1f2d3d; --ink-soft:#7d9bb5; --legal:#8a8a8a`. Sweep: `radial-gradient(120% 80% at 50% 60%, #f6f5f8 0%, #eeeef1 60%, #d6d9dd 100%)`.
4. **Card:** Inter Display 500, `font-size: 9.5vh` (cap ≈ 7 % H) for spot cards or `6.5vh` (cap ≈ 4.6 % H) for supers, `letter-spacing: -0.01em`, centred with `display:grid; place-items:center`. Keyword in a `<em>` coloured #7d9bb5.
5. **Card timing (GSAP):** `tl.from(card,{opacity:0,duration:.45,ease:"power1.out"}).to(card,{opacity:0,duration:.35},"+=1.5")`. Push: `tl.to(card,{yPercent:-120,duration:.6,ease:"power3.inOut"}).from(productClip,{yPercent:100,duration:.6,ease:"power3.inOut"},"<")`.
6. **Rhythm:**
   - Spot (30-50 s): card 1.5-1.8 s → product 1.5-3 s → card...; proof inserts 0.7-0.8 s, each with a shutter SFX; 25-30 units; logo 2.5-3 s in silence.
   - Launch (2-3 min): sections of 15-30 s; open each with a 4-8 s hold, then 0.5-1.2 s cuts; for a "history" row cut every 3-4 frames (0.12-0.17 s) with identical framing.
   - Story (30-60 s): 85-90 % story with no text, cut to action and speech, not to the beat; then 1-3 supers of 1.2-2.5 s; logo 1.5-3 s.
   - Vertical series: 3.5-4.5 s shots, one dialogue line per shot.
7. **Object camera moves (craft note):** start on a detail and pull back (`scale 2.2 → 1` over ≈4 s, `expo.out`). Light sweep across an edge: a `linear-gradient` band with `mix-blend-mode: screen`, x −120 % → 120 % over 1.6 s, `power2.inOut`. Order: reveal → details → product in context.
8. **Grade presets (CSS on video):** dark engineering `filter: saturate(.75) contrast(1.08) brightness(.95)` + #0d2a33 overlay at 20 % soft-light, product colour on its own unmasked layer. Noir story `saturate(.6) contrast(1.1)` + teal 18 % soft-light + letterbox bars `height:12.7vh`. Storybook `saturate(.85) contrast(1.05)` + #3a2a14 at 10 % soft-light. Light spot: no grade.
9. **Crash zoom on real footage:** `tl.to(video,{scale:3,transformOrigin:"62% 40%",duration:.18,ease:"power4.in"})` + 2-3 frames of `blur(4px)` + a whoosh/hit SFX; hold 0.8-1.2 s.
10. **Word-built brand line:** full line in spans with `opacity:0`, then `tl.to(spans,{opacity:1,duration:.15,stagger:.7})`.
11. **End:** product name or logo centred on black (cap 3-4.5 % H), `opacity 0 → 1` 0.5-0.6 s; legal 12-13 px #8a8a8a at bottom 6 %; music out 0.2-1 s before; hold 1.5-3 s silent.
12. **Mix:** master −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP; one SFX per action; one 0.2-0.8 s drop-out before the biggest hit.

## Variants
**A. Product precision spot** (apple_iphoneair_film, apple_airpodspro3_film). Light sweep or white void, 46-65 % empty frames, card/product rhythm, music only, SFX per feature. AirPods variant: black dancer silhouettes on white (push the footage to pure black shapes), thin iridescent CG rings the camera flies through, labels 3.5-4 % H in the empty half, RGB-split glitch only on title and logo (three R/G/B copies, offset ±6 px for 4-6 frames), heartbeat SFX at open and close.

**B1. Dark engineering launch** (apple_iphone18pro_intro). Steel/teal low-saturation grade, macro, exploded renders, CAD/blueprint flicker in 0.12-0.4 s steps, thermal and X-ray looks, lab rigs, extreme proof footage pillarboxed. ASL 1.2 s, 53 % of shots under 1 s, yet each section opens with a 4-9.5 s hold. No supers at all; small end title.

**B2. Bright hands-on launch** (apple_macbookneo_hello). White seamless table, one pair of hands as performer, every feature a physical "magic trick" (practical + VFX), long takes of 10-40 s with cuts hidden in hand moves, dense foley, friendly VO. Screen content composited live on the display (corner-pin a `<video>` with `matrix3d`). The wordmark appears on the product and a hand wipes it off (`clip-path` following the hand, 0.7 s silence).

**C. Keynote segment** (apple_ios26_liquidglass_keynote). Presenter filmed like a documentary: real studio, daylight, two framings, talking shots 6-21 s; B-roll on every new noun; UI isolated on white, moving every 1-3 s inside long shots. Glass UI in HTML: `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(1.8); background: rgba(255,255,255,.18); border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.5)`, animated with `elastic.out(1,.6)`. Mix speech-led at ≈ −19 LUFS, music bed ≈ −30 LUFS under speech.

**D. Story / proof ad** (apple_detectives_8xzoom, apple_privacy_safari_trackers, apple_shotoniphone_rose_newtrick, apple_dearapple_watch).
- Noir comedy: 2.39:1 letterbox, the feature (zoom) is the joke, supers only in the last ≈9 %.
- Literal metaphor comedy: 4:3 symmetric wides, a folk song, a VFX "poof" (≈200 silver particles, `random(-300,300)`, 1.2 s, `power2.out`), word-built brand line.
- Music-video proof: cut at ≈1.0 s median, a BTS shot of the device in its rig every 4-6 shots (≈1 in 6), one small super at ≈90 % of runtime held 1.2 s.
- Testimonial collage: off-white canvas #ece9e6, clips as borderless windows 40-60 % W that slide in (`x:120`, 0.5 s, `power3.out`), a typed-letter device with a blinking blue cursor (#5b6fd6, blink 0.53 s, chars stagger 0.045 s), hold dramatic found footage 6-9 s, finale cut at 1.0-1.2 s.

**E. Vertical drama series** (apple_nido_ep1_vertical). 9:16, ASL 4.5 s, paced like TV: one dialogue line per shot. Cast cards on black with a display serif in pale yellow #f2eb9a (cap ≈ 5 % H, letter-spacing animates from 0.2 em in 0.8 s), an ornamental SVG title frame drawn on in 0.5 s, burned-in subtitles Inter 600 at 2.5vh, y ≈ 71 %, soft shadow. Triptych: three stacked panels revealed one by one. The device appears only as a prop and in the title card.

**F. Premium non-Apple product film†** (Bang & Olufsen, Sonos, Leica, Oura; 12 films). Same grammar (one idea per shot, small centred type, hard cuts, logo in silence) but warmer materials, fewer words and more patience. Shared rules: ASL 1.1-2.5 s in cut films; products never re-coloured; one accent colour per film; 4 of 12 put no text over the picture.
- F1 Craft film (beolab90-titan): real factory footage, raw material → precise parts → assembled object in an empty white hall, then a run of 0.5-1 s macros; flat 1.25-1.50 s quarters; wordmark at the start, sans product name + italic serif edition line at the end; a 0.44 s white flash marks the turn.
- F2 One-take system tour (sound-for-how-you-live): a CG cut-away house, the camera pushes into a room (5-14 s), skims the product, pulls back to the overview (4-8 s); one VO sentence per room; no supers. In HTML: scale a large still 1 → 3.2 over 2.2 s `power2.inOut`, cross-fade to a real room clip.
