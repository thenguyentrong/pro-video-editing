# Human demo warm
**Use it for:** feature ads where a real person has a small real problem and one product feature solves it, warm or funny story ads with dialogue, calm hero spots in one brand colour, vertical feature reels, and (as a variant) a loud colour-blocked event recap; † short brand documentaries where real people use assistive tech in their own words, and calm product announcements that show the product's view life-size in a real room. **Not for:** spec-heavy launch films (see `tech-launch-loud.md`), product-only films where the object is the hero (see `apple-precision.md`, `design-tech.md`), news or investigative documentary (see `documentary-evidence.md`).

## Signature
Sell the person, not the spec. A human moment (the photographer missing from the family photo, a fan who wants to watch and film at once, a parent's life told as a job interview, two phones stuck in gift boxes) is solved by one feature, told with warmth or a deadpan joke. The world has one soft brand colour, and the product colour is the background colour. Type is friendly: sentence case with a full stop, small, built one line at a time, swapped with a soft blur. Real people, real time, real UI. Silence is a tool for comedy and emotion. The close is always the same system: one short line, the name card split around the product, a coloured AI word arriving last, the logo.
† Round 3 (Meta, Microsoft) adds two registers with the same heart. (1) The brand documentary: a real person with a disability does small real tasks, tells it in their own interview voice, and the payoff is a life moment, not the product (a dance, an anthem at a medal ceremony); no text during the story. (2) The warm announcement: show what the wearer sees - life-size in the real room, through the lens, or in a phone inset - with tiny tracked type and dark warm product macros as bookends.

## Structure
**Calm hero spot (30 s)**
- 0-8 %: name card (product centred, brand word left, model right, small AI line under). The product turns and wipes the card.
- 8-15 %: promise: two short sentences, the second appended on the same line.
- 15-86 %: three features. Each = product macro + one sentence + one real proof shot (≈4-7 s per feature).
- 86-91 %: black, one short brand line.
- 91-97 %: product rises on black, name card again.
- 97-100 %: logo on black.

**Human feature demo (30 s)**
- 0-9 %: name card with a hand holding the product (or open on the scene).
- 9-24 %: the problem, shown plainly (POV over the shoulder, or the crowd all filming).
- 24-66 %: the trick in real time. One locked-off take (≈6.7 s) or a short story cut on song lyrics; the reaction close-up is the longest shot (4-5 s).
- 66-83 %: the result on the real UI; one VO line or a super built line by line.
- 83-97 %: end line → name card. 94-100 %: logo (+ store URL in older films).

**Narrative / comedy (≈2 min)**
- 0-12 %: setup with dialogue (person + AI assistant, or two products as characters).
- 12-13 %: a black beat or dead air after the first question; the music enters after it.
- 13-70 %: montage. Every line of dialogue gets a picture with a double meaning. Pace rises.
- ≈70-72 %: flash run of 0.17-0.33 s shots (≈2 s).
- 72-90 %: the climax held long (5-9.5 s shots). ≈2.5 s of silence just before the payoff line (≈85 %).
- 90-98 %: one super over a real product-in-the-room shot; the AI word fades in last.
- Last 2-4 %: logo card.

**Brand documentary (2-3 min) †**
- 0-5 %: silence, then one 5-6 s close-up (eyes, a scarred hand) or archive photos as small insets on black; the interview voice starts.
- 5-30 %: who they are and what they lost or need, in their own words.
- 25-45 %: the turn - three small real tasks done with the product (each: problem → call or gesture → answer → done), or a quiet 25-30 s black section with small text cards, then the music returns.
- 45-80 %: the method or more tasks; pace rises to 4-7 cuts / 10 s.
- 80-95 %: a life payoff that is not about the product (dance, a ceremony), source music takes over.
- Last 5 %: a series or brand line, logos; dissolve to white or black.

**Warm announcement (≈90 s) †**
- 0-25 %: dark warm product macros, one light line growing, the name lockup fades in small.
- 25-85 %: 3-4 everyday places with one person; in each, what they see appears life-size in the room (screen on the wall, players on the floor, an app grid in the iris).
- 85-100 %: product macros again, tracked two-line end card.

## Picture
- **Subjects:** real people first: faces and hands in close-up; the product as an object in the room (on a counter, on a desk). Product macros only in hero spots. Comedy can use two products as characters (they act through light: torch blinks, screen glow).
- **Camera moves:** handheld, slow push-ins in story; locked-off tripod wides for effects that need compositing; slow slides and rotations on product macros; POV over the shoulder for UI.
- **Lens / shot sizes:** longer lenses on faces with shallow focus; 85-100 mm, f/2-ish product macros on colour (recipe value); wides for the setting; thumb and screen macros for the tap.
- **Grade:** natural and filmic, soft contrast; warm tungsten interiors, cool exteriors; concerts in violet/magenta stage light with cool fill on faces. Hero spots: one colour world (coral-pink gradient ≈#E9B0AC centre; or warm off-white #F5F3EF). Close on black or off-white.
- **Texture:** real media textures make it feel true: camcorder video with a REC dot and date, pillarboxed vertical phone video, photo-memory cards with dates. Nothing stylised in demos.
- **Aspect:** 16:9 for ads and films; vertical versions shot natively 9:16 with faces filling the frame.
- † **Showing what the product sees:** a 9:16 phone inset (≈25 % W × 96 % H, rounded corners, call UI) on a blurred, darkened copy of the POV; a through-the-lens POV with a dark circular lens rim and a small frosted card floating in the world; or the virtual screen composited life-size behind the wearer's shoulder.
- † **Documentary grade:** low key (mean luma 0.17-0.32), warm daylight or tungsten, shallow focus, long lenses on faces; archive photos as small framed insets on black, never full frame; optional 2.39:1 letterbox.
- † **Warm product world:** dark bronze-brown light on black (≈#6B5440 → #1A140F), one rim light on the product; lifestyle scenes graded into the same warm low key.

## Type
- **Typeface:** Google Sans (proprietary). A variable "Google Sans Flex" may be on Google Fonts (check the licence before use); otherwise **DM Sans** or **Outfit**. Recap register: condensed caps **Anton / Bebas Neue**, extended heavy caps **Archivo Black at width 125** or **Unbounded**.
- **Weight:** Regular 400 for sentences and the name card (Medium for feature names). Recap: heavy.
- **Case:** sentence case WITH a full stop for every sentence. Product names as written. Recap cards in caps.
- **Tracking:** default for sentences; −0.02 em on condensed recap caps.
- **Size:** sentence cards cap 2.5-6 % H (hero ≈3.5 %, vertical ≈2.5 %, end line ≈6 %); feature names 6-10 % H; name card cap 6-9 % H; AI line ≈3 % H. Recap: condensed caps cap 28-100 % H, cropped by the frame; extended caps line 16-23 % H.
- **Placement:** centred. In 9:16, supers sit lower-middle (≈45-50 % H) and the name card stacks above the product. Name card: product centred at ≈45 % H, brand word left, model right.
- **Colour:** white on footage or colour; warm grey or coral-tinted (≈#E8A496) on black; dark grey #3C4043 on off-white. The AI word in a blue-to-pink gradient (#4285F4 → #C76BD3).
- **Animation in:** `opacity 0 → 1`, 0.4 s; in 9:16 also `y 12 → 0`. A second sentence or line is appended 0.6 s later in place.
- **Swap:** blur dissolve ≈0.3 s: outgoing `blur(0 → 10px), opacity 1 → 0`, incoming the reverse, overlap 0.15 s. Not a cut.
- **AI word:** slides in last, `x 40 → 0, blur(8px) → 0`, 0.5 s; the sparkle may shrink to a dot.
- † **Meta / Microsoft type:** Optimistic and Segoe are proprietary; free stand-ins **Figtree** / **Inter** (Meta) and **Selawik** (Microsoft's open Segoe fallback). Statements sentence case WITHOUT a full stop (cap ≈4.2 % H, soft blur-fade 0.5 s); labels in tracked caps (+0.15-0.25 em, cap 1.9-2.1 % H) beside the subject; documentary lower third bold caps ≈2.3 % H + role ≈1.5 % H at x 9 % W, y 72-73 % H; text cards ≈2 % H, lines fade in one by one (0.6 s each).
- **Recap motion:** product PNG replaces a letter (`y 100 % → 0`, 0.35 s, `power4.out`); repeated-word walls scroll (`y 0 → -50 %`, 1 s, linear); words drop in and rearrange (`y -40 → 0, rotate -6 → 0`, stagger 0.12 s).

## Transitions
- Mostly hard cuts (shot/reverse-shot on dialogue lines, cuts on lyric phrases).
- **Body wipe:** a person passes close to the lens and hides a cut (≈1 s).
- **Product-edge wipe:** the phone or a glass curve crosses the frame and reveals the next shot (0.5 s, `power3.inOut`).
- **Blur text swaps** instead of new cards.
- **Black beats:** a cut to black after a question (1.6 s).
- **Cut-away wide** held in silence as comic punctuation (≈3 s).
- **Recap:** the type is the transition (words scroll off, a word scales to full frame, the phone becomes a letter).
- No light flashes, no fly-throughs, no lens pushes.
- † Documentary and announcement films add: slow fades through black, a final dissolve to white, black beats of 1.0-1.4 s as act breaks (music kept under), one light burst into the first lens POV (the only flash in the set). Insets (phone, archive) cut in and out with no animation.

## Sound
- **Music:** hero spots = upbeat instrumental with one hit per feature every 3-5 s (+16 dB). Feature demos = a licensed song whose lyrics match the story, or a light song bed under VO. Narratives = warm piano growing into a full emotional track. Recap = dense percussive track.
- **VO:** deadpan, self-aware comic narrator; character dialogue; the AI assistant's voice as a character. Hero spots have no VO.
- **SFX density:** low. Diegetic sound from the scene (a crash, laughs, a line from a bedtime story) sits in the mix.
- **Mix:** music-led ads −14 to −16.5 LUFS integrated (target ≈ −15). Dialogue comedy −20 to −24 LUFS.
- **Silence:** 0.65-1.5 s dead air before punchlines; 1.6 s black after a question; 2-2.5 s silence before the emotional payoff, then a +45 dB return; 0.15-0.5 s micro-silence before each recap card, then a hit on the card. Music ends before the logo.
- † **Real voices:** documentary films run on the person's interview voice plus the live dialogue the product makes possible (a volunteer answering, a wake phrase); 220-290 words in 2-3 min, with pauses. Announcements and teasers are music only.
- † **Source music as payoff:** the record put on in the room or the anthem played at the ceremony becomes the soundtrack for the last 20 %.
- † **Levels:** brand documentaries −14.8 to −19.2 LUFS (the lower end is broadcast-documentary level; master ≈ −16 for social); announcements −14 to −16.7 LUFS.

## Do / Don't
**Do**
- Start from a human problem, solve it with ONE feature, end on the feeling.
- Show the feature working on real people in real time, with the real UI, one action, no clutter.
- Use one colour world; make the background the product colour.
- Write type as spoken sentences with full stops, 4-7 words, built one line at a time.
- Give the effect one long locked-off shot so people can watch it happen.
- Use humour: a deadpan line that names the problem plainly, e.g. "I'll stop saying photo now."
- Use silence for timing: before the joke, before the payoff.
- Close with the same system every time: line → split name card → AI word last → logo.
- † Show what the product sees inside the real scene (phone inset, lens POV, life-size composite) instead of a separate UI screen.
- † In a documentary, let the person speak; keep the screen free of text during the story.
- † Show each task fully - problem, action, answer, done - before moving on.
- † End on a life moment (dance, ceremony, friends), then a quiet brand card.
- † For an announcement, bookend the people with slow dark product macros and keep every shot over 1 s.

**Don't (what makes a copy look cheap)**
- Spec lists, numbers, or more than one feature per short ad.
- Stock-looking "happy people" with no real problem.
- Big bold headlines over lifestyle footage in calm spots (big type belongs only in the recap variant).
- Hard flashes, zoom transitions, glowing CGI tech: the opposite of this style.
- Music wall-to-wall in comedy: dead air is the joke.
- Fake UI or UI covered by graphics.
- Making the rival product the target. In this style the rival is a friend character.
- † Dramatising disability or loss with music stings or slow-motion pity shots; archive as small honest insets, real voices.
- † Product hero shots in the middle of a human story: the product is worn or used, not presented.
- † Big statements over a documentary; type stays at 2-4.5 % H.

## Recipe
1. **Script:** one problem, one feature, one feeling. For a narrative, write a two-voice dialogue (person + assistant) where each line has a double meaning. Record dialogue or VO first.
2. **Footage (own or licensed stock):** real people in real places; a tripod for any effect plate (plate A, plate B, clean plate); POV over-the-shoulder phone shots; one thumb/screen macro; product macros on a coloured background.
   - Stock search queries: "family photo park mother daughter", "father hugging daughter college move in", "dad reading bedtime story", "home video camcorder 2000s family", "concert crowd phones filming small venue", "singer close up stage purple light", "woman watching concert smiling", "kitchen night man talking to phone", "hand holding smartphone off-white background", "friends dinner laughing tungsten".
3. **Hero spot background:** `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 45%, #F0C3BF, #D98F88)` or off-white `#F5F3EF`; ≈14 shots in 30 s, ASL ≈2.0 s.
4. **Sentence card:** DM Sans 400, cap 3.5 % H (≈ `font-size:5vh`), white, centred; `opacity 0 → 1` 0.4 s; append sentence 2 after 0.6 s; hold 1.5-2 s; blur swap 0.3 s, overlap 0.15 s.
5. **Effect shot (ghost composite):** plate A `opacity 1 → 0.4` over 1.5 s `power1.inOut`, plate B fades in, then A back to 1. Hide a cut with a body wipe (someone walks ≈0.5 m from the lens, 1 s).
6. **Pace:** hero ≈2.1 s flat; demo 2.2-3.8 s with one 5-7 s locked-off take; narrative montage ≈1.5 s, a 2 s flash run (0.2-0.3 s shots), then 4-6 s holds.
7. **Archive overlays:** camcorder REC dot + monospace date bottom-right (`font: 500 2.5vh 'VT323'`); vertical phone clips pillarboxed on black; photo cards with dates.
8. **Vertical super:** DM Sans 400, cap 2.5 % H, white, `text-shadow: 0 1px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.4)`; line 1 then line 2 0.6 s later, each `opacity 0 → 1, y 12 → 0`, 0.4 s; put it on the longest reaction shot.
9. **Close:** black 1.2-1.5 s with the brand line (cap ≈6 % H, #E8A496 on black) → name card 2-2.5 s (DM Sans 400, cap 6-9 % H, split left/right of the product) with the AI line `background: linear-gradient(90deg,#4285F4,#C76BD3); -webkit-background-clip:text; color:transparent`, sliding in `x 40 → 0, blur(8px) → 0` 0.5 s → logo 0.5-2.5 s.
10. **Mix:** ads at ≈ −15 LUFS (−14 to −16.5); dialogue comedy −20 to −24 LUFS; one hit per feature in hero spots; 2-2.5 s silence before the payoff line in narratives; music out before the logo.
11. † **Brand documentary:** record a long interview first and cut 1-2 sentences per task; film 3 tasks (wide → hands → face → product view), ASL 2.3-3.2 s, never more than 6-7 cuts / 10 s. Phone inset: `left:50%; top:2%; width:25%; height:96%; transform:translateX(-50%); border-radius:18px` over the same clip with `filter: blur(18px) brightness(.7)`. Archive photos `width:64%` centred on black. Lower third `700 2.3vh Selawik` uppercase + role `400 1.5vh`, `left:9%; top:72%`. Stock: "blind woman cane garden path", "hands record player vinyl", "veteran interview window light", "adaptive controller buttons hands", "wheelchair athletes medal ceremony".
12. † **Warm announcement:** product macros on `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 40%, #6B5440, #1A140F)`; one actor in 3-4 warm low-key places; virtual screen = clip in a div `border-radius:2%/6%`, `box-shadow:0 0 120px rgba(255,200,150,.25)`, masked behind the shoulder; labels `500 2.1vh Inter; letter-spacing:.15em; text-transform:uppercase`; ASL ≈3 s, no shot under 1.2 s; music only ≈ −16.5 LUFS.
13. † **Statement teaser:** lens POV = clip in a circle mask with `radial-gradient(circle, transparent 52%, rgba(0,0,0,.85) 70%)` rim and a frosted card `width:22%; border-radius:18px; backdrop-filter:blur(14px)`; statements `500 6vh/1.2 Figtree` (cap ≈4.2 % H) with `blur(8px) → 0` 0.5 s; slow first 10 s, then 0.3-1 s shots; two 1-1.4 s black beats with one statement each.

## Variants
**1. Calm hero spot** (pixel11pro-meet). The most Apple-like register, but in colour: one colour world, shallow-focus product macros, one sentence per card, one real proof per feature, black close. ASL ≈2.1 s flat, music only, −16 LUFS.

**2. Human feature demo** (pixel9-add-me, starstruck-vertical). One problem, one feature, real people in real time. Either a long locked-off effect take with a deadpan VO (ASL 3.75 s, 62 % of shots over 3 s), or a tiny story cut on song lyrics in 9:16 with stage lighting (ASL 2.15 s). Supers build line by line.

**3. Narrative / comedy** (dream-job-sb2025, bpf-thats-a-wrap). Dialogue is the spine; the montage illustrates each line. Montage accelerates (down to 0.17 s flash cuts), then holds 5-9.5 s for the payoff. Type only at the very end. For a product-as-character comedy on a budget: two phones on mini sets (shoe boxes, tissue paper), macro lens, one practical light each, heavy vignette `radial-gradient(transparent 40%, rgba(0,0,0,.75))`, shot/reverse-shot on each line, a silent 3 s cut-away wide after a big line, product drop onto off-white (`y -60 % → 0`, 0.6 s, subtle bounce).

**4. Editorial kinetic recap** (mbg26-full-reveal). Once a year the style flips loud: flat colour cards (coral #FD6A58, maroon ≈#4A0A14, periwinkle #6F80FF, navy #050943, blush #ECCCC8, sage #879665, lime, lilac), ONE giant word per card (condensed caps `font-size:60-100vh; line-height:.82-.85; letter-spacing:-0.02em`, cropped; or extended caps 18-22 % H), the product replaces a letter, product renders on a card of their own colour, event-stage reactions as the human layer. Cut type cards at 0.25-0.6 s on beats, product 1.2-2 s, reactions and demos 3-5 s; drop the music 0.2 s before each new section card; mix −16 LUFS; logo on black in silence.

**5. Brand documentary †** (be-my-eyes-every-step, anthem-for-all). A real person, their own interview voice, three small real tasks or one making-of (eye-gaze and adaptive buttons in a studio), then a life payoff with source music. No text during the story (or only lower thirds and a quiet 25-30 s black text section as the turn). ASL 2.5-3.2 s, 2-10 % of shots under 1 s; 3.3 s silence at the start or a 2.9 s near-silence before the music returns; −14.8 to −19.2 LUFS. Product shown only in use: a phone inset of what the glasses see, hands on the controller.

**6. Warm announcement †** (vr-glasses-intro). Apple-like dark bronze product macros as bookends (ASL 4.3 s in the first and last quarter), one person in 3-4 everyday places in between (≈2-2.4 s), the virtual content life-size in the real room, tracked caps labels ≈2 % H, a two-line tracked end card; music only, −16.7 LUFS.

**7. Statement teaser †** (ray-ban-display-intro). A human desire as the first line on black, then the product, then through-the-lens POV and a fast city montage (0.3-1 s shots, ASL 1.26 s) split by two black beats with one sentence-case statement each (cap ≈4.2 % H, no full stop); ends on the product floating in sky colour; music only, −14 LUFS.
