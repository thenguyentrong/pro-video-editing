# Creator YouTube
**Use it for:** a 10-60 min YouTube film led by one creator or a small team: a tech review, a vlog, a challenge/entertainment video, an animated explainer, a science film with hosts. Also for the first 30-60 s of any long video that must hold a viewer who can click away. **Not for:** a brand launch film (use `apple-precision.md` or `tech-launch-loud.md`), a newsroom explainer with reporters and citations (use `editorial-explainer.md`), a 6-60 s vertical clip (use `social-vertical.md`).

## Signature
The first frame is already the video: no intro, no "hi", no title card, no sponsor read in the first 30 s (branding measured: MKBHD's 2.4 s logo sting, a 5.4 s labelled own-merch spot before one Kurzgesagt story, a handwritten sponsor note at 22-29 s in one Neistat vlog). The promise is shown before it is explained, and the viewer gets one reason to stay every 20-60 s. Under that common rule the five houses sit at very different speeds: MrBeast cuts every 1.7-2.6 s and never lets the frame rest; Veritasium mixes 1-3 s live-action proof with uncut 15-40 s animations; Neistat runs 3-6.5 s with long talk takes; MKBHD holds 5-8 s on a calm talking head; Kurzgesagt hard-cuts only every 10-14 s but changes the idea every 3-5 s with camera moves. In every channel the cut follows the voice, not the music beat (cut-on-onset at or near chance in all 15 films). Type is rare except at MrBeast, where a game-state number is always on screen.

After the hook (% of a 10-15 min runtime; 30-60 min films scale the middle):
1. **Hook, 0-30 s (3-5 %).** One promise shown, not explained.
2. **Premise and rules, 30-60 s (5-8 %).** Say the question, the test, the prize or the plan out loud. Re-hook here (MrBeast scale promise at 30-50 s; Kurzgesagt chapter title at 45-58 s).
3. **First act, 1-5 min (8-35 %).** One evidence or action loop repeated: show, react, explain. Something changes every 50-100 s (MrBeast new rule or standings at ≈100, 204, 287 s; Veritasium a new animation every 60-90 s; MKBHD a chapter card).
4. **Mid-roll sponsor, from ≈4 min (≥29 %).** Kurzgesagt at 4:08 with a joke segue; Veritasium at ≈14-16 min or at the end. Never inside the hook.
5. **Long proof (not measured past 5 min).** The house's long shot type: MKBHD verdict take 17-39 s; Veritasium mechanism animation 15-40 s; Kurzgesagt world journey 25-55 s.
6. **Payoff and short end.** Pay the open loop from the hook.

## Picture
- **MKBHD:** 2:1 frame inside 16:9. Locked-off centred talking head, shallow focus, one coloured set per film. Products in hands on neutral surfaces with one accent colour; smooth slow moves; macro; physical tests (scale, wear). Clean bright grade, no grain.
- **Neistat:** wide and fisheye for almost everything, host at arm's length and moving; 360 camera for overhead follows; drone at sunrise/dusk; telephoto only for signs. Locked-off self-shot wides open the film. Natural saturated grade, night left dark.
- **MrBeast:** bright, saturated, high-key, wide lens on a real built set or location. Worlds separated by colour: day, purple night, black-and-white past, one colour per team. Triptych split screens for comparisons. CG only for game info (counters, routes, standings).
- **Kurzgesagt:** all drawn. Deep indigo ground (#0E0133, #100150, #0A022E), violet mid-tones (#3405B1), 2-3 glowing accents (cyan #12F0FC, magenta #C96AB2, orange #F2B649, mint #2DF7CF, coral skin #EB6F47). Flat shapes, no outlines, glow on light sources, star specks as texture. A new chapter can switch the whole palette.
- **Veritasium:** hosts on lived-in sets (living room, bookshelf, garden), medium close-up, natural light; handheld field footage with crew in frame; macro and microscope. Each kind of evidence gets its own look: photoreal CG, 2D cel characters, hand-drawn comic, flat diagrams (cream #FEF2C8 / teal #083846), navy graph #040811 with one pink curve ≈#E0407A.
- **All:** third-party footage keeps its own look and gets a small credit or tag.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts almost everywhere, in all five houses.
- MKBHD: one iris (circle) open after the sting; wide/tight angle cuts on sentence ends.
- Neistat: whip-blur and hand-over-lens cuts (1-3 per 5 min); 0.5-0.8 s dips to black and a windshield timelapse (6 cuts of 0.4-1.8 s) for time passing; sign montage at 0.67-0.8 s per shot.
- MrBeast: speed-ramp whips with motion blur, white glow flashes (≈10 per 5 min in the wish film), RGB-split flash frames, sliding panels, face bursts of 0.10-0.43 s.
- Kurzgesagt: inside a world there are no cuts: zoom-throughs, pull-outs into a bubble, morphs, whip pans with blur, swirl wipes. Hard cut only between worlds.
- Veritasium: countdown jump cuts (0.54 s), cut to black to hide a result, glitch tears, one white flash, one split wipe.

## Sound
- **Voice first, cut on speech.** Every house cuts on sentence ends and reactions, not on music beats.
- **Pick the bed by house:** none under talk (MKBHD); music only under movement and montage (Neistat); music + SFX under everything (MrBeast); continuous score ≈9-10 dB under (Kurzgesagt); continuous bed ≈16-17 dB under (Veritasium).
- **Silence as a device:** MrBeast 0.3-0.7 s right before each reveal, then +26 to +47 dB; Veritasium a 1-2.5 s dip at 15-18 s that ends the cold open; Kurzgesagt 0.75 s before a chapter title; Neistat 2 s digital silence under a time card; MKBHD 0.8-0.9 s around borrowed clips that enter at full level.
- **SFX density:** high at Kurzgesagt (pop on every label, whoosh on every move) and MrBeast; low at MKBHD and Neistat.
- **Loudness:** channels range from -8.0 (MrBeast) to -20.0 LUFS (MKBHD). Deliver at about -14 LUFS / -1 dBTP: YouTube turns louder uploads down, and MrBeast-level masters showed overs above 0 dBTP on the stream.

## Do / Don't
- Do start the story on frame 1; first spoken line by 0-5 s.
- Do show the promise as a picture or object first (prize prop, product in hand, premise action, inciting moment).
- Do give the viewer a new reason to stay every 20-60 s in the hook and every 50-100 s after it.
- Do keep one long shot type per house and protect it from cuts (verdict take, mechanism animation, world journey).
- Do label people once and credit every borrowed clip.
- Don't open with a logo montage, a channel intro, "hi guys", a subscribe ask or a sponsor read (a sponsor may appear as a physical prop or note, Neistat style, and be explained later).
- Don't explain the premise before you have shown it (Neistat waits until 43.5-52 s).
- Don't caption every word in 16:9; none of the 15 films does.
- Don't cut to the beat under talk; don't add hits under every sentence.
- Don't fake the event with CG (MrBeast and Veritasium use CG for information or clearly marked recreation only).

## Recipe
A 10-12 min creator film in Hyperframes (1920x1080, 25 fps): own footage or licensed stock + HTML/GSAP graphics.
1. **Choose the house** (Variants) and copy its row of the hook table into a 30 s timing grid. Write one promise and one withheld answer.
2. **Record voice or talk takes first.** Mark sentence ends; these are the cut points.
3. **Shoot or source.** Stock queries (Pexels, Pixabay, licensed only): `hands unboxing smartphone desk overhead`, `product macro shallow focus turntable`, `city sunrise drone skyline`, `skateboard follow shot street`, `windshield timelapse night`, `crowd cheering wide angle`, `drone pull back landscape`, `laboratory microscope close up`, `fiber optic cable light close up`. Search for what is in the picture.
4. **Hook (0-30 s).** Voice on frame 1 (or the MKBHD sting `0-2.4 s` + iris `clip-path: circle(0% → 75%)`, 0.5 s `power2.out`). Hold the opening shot 4-11 s unless the house is MrBeast or a stunt (then first fast section at 4-10 s).
5. **Re-hook (30-60 s).** Say the premise or question; add a chapter card or start the game-state counter.
6. **Body.** Cut at the house ASL; one change every 50-100 s; one protected long shot per 2-3 min.
7. **Graphics.** Only the house's allowed set (see Type). Pop-ons 0.25 s `back.out(2.5)`, hard off.
8. **Mix.** Bed policy by house; the house's silence device before reveals or chapters; master `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1:LRA=7`.
9. **Check.** Voice by 5 s, no logo montage or sponsor read before 30 s, ASL inside the house range, every borrowed clip credited.

## Variants
- **Tech review (MKBHD).** Calm and slow: ASL 5.8-7.9 s, median 4.9-6.1 s, at most 5 cuts per 10 s. 2.4 s logo sting + iris, product named in sentence one, stakes by 25-30 s (rival montage of 5 shots × 1.3-2.7 s, a twist promise, or the price). Proof = objects and primary sources (scale, macro, the maker's page or keynote), not motion graphics. Verdict in one 17-39 s talking-head take. No bed under talk; -18 to -20 LUFS, LRA ≈4 LU.
- **Vlog (Neistat).** ASL 3.2-6.5 s, median 2.6-4.5 s. Self-shot wide of the premise (4-11 s), first line by 5 s, 10-20 s music montage with no talk, title or sponsor card at 22-29 s, premise said only at 44-52 s. Walking talk 2-12 s per shot, sit-down talk 15-35 s with no music. Physical titles; Helvetica-style card when needed. Loud: -13 to -14 LUFS.
- **Retention-max entertainment (MrBeast).** ASL 1.7-2.6 s, median 1.4-2.1 s, 23-34 shots per minute, no slow middle. Premise in sentence one, prize with a number by 5-19 s, cast and scale in one image by 15 s, clock on screen by 21-29 s and to the end. Something changes every 50-100 s. Pop-on comic and chrome type, bed under everything, 0.3-0.7 s silence before each reveal. Masters at -8 to -12 LUFS; deliver at -14.
- **Animated explainer (Kurzgesagt).** All drawn, one glowing flat world per chapter on a deep indigo ground. Hard cut every 10-14 s (median 5.8-9.4 s) but a new camera target every 3-5 s. Narration within 0.5 s, character gag at 15-20 s, question at 24-43 s, chapter title at 45-58 s. Sparse type, dense small SFX, score ≈9-10 dB under the voice, -15 LUFS with ≈2 LU range.
- **Science host-led (Veritasium).** Cold open in a heightened register (CG, comic or stunt) with voice at 0.0 s, a 1-2.5 s dip at 15-18 s, real world with a lower third at 17-32 s, test stated at 38-60 s, result withheld. Live action at 1-3 s per shot (ASL 3.0-5.3 s, median 1.8-3.1 s), each mechanism one uncut animation of 15-40 s. One type system per film; serif lower thirds without a box. -13 to -16 LUFS, bed ≈16 dB under, short drops at section breaks.
