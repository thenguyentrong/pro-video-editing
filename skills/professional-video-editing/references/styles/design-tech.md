# Design tech
**Use it for:** launch films and teasers for design-led hardware (phones, audio, instruments, appliances, cars), product films that sell form, material and engineering more than features, mood films where the product arrives late in a built world, silent explainers with technical callouts. **Not for:** feature-heavy launches with a narrator on every shot (see `tech-launch-loud.md`), warm human problem/solution ads (see `human-demo-warm.md`), Apple-style card/product spots (see `apple-precision.md`), anything that needs long text on screen.

## Signature
The product is a designed object, and the film behaves like its design. Each film commits to ONE idea and ONE world (a white museum, black on black, a test lab with fake backdrops, a red flower planet, an x-ray of the machine) and keeps it in every frame. The palette is neutral or monochrome with one accent that comes from the product (a red dot, a green LED, a laser, a tail-light bar). Words are few, small and set in a strict system (a dot-matrix name, a lowercase line, a technical HUD label, a single wordmark); often there is no text at all until the end. Cuts are calm and even (ASL mostly 2-4 s, up to 7 s for CG one-takes, almost nothing under 1 s), the product is often withheld until 55-75 % of the runtime, the last shot is the longest, and silence marks the key moment.

## Structure
General shape (adjust per family, see Variants):
- 0-3 %: optional brand card on black (Nothing: 0.6-1 s) or one small lowercase line on black (TE).
- 0-10 %: hook = one strong image: a top-down, a detail in the dark, a light line, a splash hit, a top-down drone.
- 10-60 %: build the world or the mechanism. Macros, tableaux, details, 1.5-3 s each (renders 2.5-4 s). The product is shown only in parts.
- 55-75 %: the full product, frontal and centred, held 4-7 s. Often the longest shot of the film.
- 70-90 %: release or payoff: dance, drive-off, top-down formation, result shot.
- Last 7-21 %: the end system. Nothing: 3-4 black cards of 1-2.2 s with hard cuts. TE: product logo → pictogram + 2 legal lines → fade to black. Dyson: 1-2 cards of 2-3 s, product right, claim left. Polestar: a long empty composed wide, or a flash into a full-frame brand-colour card.
- Dyson is the exception: the mechanism is the story from second 1 (product visible at once, the inside revealed by x-ray and zoom-through).

## Picture
- **Subjects:** product macros on textured surfaces; photoreal renders with exploded views and x-ray shells; art-directed sets that are a metaphor (fake photo backdrops in a lab, a hall of fans for noise, a white box on a rock island); fashion casting with flat, symmetric staging; landscapes and aerials for cars. People never demo the product; in TE films nobody touches it.
- **Camera moves:** locked or very slow push (`scale 1.0 → 1.04`); slow drifts across macros; slow CG orbits and motion-control paths (6-16 s in one move); drone top-downs and pull-backs (up to 16 s); handheld only for a release moment (dance, drive).
- **Lens / shot sizes:** macro with very shallow focus, framed diagonally (product rotated 20-30°); ONE frontal symmetric hero; tableau wides (≈24-28 mm look) + long-lens close-ups; one-point perspective for the final road or horizon.
- **Grade (measured medians):** saturation very low, 1-25 % (TE product films 1.3-1.6 %, white teaser 3 %, Headphone 20 %, premiere 25 %); exceptions: warm story 48 %, red world 41 %, CMF 79 %. Temperature as R-B: cool −20 to −24 (lab, Nordic sea), neutral −2 to +2 (renders, desert, Dyson studio), warm +13 to +18 (restaurant, red flowers). Luma from 6/255 (black on black) to 218-222 (white seamless). Contrast crisp in renders (p95-p5 146-171), soft in moody footage.
- **Accent colour:** exactly one, from the product world: Nothing red ≈#d71921, Polestar red ≈#d12f36, laser green ≈#39ff6a, one green LED, a pale blue shirt, one saturated set colour (CMF ≈#e6350d).
- **Texture:** clean digital. No grain, no letterbox (except the 2.35:1 concept film). Selective colour is allowed (black-and-white world, product in colour).
- **Aspect:** 16:9.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts almost everywhere (Phone (3): 17 of 17; Headphone (1): 25 of 25; Polestar 4: 40 of 40, no effects).
- **Object-continuity morphs inside renders:** lens stack → module → circuit board → lock screen (Phone (3a): ≈12 soft changes in 113 s).
- **X-ray dissolves:** the shell fades to glass to show the inside (≈1-1.2 s). **Zoom-through** into particles and a whoosh back out (Dyson).
- **Material and shape rhymes across hard cuts:** chrome drop → chrome star → chrome lattice → beetle on paint → car (Polestar).
- **Physical reveals:** a cover blown off by wind, a box that opens, light that rises on the floor.
- **Reveal by framing:** a tight crop of a photo backdrop, then a hard cut to the wide that shows it is a set.
- Once per film at most: a fade through black, or a white flash + light-leak flare into the brand card.
- CMF only: iris/portal mask with cuts inside the circle, 3 colour-swap cuts of 0.28 s, 0.25 s whip pans, zoom-through into the chip.
- No glitch effects, no flashes as habit, no decorative wipes.

## Sound
- **Music:** one music bed that matches the world: levelled electronic (Nothing render tour holds −11 to −13 LUFS-M for 75 s), a pop song whose lyrics are the feature list (CMF), a punchy stutter track, a single ambient swell, a themed score (TE), cinematic builds with a loud plateau at the reveal (Polestar).
- **VO:** none in most films. Exceptions: Dyson TV spots (calm, factual VO from first to last second, numbers instead of adjectives, one fact per 3-4 s), witness voices as a documentary layer (Polestar concept film), diegetic phone-call dialogue (Nothing Ear (3)).
- **SFX density:** low to medium; designed sound carries the feature (noise that builds and then switches off; a splash hit on the first action).
- **Mix:** web/social −12 to −16 LUFS integrated; quiet moods −18 to −21 LUFS; broadcast TV with VO −23 LUFS. Do not master everything loud; master to the mood.
- **Silence:** at the feature moment (0.3-1 s near-silence or a hard gate of the noise), 1-1.3 s before the brand hit, a 4-6 s dip before the hero reveal. Logo cards in silence or on a decaying tail.

## Do / Don't
**Do**
- One idea per film, told visually. Let the title of the video do the explaining.
- One world, one palette, one accent colour from the product.
- A strict type system with fixed jobs (name / statement / spec), small and lowercase where the brand is lowercase.
- Withhold the full product; macros first, the whole object late, held 4-7 s.
- Calm, even editing: ≈2 s pulse for live action, ≈3 s for renders, 5-16 s for one-take camera moves.
- Show engineering as beauty: exploded views, x-ray shells, thin leader lines.
- End on a long, composed, often empty frame or tiny cards. No CTA.

**Don't (what makes a copy look cheap)**
- Big headlines over picture, bold claims, exclamation marks, many specs at once.
- Several accent colours, saturated grading on the whole frame (except a deliberate CMF-style colour set).
- Fast cutting under 1 s without a reason; cutting every beat.
- Glitch, RGB split, flash frames, zoom transitions as decoration.
- Hands demoing buttons, people smiling at camera holding the product.
- A loud master for a quiet mood, or music to the last frame under the logo.
- Mixing type systems from different brands in one film.

## Recipe
1. **Pick one world and one accent:** white museum (`#f2f2f0` + `radial-gradient(ellipse at center,#f4f4f2 55%,#d9d9d6 100%)`), black on black (`#000` + strip-light sweep), light/dark studio (`linear-gradient(180deg,#eef1f3,#c7d0d7)` / `linear-gradient(180deg,#14181c,#3b444c)`), a metaphor set, or a landscape. Accent token, e.g. `--accent:#d71921`.
2. **Footage (own or licensed stock):** 5-8 product macros (2-3.5 s each), one frontal hero (6-7 s), all on the same surface; or 20-30 world shots + 6-8 material macros + 8-10 product shots.
   - Stock search queries: "white seamless product macro shallow focus", "black product strip light dark studio", "concrete warehouse industrial fans", "vintage test equipment VU meter", "fashion editorial lab white coats", "red flower field aerial dusk", "rocky island archipelago drone top down", "liquid metal chrome macro", "dust particles light beam macro", "desert road dusk car tracking", "dinner table candlelight faces close up".
3. **Grade (CSS on video or in the grader):** cool world `R-B ≈ −20`, saturation ≈20-25 %, median luma 70-110; keep the accent saturated (HSL qualifier, or a masked colour layer). Selective colour in HTML: bottom video `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.05)`, top = same video with a product `mask-image`.
4. **Edit pulse:** live action 1.6-2.4 s per shot (tension loop down to 0.6 s for 8-10 s, then hold 2 s at the turn); renders 2.5-4 s; reveal the full product at 55-75 % and hold 4-7 s; last shot 4-16 s.
5. **Nothing-style cards:** `background:#000`; `tl.set(el,{autoAlpha:1},t).set(el,{autoAlpha:0},t+hold)` (hard cuts, no tweens). Sequence: name (Doto 3.6-3.8vh, 2-2.2 s) → claim (serif 300, 5vh, 1.4-1.6 s) → brand (1.2 s) → URL (1.2 s). Decode: per letter 3-5 random glyphs from `!#%&*+=?/0123456789abcdef` at 40 ms steps, stagger 0.05 s, time-based so it is seek-safe.
6. **Flank label shot:** product centred at 60-75 % H; two Doto spans at `left:5%` and right-aligned at 95 %, `top:50%`, 3.2-3.6vh; decode in, hold 2.5 s.
7. **Spec callout / HUD:** ring dot `scale 0 → 1` 0.2 s → 1-1.5 px line (`stroke-dasharray:1 3` for dotted, `strokeDashoffset` 0.5-0.6 s `power1.out`/`power2.out`) → header type-on 0.04 s/char with a blinking `_` (`repeat:-1, yoyo:true, duration:.35`) → sub-lines stagger 0.15 s → hold 2-3 s → fade 0.3 s.
8. **X-ray and zoom-through:** internals plate `clipPath: inset(0 100% 0 0) → inset(0 0 0 0)` 1.2 s `power2.inOut` while the shell goes to `opacity .35-.4`; zoom-through = macro layer `scale 1 → 6` 0.6 s `power3.in` + blur 0 → 8 px, next layer `scale 0.6 → 1` `power3.out`.
9. **Light reveal:** `linear-gradient(100deg,transparent 40%,rgba(255,255,255,.18) 50%,transparent 60%)`, `mix-blend-mode:screen`, `backgroundPosition -100% → 200%` over 3 s; floor glow `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 100%,#9a9a9a 0%,#000 60%)` `opacity 0 → 1` over 4 s.
10. **Brand moment:** tiny cards (step 5), or a neon logo (SVG stroke #fff, `drop-shadow(0 0 6px #fff) drop-shadow(0 0 18px rgba(255,255,255,.6))`, draw 0.8 s, flicker 1/0.6/1), or a 0.6 s white flash (#f4f8f8) + drifting radial flares 3 s into a full-frame brand colour with the wordmark at `left:15%`.
11. **Sound:** no VO (unless a broadcast explainer); one bed at a steady level; 0.3-1.3 s silence at the feature or before the brand hit; logo cards silent. Master −12 to −16 LUFS for web, −18 to −21 for ambient moods, −23 LUFS for broadcast.

## Variants
### Nothing
- **Feature-as-plot spot** (phone3-come-to-play): the feature is the story device (a pixel game on the back of the phone at dinner). Zero text, zero logo. A-B-A-C loop (feature macro → face → feature → face), shots shrink 1.2 → 0.56 s, 0.4 s silence before the payoff, then a hard cut from warm live action (R-B ≈ +18, luma ≈56) to a cold product render for the last 5 s.
- **Typographic product tour** (phone3a-power-in-perspective): renders on light and dark studio gradients (light/dark is the concept and the colour script), flank labels, serif statement cards on black, mono specs with dotted leaders, ASL 3.1 s in each of the first three quarters, morphs inside the render.
- **Editorial concept film** (headphone1-control-your-sound, ear3-cut-through-the-noise): an art-directed metaphor set, fashion casting, tableau wides, ≈2 s metronome cut (Headphone 1.12-4.56 s, only 4 % under 1 s), muted grade. Sound tells the feature: noise builds +10 dB over 15-20 s and is gated to silence for ≈1 s twice; or music dips 10-15 dB around the finger on the control. Brand card at 0 s and dot cards at the end.
- **One-shot deadpan teaser** (ear2-black-teaser): one locked-off 10 s take on white seamless (luma ≈222, saturation 3 %), subject small (10-20 % of width), one physical gag with the product, then two 2 s dot cards. No cut in the action.

### CMF by Nothing
- **Pop spot** (cmf-phone2pro-wonderful-by-design): the loud sibling. One saturated set colour on walls, floor and wardrobe, a white neon line, lyric captions in big dot caps (9-11 % H) built word by word on the sung words, mono spec labels top-left (2.4vh caps, tracking 0.15 em, stepped type-on `steps(20)` 0.5 s), a logo bug top-right all film, graphic transitions (portal mask `circle(0%) → circle(14%)` 0.25 s, whip `x 0 → -120vw` + `blur(18px)` 0.25 s).

### teenage engineering
- **Museum-white type teaser** (op1-field-introducing): off-white seamless, diagonal macros, five lowercase lines where only the last word swaps in place, one frontal hero 6.6 s, punchy track at −10.7 LUFS with 0.2 s stutter gaps before 2-3 cuts.
- **Black-on-black light reveal** (op-xy-introducing): black product on black (luma ≈6), a raking strip light finds edges and keys, one green LED is the only colour, the full product only at 63 % of the runtime, no words until the logo, one ambient swell peaking at ≈55 % (−18 LUFS).
- **Art-house world film** (ep1320-medieval-introducing): 20-25 frontal tableaux of a strange ritual, faded teal-grey grade (saturation ≈23 %, lifted blacks) with fire-orange accents, cut on music hits (40 % vs 18 %), product on an altar at 73 % held 6.6 s, then a 6.6 s top-down formation, themed blackletter title, −21 LUFS.

### Dyson
- **VO-led engineering spot** (v15-detect-laser, airstrait-new-way-to-straighten): a calm narrator with numbers, CG one-takes of 8-16 s (floor skim → laser on → zoom through dust into a microscopic world → whoosh out; x-ray airflow in cyan with heat in magenta), nothing under 1 s, cuts on sentence ends. Beauty variant: live action in black-and-white with the product kept in colour. End card: light background, product right third, 2-3 line claim left at x 13-15 %, wordmark under it. Broadcast master −23 LUFS.
- **Silent HUD explainer** (pencilwash-slimmest-design): no voice, six long CG moves (3.7-12.9 s) on a light grey floor, HUD callouts drawn in every 2-3 s, x-ray shells, ends on the product alone, small, centred, 3-4 s. One splash hit at ≈1 s; social master −15.8 LUFS.

### Polestar
- **Material-rhyme mood film** (polestar4-performance-coupe): 25-30 nature macros and aerials in a red-and-grey palette, liquid chrome forms, every cut shares a shape or material with the next, full car at 65 % held 4.7 s frontal, a neon logo as the only graphic, last shot an empty road to the horizon (4.7 s).
- **Architecture reveal** (polestar3-world-premiere): a white minimalist box on a rock island, top-down drone open, beat-cut details 1.3-2.8 s (63 % on onsets), frontal symmetric frame-within-frame, a 16 s drone pull-back, then 1.3 s silence under a white flash into a full-frame red card with a small wordmark.
- **Genre concept** (polestar4-sightings): a "sighting" story in 2.35:1 scope, the product covered in gold foil (its light signature glows through), wind removes the cover, documentary witness voices over picture from ≈20 % of the runtime, faster third quarter (ASL 1.36 s), ends on a winding-road aerial with no text.
