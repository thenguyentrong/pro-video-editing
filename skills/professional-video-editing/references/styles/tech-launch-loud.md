# Tech launch loud
**Use it for:** big launch films for consumer tech with many features, chaptered product introductions with a narrator, short AI or software feature spots that show prompt → tap → result, TV/display spec films where invisible tech must become visible, loud social teasers, drone/camera feature-run films that prove each spec with the product's own footage, dark hardware reveal trailers, engineering "how it's made" launch films, fast athlete manifestos for a phone. **Not for:** calm premium spots with few words (see `apple-precision.md`), human stories where the person is the hero (see `human-demo-warm.md`), design-led brands that sell restraint (see `design-tech.md`), documentaries.

## Signature
Show more, and say it out loud. A calm narrator explains every feature from second 2 to the end card. The film is built in chapters: each opens with a CGI "hero title" moment (a big bold headline lit and tilted inside a 3-D scene, held 3-5 s on one continuous push), then proves the claim with bright lifestyle shots and over-the-shoulder UI on the real device. The frame is always full (only 4-22 % of frames mostly empty), colours are saturated, 30-40 % of shot changes are visible transitions (fly into a lens, light slits, whips, page curls), and the master is loud (−10 to −12.6 LUFS), 4-6 LU louder than the Apple-style films. Deliberate 1-1.5 s drops before each chapter title make the next hit land.
† Round 3 widened the style: the same "show every claim" logic also works without CGI hero titles. DJI proves each spec with the product's own postcard footage and one fixed label system; Sony gives each claim one disciplined card (bold keyword or number + small tracked text) inside a cinematic grade; PlayStation and Surface build a dark, patient tease before one big reveal; OnePlus and Copilot run 0.75-0.8 s montages. What all share: many claims, each made visible, a fixed type system, a product-or-logo end on black. Loudness is NOT shared: −9.2 to −21.5 LUFS.

## Structure
**Chapter launch film (≈3.5 min)**
- 0-4.5 %: cold open in the dark. A light line grows along the product edge, a sparkle bursts, the wordmark is embossed in the floor. VO starts at ≈2 s.
- 4.5-13 %: promise montage. 3-4 hero titles in a row, then the name card with the name split around the product.
- 13-91 %: 5-7 chapters of 25-45 s each (12-21 % of runtime each): performance, display, cameras, AI, security. Each chapter = one CGI hero title shot (3-5 s) + 5-10 proof shots at 1.6-2.5 s (lifestyle first, then over-the-shoulder UI). A 1-1.5 s drop before each chapter title.
- 91-94 %: short security beat (vault, shield).
- 92-97 %: product hero + 3-line lockup, 8-9 s on screen, the lockup itself ≈4 s.
- Last ≈0.5-3 %: wordmark on black ≈1 s, then black.
- Two-product films: at ≈45-53 % the tone and palette flip (dark engineering → white pastel playful), joined by a shape-match reveal gag and a 0.85 s silence, then a hard hit.

**AI demo spot (30 s)**
- 0-7 %: product name super over a lifestyle scene.
- 7-12 %: the problem line, centred.
- 12-19 %: one product beat (camera module in hand).
- 19-34 %: the demo in one take: prompt pill → tap → result appears.
- 34-53 %: escalation: each new prompt bigger than the last.
- 53-82 %: second feature (stickers, share).
- 82-91 %: playful payoff (cartoon elements on real footage) + benefit line → "with <feature>" line.
- 91-97 %: lockup over the product. 97-100 %: wordmark on black.

**Tech spectacle (2.5 min)**: per claim, abstract CGI metaphor (3-12 s) → the device in a warm room shows it → before/after split. Accelerates into the final chapter. End on a real-looking room, one line, product name, wordmark.

**Feature-run film (DJI, ≈100-150 s) †**
- 0-5 %: product in hands or a postcard aerial; narrator from second 0-1.
- 5-9 %: name card ("INTRODUCING" + name) over a lifestyle shot or the product in a hand, after a 0.4 s drop.
- 9-90 %: 9-18 features at 3-12 s each (2-4 shots + one label); optional one 8-10 s CGI tech move right after the name card.
- 90-95 %: summary montage of the best people shots.
- Last 5-15 %: product spins or lights up on black → name card → logo → end screen, music out.

**Dark reveal / engineering film (60-85 s) †**
- 0-20 % (PS5: 0-33 %): dark tease - grazing-light macros with 2-3 small caps titles, or one long opener move from screen content to the device.
- One hit on the reveal (+29 dB), product in light.
- Middle: 3 claims, each one CGI metaphor or one making-of scene (lab, CNC, chamber) with its title inside the scene.
- Last 15-20 %: a slow 10-15 s build or hero, lockup 5 s, tagline or URL card.

**Manifesto / word montage (45-60 s) †**
- 0-5 %: one establishing shot (empty studio set, a silhouette).
- 5-85 %: 0.3-0.9 s shots of people in motion or at crafts; subtitles or word cards change with every line.
- 85-95 %: a flash run (3-4-frame shots) or a callback to the opening set.
- Last 5-10 %: sign-off words, product glimpse, logo on black.

## Picture
- **Subjects:** CGI exploded views, chips, pixel arrays, vaults (≈45 % of a launch film); lifestyle with the phone in hand (≈40 %); over-the-shoulder UI on the real device (≈15 %). TV films: ≈70 % CGI, ≈30 % "TV in a room" composites. Designer hands that "conjure" CGI layers over drawings.
- **Camera moves:** always moving. Slow push-ins and fly-throughs in CGI; stabilised handheld feel in live action; POV hands for every software feature; lens macros; drone landscapes; pull-back from full-frame screen content to the device in a room.
- **Lens / shot sizes:** phone-in-hand POV (≈55 mm, f/2.8 in the recipe notes), lens macros, wide lifestyle per chapter, drone wides.
- **Grade:** product world = violet/lavender/lilac devices on violet-to-navy gradients; playful sections = off-white #EEE studio with pastel art; AI = blue → teal sparkle and iridescent blue/teal/purple sweeps. Lifestyle = warm, saturated, sunny or string-lit nights. TV films = hyper-saturated CGI (magenta, cyan, rainbow) against warm brown rooms.
- **Texture:** very clean. No grain, no letterbox.
- **Aspect:** 16:9 for films and spots; 9:16 for the teaser.
- **Teaser exception:** pure black-and-white dance on a white floor rectangle, hard top light, crushed blacks, one 0.6 s colour product flash.
- **Product-made proof † (DJI):** 35-65 % of the film is footage shot BY the product (aerials, gimbal shots), labelled "shot on"; golden hour / blue hour postcards; people small in big landscapes; split screens (wide | tele, normal | log) and a 9:16 inset on a blurred copy for vertical modes.
- **Camera-brand cinema † (Sony):** long lenses, night bokeh, golden hour, teal shadows with warm skin (mean luma 0.25-0.45); real UI on a phone held in frame; CGI on graphite or high-key off-white.
- **Dark reveal † (PlayStation, Surface):** near-black first third (mean luma 0.19-0.25), one raking light; then one colour event (spectral rainbow) or a cold steel machine shop.
- **Manifesto † (OnePlus):** athletes in motion, motion blur kept, red accents, crushed blacks.

## Type
- **Typeface:** Samsung Sharp Sans (proprietary, geometric, single-storey "a"). Closest free (by eye): **Outfit SemiBold/Bold** or **Urbanist Bold**.
- **Weight:** Bold 700 for headlines and product names; Medium 500 / Regular 400 for eyebrows, tails and labels.
- **Case:** Title Case for headlines and feature names; sentence case for statements and claims.
- **Tracking:** −0.01 em on headlines.
- **Size:** title system = eyebrow (4-7 % H line, or 3 % cap in the recipe) + headline (cap 7-12 % H) + tail (2.3 % H, right-aligned under the end of the headline). Name card split around the product: line ≈10 % H (cap ≈7 %), or cap ≈12 % H in black on white. Labels cap 2.5-5 % H. End lockup 3.5 / 4.6 / 3.5 % H. Legal micro-text at the bottom of almost every shot.
- **Placement:** headlines inside the 3-D scene (tilted along the product, embossed in a floor, glowing over the lens). Labels in the left third or next to the subject. Lockup as a left- or right-aligned 3-line block beside the product.
- **Colour:** white on dark; black on off-white. One accent word in lilac (#9E8CF0 in the recipe) or a mid blue (≈#3A7BD5) for the eyebrow.
- **Two-step claim:** line 1 = benefit, line 2 = "with <technology>" appears under it 0.6-2.9 s later; both hold 3-7 s.
- **Animation in:** headline `y 40 → 0, opacity 0 → 1, blur 12px → 0`, 0.8 s, `power3.out`; eyebrow 0.2 s later. 3-D: `perspective: 1200px`, `rotateY -25° → -12°` over 4 s, linear, while the shot pushes `scale 1 → 1.06-1.08`. Glow: `text-shadow: 0 0 24-28px rgba(150-160,180-190,255,.55-.6)`.
- **Labels:** fade in/out 0.3-0.5 s, hold 2-4 s.
- **Kinetic:** a line that starts huge and cropped and pulls back to small and centred (`scale 4 → 1`, 1.2 s, `expo.out`); a title that types on left to right (`clip-path` inset, 0.6 s, linear).
- **Prompt pill:** `border-radius: 999px; background: rgba(255,255,255,.18); backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, 45 % W × 9 % H at bottom 10 %; type the prompt at 35 ms per character. Copilot variant †: a large frosted card ≈59 % W × 34 % H, prompt cap ≈3 % H, typed over a real person.
- **Fixed label system † (DJI):** name card = thin tracked eyebrow (cap ≈2 % H, +0.3 em) over the name (cap 5.5-5.8 % H, model word medium, last word light), centred; feature label = bold caps cap 2.5-2.6 % H (+ regular subline 2.4 % H) at x 6.5 % W, y 75-80 % H, hard in/out with the shot; "SHOT ON" watermark top-left. Free stand-ins: **Montserrat** (name), **Open Sans** (labels). Specs on a flying product: callout stacks next to each part, lines at 75 % opacity.
- **Keyword card † (Sony):** one bold keyword or number big (cap 7.5-9.4 % H) + the rest regular and tracked +0.1 em (cap 3.8 % H). Free: **Noto Sans** / **Source Sans 3**. PlayStation: heavy wide caps (**Montserrat ExtraBold**), tease 7 % H, claims 8 % H inside the scene.
- **Decode type † (Surface):** pixel monospace caps (free **Departure Mono** / **VT323**), cap ≈3.8 % H, centred; letters resolve from random glyphs (≈40 ms per swap), a white selection block runs over part of the line, block cursor at the end.
- **Word card † (Copilot):** one prefix in white + a gradient verb, cap ≈13 % H, centred; the verb types on per letter; in the flash run the word changes every 3-4 frames.
- **Small plain type † (OnePlus):** sentence case only, 2.5-4.2 % H, white, centred; subtitles of the narration at 86-93 % H; two-word labels, one per split-screen panel (≈3 % H).

## Transitions
- 29-42 % of all shot changes are visible transitions. This is the opposite of the Apple style.
- **Push into a lens / chip / screen** that becomes the next scene (outgoing `scale 1 → 6`, 0.7 s, `power3.in`, origin on the lens; incoming `scale 1.4 → 1`, 0.7 s, `power3.out`).
- **Light slit / white flash** for an unfold (black → white bar `scaleX 0 → 1` → full white 0.2 s → screen).
- **Screen-to-full-frame:** the photo slides out of the phone and fills the frame.
- **Glow wipe:** an iridescent band sweeps across while the edited clip crossfades under it.
- **Zoom-blur whips** of 0.4 s in friend montages; **page curl**; **phone-edge wipes**; objects flying from one card into the next; **shape matches** (a chocolate bar becomes a folded phone); map-to-drone morph.
- Hard cuts for the rest, mostly on VO sentence ends.
- † Not every film needs visible transitions: Xperia ≈10 %, DJI and OnePlus manifesto almost only hard cuts on the beat. Their specials: a long in-camera push into the sensor or pull-back from screen content to the device (10-16 s, one per film); a gimbal roll as a cut; split-screen panels that change at different times (offset 0.4-1 s); one white light flash before the end hero; type that decodes and scrambles as the transition between claims.

## Sound
- **Music:** a bed that never leaves in launch films; licensed vocal pop in AI spots; music + diegetic sound (sports commentary, a voice command) in TV films; music only in the teaser.
- **VO:** yes in launch films. One calm narrator from ≈2 s to the end card, one sentence per shot. No narrator in AI spots, TV films or teasers.
- **SFX density:** medium-high. Sparkle bursts, whooshes on fly-throughs, tap rings on UI.
- **Mix:** master loud, −10 to −12.6 LUFS integrated. Recipe: VO ≈ −16 LUFS short-term over the bed, then master to ≈ −11 LUFS.
- **Drops and hits:** before each chapter title, duck the bed ≈ −20 dB for 1-1.5 s, then hit +18 to +40 dB on the title cut. On UI taps, stop the music for 0.15-0.2 s and time the edit to the stop. One 0.7-0.85 s silence before the biggest reveal or the second product.
- **End:** lockup over music, then logo on black ≈1 s and silence.
- † **Narrator density:** DJI 100-113 words/min (one sentence per feature, spec said aloud); Sony Xperia ≈85 words/min; Alpha concept sparse and poetic; OnePlus manifesto subtitled. Flagship and reveal films (Mavic 4 Pro, PS5 Pro, Surface) have no voice at all.
- † **Loudness is chosen per film:** VO-led feature runs −17 LUFS with a flat bed (LRA 3 LU); music-only reveals −12.7 to −14 LUFS; OnePlus manifesto −9.2 LUFS (LRA 2.1 LU, a compressed wall); Copilot −21.5 LUFS. Five of the round-3 masters peak above 0 dBTP: keep yours at ≤ −1 dBTP.
- † **Mechanical SFX** (drills, servos, sprays, sand, sparks) carry engineering and material films.

## Do / Don't
**Do**
- Build in chapters with a strict rhythm: hero title (3-5 s) → lifestyle proof → real UI demo.
- Put the headline inside the 3-D scene: lit, tilted, glowing, embossed.
- Split the product name around the product.
- Show every software feature as a real hand on real UI; the result fills the screen right after the tap; each result bigger than the last.
- Make invisible tech visible: CGI metaphor + before/after split + benefit / "with <technology>" super.
- Keep one colour system across all chapters (violet product + blue AI sparkle).
- Drop the sound before each chapter, then hit hard.
- † Prove a spec with the product's own footage and say so on screen ("shot on" watermark).
- † Fix the label position for the whole film (bottom-left or centred) so the viewer learns it once.
- † Give each claim one card: the keyword or number big and bold, the rest small and tracked.
- † For a reveal, earn it: 15-20 s of dark macros or one long build before the one big colour or sound event.
- † In a 0.8 s montage, put the words in subtitles or one-word cards so they stay readable.

**Don't (what makes a copy look cheap)**
- Flat headlines pasted over footage with no depth, glow or perspective.
- Random transitions: use a few types and repeat them as a system (lens push, light slit, whip).
- Fake UI or unreadable UI; the UI must be real and legible.
- Grainy, dim or desaturated lifestyle footage: this style is clean and bright.
- A quiet master (≈ −16 LUFS or lower) for social or web: it will feel weak next to the references.
- A constant wall of sound with no drops: the hits stop working.
- Many fonts. One geometric sans in two or three weights.
- † Spec cards that float away from what they describe; put callouts next to the part.
- † Mixing two type systems (big 3-D headlines AND small plain labels) in one film: pick one.
- † Masters above 0 dBTP (five reference films do it; it clips after platform encoding).

## Recipe
1. **Script:** list 5-7 features. For each, write one VO sentence per shot, a 2-4 word hero title, and one proof scene. Record the VO first.
2. **Footage (licensed stock or own):** phone-in-hand POV at night with warm practicals, sunny day lifestyle, one lifestyle wide per chapter, product renders or a turntable on a violet gradient, abstract CGI loops for tech chapters.
   - Stock search queries: "smartphone in hand night party POV", "friends backyard string lights", "corgi running park", "basketball friends slow motion", "glamping friends night", "drone lake mountains sunrise", "abstract particles loop dark", "chip circuit board fly through CGI", "light speed tunnel loop", "lens macro camera module", "black and white contemporary dance top down" (teaser).
3. **Backgrounds:** product world `linear-gradient(160deg,#6b6290,#1d1b33)` or `radial-gradient(#5b5577,#2a2638)`; playful world `#EEEEEE`.
4. **Hero title component:** eyebrow (Outfit 600, cap 3 % H, opacity .9), headline (Outfit 700, cap 9-11 % H, `letter-spacing:-0.01em`, `text-shadow:0 0 28px rgba(150,180,255,.55)`), tail (Outfit 500, 2.3 % H, right-aligned). Wrap in `perspective:1200px`; GSAP `from {y:40, opacity:0, filter:"blur(12px)"}` 0.8 s `power3.out`, then `rotateY -18° → -10°, scale 1 → 1.06` over 4 s, `ease:"none"`.
5. **Chapter rhythm:** 1 hero title shot (3-5 s) + 5-10 proof shots at 2-2.5 s; ASL ≈2.0-2.8 s overall; montages at ≈1.6 s.
6. **Feature label:** Outfit 500, cap 3-3.5 % H, `left:8%; top:45%`, fade 0.4 s, hold 2.5 s.
7. **Spec card:** number cap 4 % H weight 700 above a 2 % H regular line; fade 0.5 s, hold 2.5-3 s.

## Variants
**1. Unpacked chapter film** (s26-ultra-intro-film, zfold8-ultra-intro-film). As above. The two-product version changes palette at the turn (dark violet/graphite with warm practicals → off-white pastel), joined by a shape-match gag and a 0.85 s silence. Engineering is shown with hands + drawings + CGI layers; spec numbers as bold cards.

**2. AI demo spot** (galaxy-ai-photo-assist-ad). One problem → one prompt → one tap → escalating results → a playful payoff with cartoon stickers on real footage. ASL ≈1.5-1.8 s with one 4-5 s demo take in the first third. Licensed vocal pop, no narrator, −10 LUFS, tiny music stops on taps.

**3. Tech spectacle** (neo-qled-2026-feature-film). Each abstract claim becomes a big CGI metaphor (colour balls = colour, aurora = contrast, light-speed tunnel = refresh rate), then the screen in a warm room, then a split before/after. Two-line benefit / technology super. Long continuous CGI moves (ASL 3.3 s, 44 % of shots over 3 s); cuts not tied to the beat. One 0.7 s silence before the biggest moment, +48 dB after it. Stock CGI loops (particles, tunnels) work for 3-6 s chapter openers.

**4. Monochrome teaser** (zfold8-ready-to-unfold-vertical). The only restrained variant: 9:16, black-and-white dance, `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.35) brightness(.9)`, three shots of exactly 2.13 s (metronome), then 1.0 s, then 0.4-0.6 s with defocused flare inserts; one 0.6 s colour product shot ≈3 s before the end; question line 4.3 % H + a smaller teaser line 2.8 % H on black 1.8 s; logo 0.9 s. Master −10 LUFS with a hit on frame 1.
