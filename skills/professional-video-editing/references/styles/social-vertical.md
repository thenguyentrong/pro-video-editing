## Signature (one paragraph)
The first frame is already the joke, the stake or the extreme thing; there is no logo intro. The video looks native to the platform: phone footage, the app's own text tool, the product's own UI as captions, a creator or employee as the voice. One idea per video, few words on screen (often 1-15 in total), text placed where the platform UI does not cover it. Short pieces end without an end card so they loop; series end on a cliffhanger. The brand shows up as a colour, a mascot or a shape (Target red and bullseye rings, Duolingo green and word tiles, Ryanair's plane with a face, Scrub Daddy's smiley sponge, Spotify green, Canva purple pills), not as a long logo moment.

## Structure
**Hook timing.** Frame 1 (0.0 s) shows the joke, the stake or the extreme: the mascot in an absurd situation, the customer's complaint, an F1 car in a metro, a person upside down. The format is clear in under 1 s. A face plus the hero product within 2 s (food). A typed human stake is complete by about 2.3 s.
Measured hooks, round 3†:
- Caption on at 0.0 s with the object already absurd (one-shot meme, mascot vlog with a clock caption).
- Someone else's complaint for 4-5 s, untouched (stitch), the reply after the cut.
- Product racks into focus by 0.4 s with a word caption already on (product launch); a 0.3 s whip-in + a heckle line (drop).
- A person mid-sentence on the most quotable line, title sticker in by 0.1 s (podcast clip); a double-meaning line with no text at all (comedy ad).
- The format named in frame 1: "BTS" title over a split of process + result (0-2.6 s); three finished results in a split (0-2.5 s).
- Flash-forward: the end reaction first with the result pixelated + a question caption (0-1.8 s), then the timeline.
- Participation: a question subtitle at 0.0 s, two choice cards at ≈6 s (pick one); or a self-aware meme pill about the video itself (0-5 s).
- Real product UI at 0.0 s, the claim typed into its own prompt bar by 2-4 s.

Beats by format (share of runtime):
- **Joke (6-8 s):** set-up 0-50 %; punch line at 50-65 % (3.0 s of 6.0, 4.2 s of 7.5, 4.7 s of 6.4); payoff to the end; optional 0.4 s mascot sting; hard end.
- **One-shot meme (5 s)†:** no beats; caption + action from frame 1 to the end; the action continues so the loop hides.
- **6 s cutdown (Target):** 0-25 % pun over the product; 25-52 % product hero + badge; 52-79 % brand lockup; 79-100 % offer + date. The offer is either on screen the whole time (Chipotle) or the last beat (Target).
- **Stunt reveal (Red Bull):** question caption 0-14 %; payoff first at 14-44 %; behind the scenes 44-100 %, cut out mid-action so it loops.
- **Real-time suspense (Red Bull):** danger shown 0-6 %; one long take with ritual captions; payoff at ≈95 %.
- **Challenge / series (Duolingo, Target, Notion, LEGO†):** stake 0-4 %; rules or set-up to ≈20-28 %; game loop with a visible score, timer or verdict; payoff; CTA line and a 2-3 s brand card in the last ≈2-8 % (series only). Notion's game: set-up 0-28 %, loop 28-88 %, best result last. LEGO prank: flash-forward 0-5 %, set-up to 20 %, timed build 20-57 %, split reaction 57-66 %, reveal 66-92 %, laughter to the end.
- **Craft rounds (LEGO)†:** title 0-7 %; 3 rounds of process (≈25 % each) + split-screen reveal with label and emoji (≈7 % each); ends on the last result.
- **Comedy ad (Liquid Death)†:** product or line 0-10 %; 5-7 set-ups that repeat the contradiction to ≈75-85 %; the biggest gag in the longest take; end card the last 13-16 % (4-7.5 s).
- **Podcast clip (Spotify)†:** quotable line + sticker 0-8 %; 1 wide + 1 reaction cutaway (0.75 s) inside the first 15 %; one long answer; green card the last 2.5 % or no card.
- **Feature call-and-response (Spotify)†:** real UI + typed claim 0-16 %; 4 × (request card + answer UI) 16-85 %; callback to the first shot 85-95 %; logo 1.6 s.
- **Creator explainer (Canva)†:** question 0-10 %; 3 steps or 3 examples (UI zoom, split screen) to ≈85-90 %; CTA to try it or to the description; logo 0-4 s.
- **Ending:** see the table below. Codes, prices and legal go in the post caption (legal lines that must be on screen: cap ≈1.2 % H at 85-90 % H in the last 2-3 s).

## Picture
- Phone footage, no grade, real light (hard sun on food, fluorescent metro, overcast airport). Polish is optional; tone is not.
- Contrast is in the camera: glossy stock for the expectation, shaky phone for the reality (Ryanair). Or the reverse: full TV-commercial production for a deadpan parody (Liquid Death)†.
- Brand as colour and shape in every frame: wardrobe and props in the brand colour (Target red), the mascot or the product as the hero, logos only where they exist in the world (dive board, livery, crash pad).
- One device per video: a growing paragraph, a material montage with shape rhymes, a fake CCTV sketch, a 1.4 s repetition grid, a game with a score, a stacked split screen of process and result†, a selfie inside a phone frame over swapping backgrounds†, a mascot lit by purple/blue LEDs†.
- Premium product montage (Nike): near-monochrome (grey clay, white, black), 3-4 false-colour accent shots, extreme macros, slow push-ins inside shots, grayscale ≈85 %.

## Type
- **Platform-native (memes, Ryanair, Chipotle, Scrub Daddy†):** TikTok Classic look. Free: TikTok Sans 500, white, 2 px black stroke (`paint-order: stroke fill`), lowercase or ALL CAPS, 56-60 px, cap 3.0-3.3 % H, top third. Hard switch, no animation. One-shot meme block: 40 px, cap ≈1.6 % H, 4 lines, at 66-80 % H†.
- **Word captions for a voice:** CapCut look. Free: Montserrat 800, 100 px, uppercase, white, `text-shadow: 0 4px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.6)`, 1-2 words per card, cap ≈5.3 % H at ≈78 % H, switched with `tl.set` on each spoken word.
- **Comedy ad phrase captions (Liquid Death)†:** heavy italic caps, cream `#F3E3B5` with a 3 px black stroke, 2-4 words, cap ≈1.6 % H at ≈67 % H; free: Archivo Black with `skewX(-10deg)`. End card: Archivo Black caps, 2-3 lines, cap 1.9-3.0 % H, upper third.
- **Podcast clip system (Spotify)†:** title = black Inter 800 on white strip boxes (one per line), 2 lines at 53-58 % H, stretches in (`scaleX 0.2 → 1`, 0.25 s), out at ≈4.5 s; show tag white on black below; subtitles Figtree 600 white on `rgba(0,0,0,.45)` rounded boxes at 68-74 % H.
- **Product UI as caption (Duolingo):** word-bank tiles, Nunito 600 84 px, `#3C3C3C` on white, 2 px `#E5E5E5` border with a 5 px bottom edge, radius 24 px; pop per sung word `back.out(3)`, 0.08 s.
- **Feature quote cards (Spotify)†:** wide heavy rounded sans (free: Unbounded 700), 74 px, sentence case, 4-6 lines on a flat brand colour, giant quote marks; the answer is the real UI with a neon outline in the same colour.
- **Craft labels (LEGO)†:** Montserrat Light Italic caps, cap ≈3 % H, on the split seam, + 1-3 emoji below; counter "ELEMENT 1/3" with a bold number.
- **Game graphics (LEGO)†:** name tag = 2 black boxes, white caps cap ≈2.2 % H, top centre; countdown = black rounded box, white digits cap ≈3 % H, yellow at 00, top right.
- **Brand pills (Canva)†:** white Figtree 500 on `#7D2AE8` rounded boxes, cap ≈1.6 % H, 3-7 words; CTA = white text on a `#8B3DFF` highlight, top band.
- **Sport (Red Bull):** Poppins italic 700, 40-42 px, white, lowercase, `text-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.55)`, no box, cap ≈1.7 % H, placed in empty sky or on the subject's body; hard on/off.
- **B2B sketch (Notion):** Inter 500 46 px, white, thin shadow, one centred line at ≈53 % H, sentence case.
- **Creator subtitles:** Inter 500 40 px in a `rgba(42,32,51,.85)` rounded pill, max 2 lines, max 50 % W (DuoTV); or white 40 px with a 1.5 px dark stroke and speaker colours (Target, second speaker `#FF6FA0`).

- **Brand promo type (Target):** Helvetica-Neue-like bold lowercase (free: Inter 700 or Arimo Bold), tight tracking (-0.03 em); pun at 150/165/330 px; product in front of the last word.
- **Editorial type story (Nike):** condensed serif body, cap 2.7 % H, line height 4.3 % H, word by word with the voice (≈2.8 words/s); payoff in condensed extra-bold caps ≈6 % H (free: Anton), one word every 0.17-0.33 s.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts on the lyric line, the spoken word or the bar. Cuts on audio onsets: 100 % (Ryanair), 67 % (Chipotle cutdown), 70 % (Nike sketch), 69-83 % (Canva steps, Spotify podcast sentences)†; picture-driven in the Nike montage (13 %) and the LEGO craft film (5 %)†; Duolingo cuts on lyric line starts, not drum hits.
- Allowed devices, once each: in-camera whip tilt; one-frame directional-blur whip; digital punch-in to ≈2.5x in 0.12 s with meme arrows; freeze + black-and-white verdict of ≈0.5 s with a red X or green check; brand iris wipe (white dot to disc, rings `scale 0 → 6` in 0.5 s); coin-flip card (`rotationY 0 → 90 → 0`, 0.12 s each half); shape-rhyme matches in a product montage.
- Round 3 devices†: stacked split screen cut in for a reveal or a reaction; liquid/blob morph from card to UI (0.3-0.5 s); background swaps behind a fixed phone frame (one per spoken list word); in-shot push-in to macro and back instead of cuts; a letterboxed 16:9 meme insert (map + orange circle + arrow).
- No dissolves. The loop is the last transition: make the last frame lead into the first.

## Sound
- Memes: one song does everything, no voice-over, mastered loud (-7 to -14 LUFS).
- Joke timing: 0.15 s of full silence before the punch word, then the hit; a music hit exactly on the text switch; 0.85 s silence before a comeback line†.
- Creator and employee clips: original dialogue on top, light bed; mixes are quieter (-11.7 to -25.6 LUFS). Podcast clips: dialogue only, no music, -19 to -20 LUFS, natural pauses up to 1.3 s kept before the key line†.
- Comedy ads: dialogue-led, music as a bed and on the end card, -15 to -18 LUFS; cuts follow the lines, not the beat†.
- Sport: location sound first (sea, crowd, engine, breathing, chalk); music low or absent; the real impact (splash) is the loudest sound.
- 6 s cutdowns: music only, never a voice-over. Decide sound-on (≈-6.5 LUFS) or built for mute (≈-24 LUFS, everything readable without sound).
- Default master for new work: -14 LUFS, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP (see `sound-and-mix.md`); the loud meme masters (and LEGO at -9.7 LUFS†) are a choice, not a rule.

## Do / Don't
- Do put the joke, stake or extreme in frame 1; no logo intro.
- Do use one idea and one device per video.
- Do write captions in the platform's or the product's own visual language.
- Do keep important text between 14 % and 65 % of the height and away from the right edge.
- Do show the result before the explanation; keep impressive moments in one take.
- Do give the product a face and a routine if it has a character (mascot vlog, first-person meme)†.
- Do show process and result in one frame (split screen) when the craft is the story†.
- Do add a visible progress device (1/3 counter, countdown box) to anything over 30 s†.
- Do hide the reveal in a flash-forward hook (pixelate the result) instead of spoiling it†.
- Do let a podcast or comedy take run 16-34 s once the hook has landed; one early reaction cutaway is enough†.
- Don't use brand fonts for platform-style captions, or default captions for a premium product film.
- Don't put a CTA, end card or promo code in a meme; codes and dates go in the post text.
- Don't caption every word unless people talk; most memes carry under 15 words in total.
- Don't crop a 16:9 master blindly: re-frame each shot for 9:16.
- Don't copy brand subtitle placement at 80-85 % H (Canva) or a top caption at 10 % H (Scrub Daddy): the platform UI covers them†.
- Don't tighten the pauses in an emotional answer; they are the moment†.

## Recipe
Build at 1080x1920, 25 or 30 fps, in Hyperframes. Real phone footage first; stock only for contrast or B-roll.
1. **Pick the format and the device** (meme, cutdown, stunt, game, sketch, comedy ad, podcast clip, craft rounds, creator explainer). Write the one-line point and the punch line, and its time (50-65 % for a joke).
2. **Shoot vertical yourself where possible.** The stock gate rejects vertical clips; for stock, take 4K landscape and crop a 1215x2160 slice per shot, re-framed on the subject. Queries: `airplane landing handheld fence`, `business class cabin luxury` (the expectation look), `hands holding phone close up`, `sauce pour macro slow motion`, `office worker desk candid`, `city street people walking handheld`, `skateboard trick slow motion`, `rock climbing bouldering`, `ocean waves cliff aerial`, `glass blowing furnace close up`, `podcast studio microphone interview`, `top down picnic blanket flat lay`.
3. **Frame 1:** start the clip at its most unusual moment (mid-move or mid-sentence). Text, if any, is on screen at 0.0 s.
4. **Meme (5-8 s):** 4 clips of ≈2 s or one locked shot; label `500 56px "TikTok Sans"; color:#fff; -webkit-text-stroke:2px #000; paint-order:stroke fill`, top at y 270-340 px; swap labels with `tl.set` at the punch time; music hit on the swap; 0.15 s silence before it; no end card. One-shot version: 4-line block at y 1270-1540 px, on for the whole clip, last frame ≈ first frame.
5. **6 s cutdown:** 0-1.5 s pun over product (beat pulse `scale 1.3`, 0.12 s `power2.out`, back in 0.25 s), 1.5-3.1 s product + badge, 3.1-4.75 s brand lockup (disc zooms 50 → 100 % in 0.12 s), 4.75-6.0 s offer + date (coin flip). Everything readable on mute.
6. **Stunt / sport:** one locked reveal of 4.5-5 s (subject enters from depth and passes the lens), then 3-4 BTS shots of 1.0-1.7 s; caption `italic 700 40px Poppins`, lowercase, centre at y ≈920-1435 px, only during the reveal; natural sound ≈-20 LUFS.
7. **Creator / employee clip (45-80 s):** name and job in the first sentence; hero macro by 2 s; talking head + B-roll; 8 s money shot of the product; caption chunks of 2-5 words synced to the transcript; end on a question to the viewer.
8. **Game (20-70 s):** question + answer key on screen for the set-up; per round 1.2 s wide / 0.9 s close / 0.5 s verdict; timer box with a 10 px `#FFC800` border at y 1250-1400 px if there is a clock (or top right at y ≈230 px, LEGO style); compress dead time with jump cuts.
9. **Safe-zone pass:** overlay a guide (top 14 %, bottom 35 %, 6 % sides, right-edge buttons) and move every text block that touches it.
10. **Choose the ending** from the endings table: loop (meme, vlog, stunt), callback (feature), question (creator), or a short card (comedy ad, podcast). Trim the last frame to lead into frame 1 for loops.
11. **Mix and export:** master -14 LUFS (or louder for a sound-on meme), H.264 1080x1920, poster = frame 0; test on a phone at arm's length, sound off and on.

## Variants
- **Brand meme / persona (Duolingo, Ryanair, Scrub Daddy†).** A mascot that acts like a chronically online creator, or a rude self-aware brand that answers complaints. Stitch the customer's clip untouched for 1.0-5 s, reply in under 10 s, punch line at 50-65 %, 0.4 s mascot sting. Captions in the platform's text tool or the app's own UI. React to news within 24 h. ASL 1.1-2.2 s or one shot; -7 to -19 LUFS. Scrub Daddy adds the product-as-creator formats: a 5 s one-shot meme in first person, a 22 s "morning routine" vlog with a clock caption and 1 s cuts under purple LED light, and drops announced with a two-voice meme argument + a letterboxed map insert.
- **UGC-style (Chipotle employee, Target creators, Red Bull athlete footage).** A real person with a persona carries it; the brand gives the game ("spot the bag") or the product. Interview grammar: wide approach → close answers → product insert (ASL ≈1.5 s), or long handheld takes with stickers and speaker-coloured subtitles (ASL ≈4.3 s). Borrowed TV formats (reality confessional with a fake REC frame) give structure.
- **Challenge / craft / BTS (LEGO)†.** TV-show packaging on phone footage: name tag and countdown in the top band, flash-forward hook with the result pixelated, split-screen reaction at time-up, reveal at 66-92 %. Craft: 3 counted rounds, split-screen reveal next to the real object, light-italic labels + emoji. BTS: stacked split of hands posing (top) and the finished stop-motion (bottom), title on the seam, light-leak loop. ASL 1.0-1.9 s, music wall-to-wall, -9.7 to -13.3 LUFS.
- **Comedy ad / talent collab (Liquid Death)†.** Full commercial production for a deadpan contradiction: sober claims from extreme people, innuendo testimonials, or a famous band that cannot read the brief. One set-up per line (2.5-6.5 s), biggest gag in the longest take, captions only when the picture is busy (cream heavy italic caps at 67 % H), product truth on a 4-7.5 s end card. -15 to -18 LUFS.
- **Podcast clip (Spotify)†.** Start mid-sentence on the best line; title sticker 0.1-4.5 s; one wide and one 0.75 s host reaction early; then hold the close-up for 16-34 s; subtitles 68-74 % H; 1.5 s green card or no card. Dialogue only, -19 to -20 LUFS.
- **Product hero (Nike montage, Target and Chipotle cutdowns, Spotify feature†).** Montage: 30+ shots, ASL ≈0.87 s, shape rhymes (same object in many materials), zero words, hold the clean hero 2.5 s and the logo last. Cutdown: 3-4 beats of 1.5-2 s, one message each, huge type, brand device as transition or background. Feature: real UI first, claim typed in the product, 4 colour-coded request cards each answered by the UI, blob morphs, callback ending, 1.6 s logo.
- **Sport / action (Red Bull).** Most extreme frame first, or show the danger and hold the payoff to ≈95 %. One take = proof. One small italic caption, often a question; ritual captions every 3-8 s in long takes. No VO, no logo, no outro; cut out abruptly so it loops.
- **B2B / SaaS (Notion, Canva†).** Notion never shows the product UI: one absurd office metaphor with one plain line, cut on a 1.4 s grid, ending on a 4.8 s find-it wide with the music dropped; or a street game with the logo as the puzzle (answer key on screen, fails first, best result last, music cut on each verdict). Canva sells through people: a creator with a credential tag, a what-if or pick-one question hook, 3 steps shown as zoomed UI or a split screen with the expert below, purple subtitle pills; or a selfie inside a phone frame over backgrounds that swap per spoken word, opened by a self-aware meme pill. ASL 1.3-7.3 s; move Canva's low subtitles up to 60-70 % H.
