# Broadcast TV
**Use it for:** series main titles, channel idents, channel or platform promos for a series, sports opening titles, season-launch spots, in-match or data graphics packages, and any recurring piece that must work as a "brand frame" around changing content. **Not for:** one-off cinema trailers (use the movie-trailer style), product explainers, or social-first vertical edits that need a hook in the first second.

## Signature
Broadcast work is a system, not a single film. A fixed brand frame (a corner bug, a logo lockup, one brand colour, one sonic asset, a branded end card) wraps content that changes every time. Inside the frame, each format has one strict rule that viewers learn: one material for a whole title sequence, one long locked-off take for an ident, one template repeated for every driver, one shot length for a ritual montage, one bar that never moves during play. Type is either tiny (2–3 % of frame height in titles and data) or huge (12–23 % on promo word cards, up to ≈50 % on sports one-word cards†), never in between. The brand moment is slow and loud; the content around it is fast or calm, but always regular. Sports leagues and channels add one more habit: one treatment over all footage (a gold world, a "memory" vignette, a trailer letterbox) so that archive tape, live feed and new shoots read as one film, and the brand appears inside the world (on shirts, in pyro, in 3D type on the tarmac) before it appears as a graphic.

## Structure
- **Title sequence (60–100 s):** hook 0–8 % (first material macro + two lead names) → build 5–50 % (one panel per shot or one move) → denser middle 34–45 % → turn 55–90 % (the current conflict, longest shots 8–9 s) → title in the last 5–8 s (≈86–100 %), made by the material, then a fade to black.
- **Channel ident (40 s):** logo layer static from ≈1 % → brand device enters at 4 %, faces camera 8–16 %, exits by 22 % → plain tail 22–100 % so continuity can talk over it or cut out.
- **Series promo (85–115 s):** cold open 0–21 % → platform or "returns" card ≈21 % → world montage 26–44 % → 1–2 black holds 44–51 % → acceleration 51–86 % with the tagline split into 2–5 cards at 81–92 % → quiet dialogue or two slower shots 86–97 % → title slam at ≈92–97 % → branded end card 2.5–5 s.
- **Sports opening titles (45–60 s):** hook 0–3 % (brand mark macro, first hit) → roll-call or action 3–87 % → light or shape collapse into the logo 87–89 % → logo hold 11–20 %, last ≈5 % silent.
- **Ritual montage (60 s):** one subject per shot, 2.0 s each, for 95 %; final wide shot 3.5 s; no end card.
- **Package open (80 s)†:** one-material CG hook 0–9 % → archive inside brand frames 9–37 % → 2–3 long CG moves 37–68 % → full-frame archive 68–93 % → wordmark + trophy lockup 93–100 %. Game versions reuse hook and lockup, change only the middle.
- **Tune-in promo (30 s)†:** two identical brand-world shots, one per team, 0–20 % → atmosphere 20–35 % → alternating action 35–86 % → end card 86–100 % (brand → game → teams → time → network), loudest on the card.
- **Season campaign (60 s)†:** slow establishing shot 0–10 % → story scene 10–25 % with a 0.3 s-per-shot logo burst → montage 25–70 % at 0.3–0.6 s → pay-off 70–92 % with the longest shots → 3 one-word cards or the identity card 92–100 %.
- **Long highlight film (180 s)†:** silent aerial 0–2 % → culture prologue 2–13 % → fast body at 0.5–0.7 s with a recurring chapter shot every 20–30 s → quiet valley at 55–63 % → end on one object with the message.

## Picture
- **Titles, subject:** one physical material for the whole piece (fungus, embroidery, paper, rust, ink). No actors, or actors as tiny doll-like figures (5–10 % of frame height) on giant objects.
- **Titles, camera:** macro, very shallow depth of field, one hard light (backlight haze or ≈15° raking light), floating particles (spores, embers) for life. Slow glides, cranes and push-ins; focus pulls give the sense of an edit.
- **Titles, palette:** 3–4 colours in total (for example near-black + ochre + accent red + haze white). 1–37 % of frames are mostly empty and dark.
- **Idents:** one locked-off wide of a real shared space with real people (documentary casting); the brand device is the only effect.
- **Promos:** live action, macro inserts, faces in close-up, drone aerials as chapter markers for each new place. Slow push-ins in the cold open, handheld in action.
- **Promo colour:** one colour world per act (green open, amber middle, steel-blue action), ending in the season colour that the end card repeats.
- **Sports:** faces to camera are the core shot (≈30 of 72 shots in the launch spot); studio portraits on 2–3 rotating sets; cars or the ball as macro or CG inserts; real action short (0.5–1.9 s), brand moments slow (3–12 s).
- **Grade:** deep blacks; one brand colour in light, not as a filter (F1 red #E10600 on #15151E; UCL navy #101030 with royal blue). Real match footage keeps its natural colour.
- **Texture:** grain 0.06–0.08 on titles; crisp and glossy on sports.
- **One treatment over mixed footage†:** gold world with bloom and particles (archive composited on CG frames and screens), or an oval vignette + edge light leaks on every clip, or a teal/warm commercial grade with shallow focus. Archive is never a separate grey section.
- **Brand inside the world†:** type built in 3D into the location (place names on tarmac), broadcast straps on ordinary kids, logos on tattoos and shirts, rings in pyro, a message printed on a garment.
- **Channel promos sell anticipation†:** empty venues, fans, kids and cities; little or no rights footage; one CG element (a swarm) or one VFX transformation carries the idea.

## Type
- **Title credits:** tiny, in negative space, fade only. Condensed grotesque caps → Oswald 600 at 3.3 % H, label Montserrat 500 at ≈1.5 % H, 70 % opacity. Historic → Cinzel 400, tracking 0.25–0.3 em, 2.5–3 % H. Corporate/surreal → Inter 400, sentence case, grey #a9a9a9, 2.5 % H, a 1 px hairline above. Pop → Montserrat 500 caps, 0.35 em, ≈1.8 % H. Fade 0.5 s `power1.out` (or 1.6 s in / 0.7 s out `sine.inOut`).
- **Ident:** logo and channel name (Inter 700) static from the first 0.4 s; no reveal.
- **Promo word cards:** wide heavy caps (Archivo Expanded 800 or Syne ExtraBold), white on black, cap ≈23 % H, hard cut in and out, ≈0.45 s. Show-face cards: Oswald 700 or Anton with a grunge mask and a white-to-grey gradient, cap 12.5 % H, opacity in/out 0.15 s. Shred reveal: Bebas Neue or Anton, 8 slices, 0.4 s `steps(4)`.
- **Factual captions:** Montserrat 400 caps, tracking 0.2 em, cap 2.5 % H, centred at ≈49 % height, fade in 0.4 s, only on calm aerials.
- **Corner bugs:** logo top-left at 3–4 % H with ≈2.5 % margins; show title top-right in Oswald 500, 0.2 em, ≈2 % H.
- **Sports names:** surname Archivo 900 / Archivo Expanded / Unbounded 800 caps, cap 8.5–9.5 % H; script first name (Mrs Saint Delafield or Allison) at ≈10 %, rotated −8°; data in Space Mono 700, tracking 0.4–1 em. Surname in: `x +120→0`, `blur 18→0`, 0.25 s `power4.out`; red colour wipe 0.35 s via `clip-path`; script write-on 0.4 s.
- **Data bar:** Barlow Condensed 700 names and digits, Barlow 500 for secondary lines. Text swaps on the cut with no animation; a new item rebuilds centre-out in 0.8 s.
- **Big word cards†:** one word per card, very condensed heavy italic caps (Bebas Neue, `skewX(-8deg)`), cap ≈50 % H, white on a face, hard on with the cut, 0.85 s each.
- **Season title / wordmark†:** condensed heavy caps (Anton) cap ≈14–16 % H in two lines beside a logo at ≈37 % H, over live footage at the music peak; or a script wordmark built in 3D gold at 20–25 % H; date line Montserrat 800 at ≈3.5 % H.
- **Score bug goal state†:** club-code tab, score + crest disc, gradient wing, clock tab; geometric heavy sans (Outfit 800) for codes and "GOAL"; wing swaps to the club colour for ≈1.7 s.
- **Song credit†:** three lines, Inter 500 at ≈2.5 % H, left 10 %, 73–84 % height, first 3 s, like a music video.

## Transitions
- **Titles:** hard cuts on a fixed length, or invisible camera-move transitions (push through an object, pull out, tilt through a surface; ≈10 in 76 s). One fade to black at the end. No dissolves or wipes.
- **Idents:** none. The device itself is a moving window between two places.
- **Promos:** hard cuts; 1–2 black holds; at most one cross-dissolve (≈1 s out of the first card).
- **Packages and graphics†:** one gradient wipe (≈0.3 s, lime → cyan) for every state change; camera moves through CG geometry; particle bursts reveal the wordmark; a VFX transformation inside the shot instead of a cut.
- **Sports:** hard cuts plus speed transitions only: motion-blur smears 0.15–0.25 s, whip pans 0.12 s per side, giant-glyph wipes 0.2 s, a light-streak morph into the logo, a brand-shape edge wipe 0.9 s, a fade through navy 1.5 s. No dissolves.

## Sound
- **Titles:** one theme, flat (LRA 3–7.5 LU, or very dynamic at 16.6 LU for a quiet piano piece), no SFX, no voice. The title is the loudest moment, then a decay.
- **Idents:** music in at ≈1.2 s, peak while the device faces camera (−8 LUFS momentary), then a steady bed that can be faded anywhere.
- **Promos:** dialogue on top of a steady bed. HBO-style breathes (LRA ≈13 LU, black-hold drop-outs, re-entry hits); BBC-style stays flat with no mid-trailer hits, then a 0.2 s silence and a sting on the end card.
- **Sports:** a compressed wall (LRA 2–3.4 LU), one hit at the start (≈1.5 s), the loudest moment on the logo (−9 LUFS momentary), then 2–3 s decay. Cuts follow the edit grid, not every beat (15–28 % of cuts on onsets, near chance). An anthem can be the sonic logo: a flat plateau around −15 LUFS momentary, no drops.
- **Highlights:** no music, natural stadium sound only.
- **Brand sound:** one sonic asset with years of equity (an anthem, a theme) does more than any new SFX. Use a licensed cue of similar character, never the real one without a licence.
- **Two mixes per spot†:** TV-origin promos measured at broadcast spec (−23 LUFS EU, −24 LUFS US, true peak −10 to −14 dBTP); online music-led versions at −11 to −12.4 LUFS. Event social cuts keep the live sound at ≈−23.
- **Short silences before peaks†:** 0.35–0.85 s drop-outs before a montage, a reveal or a logo; a 0.7 s drop-out before a VFX transformation (+24 dB after).
- **Mix targets:** −14 to −16 LUFS for online sports and loud promos; −18 to −23 LUFS for quiet drama promos and titles; −12 LUFS is ident level on the measured upload; true peak −1 dBTP.

## Do / Don't
**Do**
- Define the brand frame once (bug position, end card, colour, sonic asset) and reuse it exactly.
- Pick one rule per format and keep it for the whole piece (one material, one shot length, one template).
- Place tiny credits in the emptiest part of each shot; move them per shot.
- Make the logo the payoff: longest shot, loudest moment, plain background.
- Change graphics on the cut; keep them still during content.
- Give every chapter of a promo an aerial or a wide as its first shot.
- Keep idents usable: after the brand moment, leave a clean tail that can be cut anywhere.
- Split a tagline into one-word cards inside the fastest part, so the message and the climax land together.
- Put one treatment on all footage (gold world, vignette + light leaks, letterbox + film grade) so archive and new material read as one film.†
- Build a package once (hook, lockup, transition) and change only the middle for each game or week.†
- Give match-ups symmetry: same shot type and the same number of shots per team, alternating.†
- Put the brand inside the world first (shirts, tattoos, in-scene 3D type, pyro), then on the end card.†
- Deliver a broadcast mix (−23 EU / −24 US LUFS) and a separate online mix (−12 to −14).†
- In a long fast highlight film, plan silence: a silent establishing shot, short gaps at section turns, one quiet valley.†

**Don't**
- Don't mix materials or media inside a title sequence.
- Don't put lower thirds or explaining supers on promos; recognition is assumed.
- Don't use a second graphic colour beside the brand colour.
- Don't animate the ident logo; it is part of the composition.
- Don't use dissolves in sports pieces.
- Don't put captions on busy action shots; text sits only on calm, graphic shots.
- Don't slow a sports roll-call down before the logo; the logo is the only long shot.
- Don't show archive tape raw beside HD footage; grade or frame it into the same world.†
- Don't add names, scores or captions to highlight packages of live events; selection and order are the edit.†
- Don't move the fixed parts of a score bug in a goal or state animation; only the wing changes.†
- Don't master an online upload over −1 dBTP (two measured uploads hit +0.1 and +1.3 dBTP).†
