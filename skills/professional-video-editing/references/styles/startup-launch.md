# Startup launch
**Use it for:** software and app launches, new-feature films, AI product demos, "it's out" launch-day videos, founder announcements, product-update recaps, conference/launch-week sizzles, customer story films for a SaaS or developer tool, hackathon and demo-day videos for a software product. **Not for:** hardware with many features and a narrator (see `tech-launch-loud.md`), design-led physical objects (see `design-tech.md`), calm consumer product spots with one claim (see `apple-precision.md`), robots and machines (see `industrial-robotics.md`), long explainers that argue a case (see `editorial-explainer.md`).

## Signature
The software is the hero, and it is treated like a physical product. The interface is either rebuilt as a 3-D object in a dark or flat colour void (tilted 10-35°, shallow focus, a slow virtual camera) or filmed on a real screen inside a real scene. One task plays from the first click to the result; nobody explains features with on-screen bullets. Words are few: a product name, one 2-4 word line, a URL, the logo. The brand stays monochrome (black, white, one accent) and colour comes from the product UI. Feature films have no voice and no shot under 2 s; recap sizzles cut at about 1 s; founder and customer films let real people talk and cut on their sentences. Sound is one music bed, mastered around −14 LUFS, with one lift on the key action and silence under the logo.

## Structure
**Rendered-UI feature film (30-70 s)**
- 0-10 %: hook on the result or the empty input: a macro of the finished design, the sidebar, or a big prompt/question pill.
- 10-15 %: title card (optional; Linear skips it).
- 15-85 %: one task in 5-9 set-ups of 2-10 s: open → ask/type → result builds → refine → approve/ship. The longest shot (8-10 s) is the AI or "magic" moment. Music lifts +6 to +8 dB on the key action at ≈50 %.
- 85-92 %: one 2-4 word line on black or brand colour.
- 92-100 %: logo (shapes morph into it, or a hard cut), 1-2 s silence.

**Keynote / launch-week sizzle (60-110 s)**
- 0-5 %: cold open (city, arrivals, dark stage). 5-6 %: event lockup on a hit.
- 6-20 %: arrivals, expo, community. 20-60 %: stage launches with speaker bites; partner-logo burst (0.17-0.3 s shots) at ≈58-60 %.
- 60-90 %: guests, a star guest or fireside chat at 81-90 %, the big hall wide at ≈90 %.
- 90-100 %: founder close, lockup again.

**Customer documentary (75-240 s)**
- 0-10 %: the customer's world before any brand (factory, desk, café). 10-50 %: interviews + inserts; the product appears as a filmed screen or a 3-4 s render. 30-50 %: one graphic moment (split-screen labels, HUD flow chart). 50-95 %: real use and result. 95-100 %: end card, partner logos or credits.

**Story / comedy ad (60-100 s)**
- 0-20 %: a normal life scene. 20-48 %: the pain as a gag (a prompt that keeps growing, a horror-parody flashback); fastest cutting here. 48-52 %: the turn, a 0.4-0.9 s drop-out. 52-75 %: the product solves it in one calm take (up to 9.4 s), dialogue stops. 75-90 %: 1-2 claim cards and the payoff scene. 90-100 %: logo, URL or store badge.

**Founder / presenter launch (3-15 min)**
- 0-5 %: cold open, founder on a black set, no music, pauses left in. 5-14 %: the problem and the promise. 14-88 %: 3-4 chapters, each opened by a full-screen chapter card; demos at a table or over the shoulder with real reactions; one archive/history interlude. 88-99 %: summary with status lower thirds. Last 1 %: logo + URL. Presenter-to-camera videos may end with no card.

## Picture
- **The UI as an object:** real UI rebuilt in 3-D, tilted 10-35°, sharp band in front and blur behind, vignette into black (#000, mean luma 0.05-0.06), or flat on one solid colour per feature (Figma: lime #d9ff82, violet #4b00c9, orange; Vercel: pure white #fff). Zoom the UI 200-400 % until labels read at 3-6 % H. Never show a full page flat at 100 %.
- **The UI in the world:** filmed on a real laptop or phone, at an angle, shallow focus, inside a lit scene; tint the screen to fit the grade (mint for the rival app, beige for yours).
- **People:** documentary interviews off-axis (never into the lens) in warm practical light against cool daylight; actors in comedy shorts with a warm film grade and grain; a founder on a black void set; event crowds in magenta/violet stage light.
- **Brand colour:** monochrome plus one accent (Linear indigo ≈#5e6ad2 by eye, Stripe violet ≈#796af9, Raycast red #ff6363, Figma orange #f2643a). The rest of the colour comes from the product UI.
- **Cursor:** large, visible, and moving with intent; clicks land on cuts or music hits.

## Transitions
- Hard cuts between camera set-ups (Linear 8 of 8; Stripe Sessions 91 of 92; Vercel Ship all but 2 graphic moments).
- Inside rendered-UI films: virtual camera zoom-in/zoom-out instead of cuts (Vercel v0: zero cuts in 47.5 s), panel swings (`rotateY −25° → 0`), shape morphs into the logo (Figma, all three films).
- Colour-change hard cut = new feature (Figma).
- Branded wipes, used once or twice per film: split-screen wipe, circle iris, wave wipe (Stripe); glitch/scan-line or pixel dissolve (Vercel, Figma Config); a 0.4 s dip to black into the end card (Linear).
- No dissolves as habit, no whip pans, no flash frames (except a deliberate genre parody).

## Sound
- **Feature films:** music only, no VO. Either a hushed ambient bed or an electronic bed that grows to a peak at 50-60 % (+6 to +11 dB); UI clicks and soft whooshes sparingly. Silence 0.9-2.2 s under the logo.
- **Sizzles:** voice spine of stage sound bites (2-6 s each) over a pulsing bed; 0.4-0.7 s music drops before key lines; hit on the lockup and on the hall wide.
- **Customer films:** interview bites carry everything, no narrator; music steady and low (loudness range 2.5-4.5 LU).
- **Story ads:** dialogue until the product arrives, then music only; a 0.4-0.9 s drop-out at the turn, then the biggest hit (+30 dB).
- **Founder films:** voice for 77-100 % of the runtime, pauses left in, a cold open without music.
- **Master:** −14 LUFS for web (measured −13.2 to −17.8 for most films), true peak −1 dBTP. Avoid the −28 LUFS whisper unless the brand already owns it.

## Do / Don't
**Do**
- Show one real task from start to result; let the product do the explaining.
- Treat the UI as a filmed object: tilt, depth of field, zoom to the line that matters.
- Keep a calm pulse in feature films (ASL 3-5.5 s, nothing under 2 s); save ≈1 s cutting for sizzles.
- Use one type system (one sans, one mono) and one accent colour.
- End with 2-4 words, the logo and silence.
- In people films, cut on sentences, film screens for real, and let customers or founders speak.

**Don't (what makes a copy look cheap)**
- Flat full-screen recordings at 100 % with a tiny cursor.
- Feature bullet lists, checkmark icons, "all-in-one" claims, stock "tech" particles, gradients on everything.
- A narrator reading the feature list over a screen recording.
- Animating every UI element in the same bouncy way; text on every shot.
- A loud master and hits on every cut in a feature film.
- Ending with a fade-out on a busy screen instead of a clean card in silence.

## Recipe
1. **Pick the variant** (see Variants) and write the task chain: 4-6 verbs (open → ask → result → refine → ship). One sentence for the point.
2. **Capture the UI** at 2× resolution: real screenshots or live HTML of the real product (never AI-drawn UI). Crop each state to the part that matters.
3. **Build the stage (Hyperframes, 1920×1080):** background `#000` (or one flat colour per feature, or `#fff`); UI card in `perspective:1600px` with `rotateX(25-30deg) rotateZ(-12 to -16deg)`; blurred copy behind + masked sharp band in front; vignette `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 45%, transparent 40%, #000 85%)`.
4. **Shots:** 6-11 set-ups of 2-10 s. Each = one slow camera move (`x/y` 3-8 %, `scale 1 → 1.08`, `sine.inOut`) or a zoom into a detail (`scale 1 → 2.2`, 1.2 s `power3.inOut`). Hard cut between set-ups; the AI/"magic" shot is the longest (8-10 s).
5. **Animate the task inside shots:** caret typing with `steps()` at ≈10 chars/s; result rows `opacity 0 → 1, y 6px → 0` stagger 0.1 s; documents write line by line; diagram lines `strokeDashoffset` 0.6 s; a large cursor with clicks on beats.
6. **Type:** tagline `500 7vh Inter` centred, fade 0.5-2 s or word build 0.3 s apart; logo hard cut, 2-5 s; URL 3.5-4 % H.
7. **Sound:** one licensed electronic or ambient bed; lift +6 dB on the key action at ≈50 %; 1-2 s silence under the logo; master −14 LUFS, −1 dBTP.
8. **People variants:** real footage only (own shoot or licensed stock), off-axis interviews, inserts, screens filmed in camera. Stock queries: "developer typing laptop night office", "team meeting whiteboard startup loft", "hands typing keyboard macro shallow focus", "conference keynote stage crowd wide", "tech meetup audience applause", "coffee shop owner card payment terminal", "founder interview black background", "city street morning commute handheld".
9. **QC:** no shot of a full flat screen; every UI label readable at phone size; ≤ 4 words per card; loudness checked.

## Variants
### Polished rendered-UI product film (Linear, Figma, Raycast teaser)
- Dark void (Linear: #000, tilted UI, shallow focus, ASL 4.7-5.5 s, zero words until a 3-4 word tagline, no VO) or flat colour worlds (Figma: one colour per feature, UI zoomed 200-400 %, title cap ≈14.6 % H in orange on black, ASL ≈3.1 s, end shapes morph into the logo). Raycast teaser: huge tilted panels over dark chrome folds, then a mono "NEW ___" word list swapping every 0.25-0.5 s as the only fast part, "coming" date card, logo 1.9 s.
- Single-property social reel (Figma glass): 30 s, no text, a strict shot grid that speeds up (3.8 s ×4 → 1.7 s) into a 5.8 s logo, cut on the bar, mastered loud (−9 LUFS).

### One-take screen-recording demo (Vercel v0, Develop. Preview. Ship.)
- No cuts at all: a virtual camera zooms and pans over real UI; ≈10-12 beats of ≈4 s. Hook = a big question in a prompt pill that becomes the input. Or a chapter explainer on black: the product's verbs as 4 numbered chapters (5-8 s), one gradient colour each, a 1 px line drawing down to link them, narrator ≈2.9 words/s.

### Founder-led launch (Arc Act II)
- CEO to camera on a black set, cold open with pauses and no music; full-screen chapter cards (wide heavy caps on royal blue + a serif subline) only at act breaks; demos at a diner table with real reactions; archive web pages as history; status lower thirds in the summary; ASL ≈3.6 s with 0.3-1.5 s montage bursts; voice ≈77 % of the runtime.

### Presenter "it's out" video (Raycast It's out)
- A team member talks to camera in a warm home studio, two cameras A/B, cut every 3-15 s on sentence ends (ASL ≈5.2 s, slowing down over time); ≈27 % screen inserts (website, download, the app in use); only 2 designed graphics; no end card.

### Keynote / launch-week sizzle (Stripe Sessions, Vercel Ship, Figma Config recap)
- Event footage at ASL ≈1.15-1.2 s, median ≈0.9-1 s, 50-59 % under 1 s; sound-bite voice spine; event lockup at start and end (−14 LUFS). Vercel adds typed mono words (line ≈17 % H) with a block cursor and a corner bug. Product recap version (Figma Config): no people, 4 black chapter cards (cap ≈11 % H) each with one effect that hints at the product, ASL ≈1.8 s.

### Customer documentary (Stripe S700, Stripe Radar, Linear INSPECT)
- No narrator; customers and staff speak (≈2.7-3.5 words/s); the product as filmed screens or 3-4 s of renders; one small brand graphic system (violet split-screen labels, angled-ribbon lower thirds, or a white mono HUD); calm and flat ASL 1.6-2.5 s, or 5.6 s for a cinematic short with a 37 s HUD hold; quiet mix −14 to −20 LUFS; crew credits or partner logos at the end.

### Comedy / story ad (Dia, Arc Search, Raycast iOS)
- A short film with a cast: the pain as a gag in the first half (fastest cutting, ASL ≈1 s), a 0.4-0.9 s drop-out at the turn, the product in one calm take, dialogue stops when the product appears, 1-2 plain claim cards, logo + URL or app badge. Deadpan version (Raycast iOS): two locked-off positions, ASL ≈4.8 s, no words until one wide-caps end line, product only at ≈89 %.
